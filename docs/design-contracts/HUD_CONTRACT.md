# HUD and fighter identification — F24, Option A

Decision: 2026-09-12. This is the shared design contract for HUD summaries, dimensions, hierarchy and ownership/effect rendering. Main-document and HTML summaries must be derived from these rules; validate mirrors against them when changing the contract. No automatic documentation generator is claimed. Implementation, artwork and visual/runtime checks remain pending.

## Status slots

| Mode | Geometry | Display |
|---|---|---|
| Story | Two 44 × 44 px glass badge chips (a glass disc with a 1.5 px edge ring and the status glyph at 62 %, a 3 px duration ring whose outer edge sits 5 px outside the badge, and a name and time column 12 px beside it) below the block charges; each slot keeps a fixed position as wide as its widest status name in the current language, and the control slot starts 30 px after it. Was 28 × 28 before the pulp redesign (2026-10-26, user ruling "bigger"), and a pulp badge with a 5 px ring until the Temporal Glass rebuild (2026-10-27) | Damage slot left; control slot right |
| Fighter | Two 40 × 40 px glass badge chips (the Story chip's parts at 40 px, 18 px apart; the text column drops below a 1700 px canvas) in the row under each player's cooldown lenses; was 24 × 24 beside the HP bar before the pulp redesign | Damage slot left; control slot right, including P2 rather than reversing their semantic order |

Hide an empty slot's icon/radial while retaining its reserved position (since 2026-10-26 an empty chip keeps its size and fades its text to 0.35; since 2026-10-27 it is a quiet ring, a 1.5 px dim gold ring with no fill and no glyph, where the pulp chip drew a dashed ghost ring). Use a fixed-position StatusEffects_Panel so hiding one TextureRect does not shift the other. Show both occupied slots simultaneously; never choose a single winning HUD icon based on outline priority.

Bind each slot's icon and radial duration to the authoritative status type, duration and intensity in that category. Same-slot replacement/refresh follows the existing status system; rendering does not stack, extend or recreate gameplay effects. The damage slot covers Venom and Radiant Burn; the control slot covers Time Dilation, Static Charge, Root and Story-only Suppression. Radiant Burn remains vulnerability, not periodic damage. Suppression also retains the ability-lock overlays. Tesla's Conductive mark stays outside both slots under F07.

Use distinct status glyphs plus localized names in the existing reference UI; color alone must not identify an effect. Update from actual state on apply, expiry, overwrite, recovery, loading and rollback. Radials follow authoritative simulation clocks, including pauses and Time Freeze; the HUD must not independently count down a frozen status.

## Fighter ability cooldowns — M25 (2026-09-26)

Each Fighter player shows three `20 × 20 px` cooldown icons (Special 1, Special 2, Movement Ability) in a row beside their block charges, P2 mirrored. Radial timers and a brief ready flash come from authoritative cooldown state, including Resonance Momentum refunds and rollback corrections. Both players' icons are always visible. Icons use distinct glyphs, so readiness never depends on color alone.

**Amended 2026-10-26 (pulp UI redesign, user rulings "bigger sizes" and HUD-F2).** The three `20 × 20` radials are replaced by `54 × 54 px` square slots in a row under each player's cluster (S1 on the outer edge, P2 mirrored), each with a small S1 / S2 / MOV key tag, the ability's icon, a clockwise pie sweep over the elapsed share and the remaining seconds as the centre number; the sweep is the readiness radial for this contract's purposes. The brief ready flash and the D10 "not yet" flash are kept. Readiness still never depends on colour alone (the sweep, the seconds and the READY frame carry it).

**Amended 2026-10-27 (Temporal Glass rebuild, the user's round 5 choice of 2026-10-26; it supersedes the slots' look above).** The square slots with a pie sweep become round glass lenses with readiness rings, as this contract first asked, plus the sweep: a `54 px` lens per slot (S1 on the outer edge, P2 mirrored, a 74 px pitch) shows the ability's icon clipped to the lens, a clockwise sweep over the elapsed share, a readiness ring outside the lens (3 px wide, its outer edge 6 px out, so 66 px across) and the remaining seconds as the centre number, with its S1 / S2 / MOV key label under it. The ready flash is one 600 ms bloom of the lens as it becomes ready; the D10 "not yet" flash is a 2 px red circle contracting round the lens's ring over 0.25 s. Readiness still never depends on colour alone (the sweep, the ring, the seconds and the bloom carry it).

## Beacon anchors — M15 (2026-09-26)

Beacon Anchors (M15, 2026-09-26; Act III only, hidden in Levels 0–12) — a `46 × 46 px` Beacon badge (was `24 × 24` before the 2026-10-26 pulp redesign; a glass badge since 2026-10-27) 24 px right of the rewind counter, followed by up to three `18 × 18 px` gold diamond pips (was `8 × 8`), 8 px apart, one per remaining anchor charge (Easy 3 / Normal 2 / Hard 1); a spent charge stays as a thin outline. An Anchor Snap splits one pip, its halves parting and fading over 0.6 s (0.15 s under Reduced Temporal Effects; the pulp pip cracked). At zero charges the badge shows a hollow Beacon outline with a steady "last stand" tint (`Danger`), because the next collapse is Smothered. Pips use a diamond shape distinct from the round rewind glyph, so the count reads without color; the localized accessible label reads "Anchor charges: N". Bind to the authoritative Beacon charge count, updating on fresh entry, full Restart Level, Snap, load and completion. Validate on small displays alongside both status slots, cooldowns, Integrity and the Time Freeze icon (C01a).

## Chronal Dust

**Amended 2026-10-26:** the counter is a plate at the top right (a glass plate since 2026-10-27) with a 38 px dust glyph and the bare number (the long form is its tooltip and accessible name); the `+N` float is unchanged.

During active Story-level HUD display, the top-right Chronal Dust counter remains visible, including at zero and during the boss fight after the Integrity clock hides. Bind it to the current level's undeposited levelChronalDust balance. Previously deposited spendable dust remains separately identified in the Repository/Beacon; never display a combined balance that suggests current earnings can be spent there.

Only the pooled +N pickup notification rises and fades after one second. The numeric balance does not fade after inactivity. Update it from committed wallet changes on pickup, fee, restart, recovery, load and completion; a mirrored event or rollback must not add dust again. Pickup notifications are cosmetic and cannot drive the balance. Full-screen menus/cinematics may use their existing HUD visibility rules; persistent means no pickup-triggered or inactivity fade while that HUD is displayed.

## Ownership and effects

**G12 (2026-09-26):** ownership also carries a slot shape (P1 ▲ / P2 ●) on the name tag and outline badge. The Settings player-slot palette (Default cyan/red, Blue/Orange, High-Contrast) recolors only the local presentation of the outline and HUD slot colors.

For visible Fighter combatants, the thin ownership outline is a separate persistent layer: P1 cyan #00f0ff, P2 red #ff3366 at the existing one-pixel reference thickness and static intensity. Future P3/P4 colors remain deferred. Match slot identity drives this layer; damage, statuses, armor, invulnerability, ability decoys and an effect expiring cannot recolor, pulse, disable or replace it. Keep the existing P1/P2 HUD labels.

Status and armor feedback uses a separate interior glow/flash and existing sparks, particles and HUD glyphs. Compose the ownership edge after these effects and keep bloom/flash intensity low enough that the edge remains distinguishable. Do not paint a second opaque effect-colored outline over it. This does not require the alternative permanent overhead P1/P2 badge.

Within the secondary effect layer only, retain the existing armor/spawn-protection precedence over status glow. Resolve simultaneous armor and spawn protection in a stable order (spawn protection, then armor); with both status categories occupied, use control glow before damage glow, while both HUD icons remain visible. On expiry, recompute from current state rather than restoring a stale cached color. PointLight2D uses the secondary effect color/intensity and never changes the ownership edge.

**Combat and movement update (2026-10-16).** Three effects use this secondary layer and leave the ownership edge alone; the outline shader composites the edge after the sprite's tint and flash. A **perfect block** (decision 3.4) plays a short pale-cyan hit flash on the blocker, with the ordinary block's shake and haptic, in both modes. The **Ultimate activation wind-up** (decision 2.1) tints a live caster a steady Unblockable red (built steady, not flashing; whether that matches the ruling's "red flash" is open for the user), never warmed by the hero aura, with the Unblockable X glyph above it, for exactly the wind-up; a caster knocked out mid-wind-up drops it on the KO tick, and a hit flash plays over it with the red returning after. **Spawn protection** now means the Fighter respawn grant alone (decision 4.2: 120 frames at the first legal drop, shrinking toward 60 the longer the fighter waits, ended by its first attack or Special): its glow and the "Respawning" label read the simulation's record of that grant, so a roll, air dodge, tech, knockdown, throw or Foresight grant on the shared invulnerability counter no longer lights it, apart from two recorded presentation limits: a roll or air dodge started in the grant's last 8 frames or fewer can keep the glow and the label up through its own invulnerability, and a grant shorter than 30 frames that the first ledge grab merged keeps them up until the hang ends. None of the three adds a HUD element, and the telegraph has no off-screen indicator. Nobody has looked at any of them.

The existing _OutlineColor, _OutlineThickness, _GlowIntensity and _PulseSpeed controls describe the secondary effect treatment; for Fighters, its mask cannot cover the ownership edge. Add independent _OwnerOutlineColor, _OwnerOutlineThickness and _OwnerOutlineEnabled bindings for that edge. These are design bindings requiring shader/resource implementation. Story entities can retain their existing effect outlines; Story Suppression still smothers the gold resonance aura. No Fighter Suppression source is added.

Keep each actor's visual state isolated. Initialize ownership on spawn/slot assignment and restore it from match identity on rollback; do not mutate a shared material resource so that changing one actor recolors another. Choose a supported per-instance or preallocated isolated-material implementation and verify batching/memory cost; parameter changes alone do not establish a no-allocation or batching guarantee. Sample glow lifetime from the real protection/status state, not a duplicate hard-coded timer. F22's Sudden Death starts without spawn protection. D04's brief post-Defy hit protection uses the same secondary invulnerability-glow layer and its actual remaining phase/timer. Keep the F13 Defy seal broken during and after the window; glow expiry does not restore availability. Do not add a damage/control-slot icon, new persistent counter or ownership-edge recoloring. A load cannot replay the proc or restart its glow.

## Reduced Temporal Effects — C01a

The [comfort preset](COMFORT_SETTINGS.md) replaces decorative distortion, repeated after-images, full-screen flashes and heavy desaturation/pulsing with stable cues. Preserve the P1/P2 ownership edge, both status glyphs/radials, attack-class signatures, hazard footprints, clocks. Armor/spawn/Defy feedback becomes steady secondary glow with a smooth expiry fade; the Defy seal stays broken. No preset toggle changes authoritative timers or replays an effect. Screen Shake, HUD opacity and UI scale remain independent; validate small displays and long localized labels under both presentations.

## Acceptance checks

Check Story with zero/one/two statuses, either slot expiring or being overwritten, Suppression, Time Freeze, boss entry and zero dust. Verify dust remains visible while only +N fades, and check fees/load/restart against the economy ledger.

Check both Fighter slots and mirrored characters with both statuses, armor, spawn protection, flashes and persistent objects active. P1/P2 ownership must remain distinguishable when effects start, overlap and expire, including rollback and Sudden Death. Test both damage/control icons simultaneously and their fixed positions.

Review bright/dark backgrounds, supported color/contrast options, reduced-effects settings where available, HUD opacity, 90%–140% UI scale, long localized labels and small displays. Validate readability, per-actor material isolation and rendering budgets in the actual game. These checks remain pending; documentation consistency is not visual or runtime sign-off.

**Feel and UI update (2026-10-25).** The M25 cooldown row is three radials (Special 1, Special 2, Movement Ability) per player, P2 mirrored: Echo Step was removed from both modes, with its fourth radial and its under-30-meter dim. The Story HUD's authored encounter-lock panel (`SafeArea/EncounterLockPanel`: the "Enemies left" count and the 3 s GO prompt), which the 2026-10-12 gameplay update added and this contract never named, was removed on the user's request (ruling R4); no amendment for it is owed. A lock's objective line is unchanged. A successful block now also plays a code-drawn effect at the blocker's front (presentation only, outside the HUD layers and outside the ownership edge).

## UI redesign — layout, sizes and gauges (2026-10-26; the Temporal Glass look since 2026-10-27)

The user's selection and rulings are recorded in the ledger. On 2026-10-26 the user chose **HUD-S1** (Story corner cluster), **HUD-F2** (Fighter top versus bars) and "bigger" sizes (about 1.5–2× the earlier contract), first in the Retro Pulp component family with the **BOSS-B** pulp nameplate boss bar (`USER-UI-PULP-REDESIGN`). Having seen that build in the game, the user rejected it the same day and chose Direction A, **Temporal Glass** (`PNL-A` panels, `BTN-A` buttons, `GAUGE-A` gauges, `ICN-A` icons) with the **BOSS-A** glass boss bar, keeping HUD-S1, HUD-F2, the Resonance Gold palette, Cinzel + Outfit, the sizes and the Fighter Ultimate bar (round 5, `USER-UI-GLASS-REBUILD`). The Temporal Glass rebuild (2026-10-27) amended the look words of this section in place; its sizes and placement stand, except that the Story Ultimate ring is 110 px across (pulp 116) and the Fighter timer plate is `234 × 111.6` (pulp `240 × 118`). Every size below is a 1920 × 1080 reference value at UI scale 1.0 and is provisional (`PROVISIONAL-UI-GLASS-HUDS`, `PROVISIONAL-UI-GLASS-HUDF`). The interface wears the Wardens' gold: cold cyan survives only as player 1's ownership colour and the Severed's cold light, so no HUD chrome is cyan (`UiChromeCyanSourceScanTests`).

**Story HUD (HUD-S1).** The top-left holds three glass panels under the level title and objective, 620 px wide and 26 px apart, with light tags on *Vitals* and *Abilities* and none on the time tools (the pulp panels were tagged ink plates): *Vitals* (a 112 px chamfered glass portrait frame, the hero name, an HP tube `380 × 30` with its value, the numeral and a dimmed "/max", 14 px inside its right end, block pips at 30 px, a spent pip staying at 0.28, and the two status chips), *Abilities* (the Ultimate as a 92 px lens inside a 110 px ring, where the pulp build drew a 92 px frame inside a 116 px ring, the Defy seal at 34 px with its label, and three 74 px lenses at a 104 px pitch, 30 px apart, with S1 / S2 / MOV key labels under them; each lens shows the ability's icon clipped to it, the cooldown sweep and a readiness ring outside it, 86 px across) and a time-tools panel (the rewind badge 46 with its count, the Beacon anchors in Act III, the Time Freeze badge 46, 48 px after whatever precedes it). The top-right holds the dust plate and the 112 px Integrity dial. The earlier `20 × 20` readiness radials and the glyph text characters are replaced by icon textures (shape distinguishes each status, lock state and Defy state; the `Label` nodes keep their text and tooltips for accessibility). The Ultimate keeps a ring in Story: charging reads as the meter percent on a gold gradient arc, ready as a full gold ring **plus** READY (never hue alone), suppressed as a cold ring. The rewind badge and number turn `Danger` at one or no charges left. Time Freeze shows ready in `Res2` with no ring, active as a ring on the remaining 5 s with the seconds, cooldown as a ring on the remaining 45 s in `Ink2`. A refused press draws a red circle contracting round the lens (D10).

**Integrity dial (since 2026-10-27 the glass dial; the pulp dial was a stopwatch).** A thin edge ring round a 112 px glass disc; the remaining Integrity is a gold (`UIPalette.Res`) annulus wedge, clockwise from 12 o'clock over a darker remainder, under twelve ticks. It turns `Danger` below the Collapse Tremor's first threshold (20 %, read from `TimelineIntegrityRules`); while the clock is paused the wedge reads `Ink2` at 0.6 (the pulp gauge read slate) and the siphon streams stop. The caption sits 14 px to its left, centred on it, the percent beneath, one cold siphon stream per living Extractor (at most five drawn), and at the pre-boss checkpoint the dial gives way to the sealed note, right-aligned under the dust plate.

**Boss bar (BOSS-A since 2026-10-27; it replaced the BOSS-B pulp nameplate).** Top centre, in the free band between the left stack (plus 24 px) and the dust plate (minus 24 px): `min(840 s, 1920 − 1008 s)` wide at UI scale `s` (840 at 100 %, 509 at 140 %; never under 320). A 12 px boss-red diamond leads the boss name (Cinzel, shrinking to fit, never below 22.8 px on screen); the plain label `Phase {n}` (one-based) sits right-aligned on the name's baseline and drops under the name when the two cannot share the row; below them the glass boss tube `840 × 34` (a boss-red edge, an opaque dark track, a white damage tracker, the boss-red gradient fill, no cap or ticks), a white notch with a gold diamond under it at each phase threshold, and the value (the numeral and a dimmed "/max") 16 px inside its right end. **Fighter HUD (HUD-F2).** The Fighter HUD hangs from the **top** of the screen (it replaces the earlier bottom placement; the bottom stays free for Open-stage pits): one cluster per player at the top corners (36 px side margin, 22 px top), the match timer plate (`234 × 111.6`, a glass slab with the mode line beneath the digits; the pulp plate was `240 × 118`) at the top centre over a dark top shade, and a lens row per player under its cluster. A cluster is a 110 px chamfered glass portrait frame, the player's slot shape (P1 triangle, P2 circle, 30 px) and name (a long name shrinks to fit, never below 18 px), an HP tube `510 × 44` (was `400 × 28`; P2's tube is mirrored: it leans the other way and anchors at the outer right) with the HP numerals 14 px inside its inner end, a row of stock stars (28 px, filled in the slot colour while held, an outline when lost), block pips (24 px) and the Defy icon (32 px), then the Ultimate tube. P2 mirrors P1. Each cluster keeps its screen corner at every UI scale, and the HP tube shrinks before it meets the timer plate (`clamp(510 s, 300, (960 − 117 s − 24) − (36 + 130 s))` screen px, 554 at 140 %; the pulp rule gave up half the shortfall, floor 240), the Ultimate tube keeping 390 / 510 of it; the status chips drop their text column below a 1700 px canvas. In Sudden Death the plate shows only its line, widening to fit.

**Fighter Ultimate meter.** The Ultimate meter is a **bar**, `390 × 16 px`, directly under each HP bar and filling from the outer edge (a glass tube since 2026-10-27, P2's mirrored), replacing the earlier `48 × 48` circular indicator. A full meter shows a gold gradient with a soft glow and no number (the Story Ultimate remains a ring).

**HP and meter gauges (GAUGE-A since 2026-10-27; the pulp bars were skewed plates with a hatch overlay).** Gauges are glass tubes: a leaning parallelogram track with a gold hairline edge over a dark inner gradient, and a gradient fill running from the anchored to the moving end, whose moving end is vertical and carries a white cap, under a top highlight and decile ticks. The damage tracker (`#FF5A5A`) holds 0.5 s and then drains over 0.6 s (the pulp tracker dropped in six steps); a heal stops the tracker at the healed fill (the Fighter tube raises its fill over 0.2 s; the Story tubes snap); the Rally echo band (`#FFF7DA`) extends from the HP fill by the pooled echo, inset 22 % of the tube's height; and low HP (25 % or less, provisional) pulses a 7 px red glow round the track every 2 s. There is no hit flash (the pulp flash blackened the plate for one frame). Reduced Temporal Effects hold the low-HP glow steady, make the tracker wait 0.2 s and then land at once, and land a Fighter heal at once.

**Fit.** The Story HUD's left stack (the level title, the objective and the three panels) and its right cluster scale about their screen corners and the boss bar fills the band between them; the Fighter clusters and timer plate keep their screen corners and the HP tubes shrink before they meet the plate. So nothing overlaps at UI scale 90–140 %: the clusters stay inside the frame and overlap neither each other, the timer plate, the level title nor the boss bar (`StoryHudBoundsTests`, `FighterHudTopBandTests`), and the checkpoint toast and the first-use tooltip keep to the same free band.

**Typography and contrast.** HUD text uses Outfit (numerals tabular) with Cinzel on names, at 18 px or more at 1080p; text over the scene, a fill or a lens carries a dark rim and shadow (the scene halo), text on glass none; text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large display text). Status and ownership never rely on colour alone.

Nobody has looked at any of this in play, on a pad, or at 140 % (`VERIFY-UI-GLASS-HUMAN-REVIEW`; the pulp look it replaced was rejected after one look in the game).
