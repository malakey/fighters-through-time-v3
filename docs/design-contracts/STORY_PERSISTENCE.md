# Story persistence contract — F10 Option A

> **Amended 2026-09-28 (story review S27):** the per-character Legacy Level (4A) is retired, and with it F04 (the Nexus Resonance Source) and F12's two-checkpoint 4A structure. Every rule below that applies only to 4A, F04 or the Legacy checkpoint contract is history, not design of record; the shared-level rules are unchanged. The ending counts Levels 2–15 (14 IDs).

Decision: 2026-09-12. Resume at a checkpoint while preserving the current attempt's spent resources and durable progress. This contract supplements the save tables in [the main design](../design-godot-v7.md) and the [F05 economy ledger](DUST_ECONOMY.md). It specifies required behavior; save code, migrations, scene manifests, and runtime validation remain pending.

## Boundaries and recovery

An attempt is identified by the story slot, level ID, and a unique `attemptID`. Loading a scene, returning from the hub, activating a checkpoint, dying, or replaying a presentation does not create an attempt. Only first entry into the next uncompleted level or explicit full Restart Level creates one.

| Boundary/event | Destination and recovery | State retained or reset |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh level entry | Authored entrance; 100% HP, full difficulty rewind pool, full block charges, zero Ultimate meter, abilities ready; Integrity 100% where enabled | New attempt; clear level claims/uses and Defy-used; Time Freeze ready; Act III anchors Easy 3 / Normal 2 / Hard 1, otherwise zero. Preserve campaign progress, purchased grid and deposited dust. |
| Full Restart Level | Same initialization, from level beginning | Atomically discard active wallet, claims, puzzle/Extractor/secret state, checkpoints, pending level bonus/recovery, and Smothered; new attempt ID. Preserve deposited dust, grid, completed levels and Legacy unlocks. Restart is the explicit cost-bearing way to replenish attempt resources. |
| New checkpoint activation | Remain in play; save anchor and current Integrity after its activation effects | Apply Mending and Easy-full/Normal+1/Hard-none rewind refresh once for that checkpoint ID. Persist activation/benefit flags; revisiting or loading cannot apply them again. Hard's inactive checkpoints do nothing. |
| Ordinary Save, quit/resume or crash recovery | Reconstruct the level at the last activated checkpoint, or implicit entrance; retain latest durable HP, meter, rewind/anchor counts, Integrity, spent flags, and cooldowns | No heal, charge refill, cooldown reset, new checkpoint activation, or replayed reward. A deliberate live-attempt exit charges the existing 20% once; crash recovery charges nothing. Saving in place does not teleport until a later load. |
| Death rewind | In uninterrupted play use the existing grounded-history landing and difficulty HP restoration, then the 60-frame Post-Landing Hold and existing invincibility from the thaw (R03) | Spend one rewind once; persist the resulting HP and count, including 1 → 0. Keep anchors, Defy-used, world claims, uses, wallet and Integrity. T01a clears temporary effects on the hero and Rally; settle outstanding damage-taken meter once with the recovery event. Surviving world objects retain remaining state in uninterrupted play under the [temporal contract](TEMPORAL_STATE_CONTRACT.md#death-rewind-temporary-combat-effects-t01a--option-a). Reload during its presentation instead reconstructs the checkpoint, discards transient objects and finishes the committed recovery without another spend, repeated meter settlement or a full heal. A save during the Post-Landing Hold stores the landed state; reload never regrants the hold. |
| Acts I–II Timeline Collapse | Hub, then checkpoint resume; retain same attempt | Commit existing dust fee once and recovery to 100% HP/full rewind pool. Keep Defy-used, uses, claims, meter and cooldowns. Restore the checkpoint Integrity allowance; apply the F11 authored checkpoint/difficulty minimum (at least 25%) only for a granted timer-caused recovery. |
| Act III Anchor Snap | Checkpoint in place; retain same attempt | Same paid recovery as Collapse, plus spend one anchor once. Spending the last anchor 1 → 0 still completes the Snap. Loading cannot replenish that anchor. |
| Act III collapse with zero anchors | Smothered Game Over | Persist Smothered before its presentation. No playable recovery, HP/pool refill, or hub route. Load returns to Game Over; only explicit Restart Level starts a fresh attempt. |
| Level completion (N01: accepted sealing interaction after boss defeat) | Hub in Acts I–II; next level in Act III; ending flow after Level 15 | Commit retained reward/bonus, deposit, completed-level marker, final Integrity, unlocks and destination once. Initialize the next attempt only once when it is entered. Final rewards cannot fund the already-finished final fight. |

The existing 20% fee means `fee = floor(levelChronalDust * 0.20)`; subtract that fee once. Smothered's triggering collapse is one failure event. Quit from its Game Over screen, or quit from an already-settled recovery/results screen, does not charge that same event again. A later exit after actually resuming active gameplay is a new voluntary exit. Reloading a fee or completion overlay never repeats the transaction.

For eligible lethal hits, a death-prevention event resolves before death rewind: save `defyHistoryUsed = true`, HP 1 and meter expenditure together. Story Defy is once per attempt: death rewind, Collapse, Snap, hub visit and loading do not reset it. Fresh entry/full Restart Level does. Fighter's once-per-match rule is unchanged. **D04:** uninterrupted play protects immediately, releases the triggering hit's forced reaction/capture and grants 60 active protection ticks after normal control returns. Checkpoint reconstruction clears the transient protection and pending presentation while retaining committed HP, meter and spent use; loading cannot replay the proc or grant a fresh second. See [the defensive contract](DEFENSIVE_EFFECTS.md#d04--defy-grants-a-brief-protected-recovery). **F16 Option B:** all Story bottomless-pit/void boundary crossings are non-hit lethal falls, so skip Defy without consuming its used flag. Resolve one ordinary death rewind if a charge exists; otherwise Collapse/Snap/Smothered applies. No fall-specific fee, Rally echo or damage-based meter credit. Fall-caused paid recovery uses the non-timer checkpoint allowance; simultaneous Integrity-zero takes timer-collapse priority without an additional rewind spend. Persist the cause/result once; Time Freeze does not prevent boundary death. Normal drops between playable areas are not kill boundaries.

## Lightweight state, not exact combat suspension

Ordinary loading restores the latest durable player resources and Integrity, rather than their older checkpoint values. This explicitly supersedes the former checkpoint-time HP/meter and universal checkpoint-gauge reload wording. It does not restore the player's last position or an enemy's interrupted attack.

Rebuild ordinary encounters from an authored checkpoint baseline: encounters cleared before that checkpoint stay cleared; encounters beyond it can respawn at their authored state. Bind baseline membership to stable encounter IDs, not an enemy's physical position at death. Overlay the attempt's persistent facts before enabling gameplay: broken Extractors remain broken, found secrets remain found, solved latched gates remain solved, used healing remains used, and claimed rewards stay claimed. A respawned enemy may fight again but cannot reissue a claimed reward. **T01b:** First Severed history, its once-per-encounter rewind flag and pending phase event belong to the live boss encounter. A live player Death Rewind preserves that encounter; checkpoint reconstruction discards its pending event and initializes the authored undefeated encounter baseline with new encounter-local history/phase flags. Ordinary loading does not resume a partial boss rewind or revive a completed boss; claimed rewards remain claimed. See the [boss rewind contract](TEMPORAL_STATE_CONTRACT.md#first-severed-capped-historical-recovery-t01b--option-a). Scene manifests must keep pending pickups and mandatory gates reachable from every supported recovery anchor.

Projectiles, temporary constructs, combat statuses/marks, grabs, attack phases and movement history are discarded during checkpoint reconstruction; baseline platforms/hazards restart in authored safe phases. Preserve the player's actual HP/meter, block charges and remaining lockout/regeneration timers, ability/Echo Step/Time Freeze cooldowns, and unfinished healing entitlement. Resume idle at the anchor; do not award a fresh combat buff or invincibility merely for an ordinary load. KO/recovery invincibility already earned by a committed death rewind is a separate pending recovery benefit. F07 ownership/remaining-time rules still apply when an actual instance is restored in other systems; checkpoint reconstruction restores no old Conductive mark or vortex allowance.

Discarding a Rally pool on reconstruction grants no HP; settle its still-uncredited damage-taken meter once as expired echo in the recovery transaction, bounded by the ordinary meter cap. D03f bases new damage/echo records on actual HP removed after defenses/Defy, excluding absorbed damage and overkill. Persist committed HP/meter/echo and settled amounts together; loading cannot revalue or award the same damage record again. Never replay damage-dealt credit. Clear temporary buffs as well as negative statuses. This checkpoint reset is not evidence of an exact-position combat save or a universal in-combat escape.

### Integrity and level progress

Save live `currentIntegrity` with the current Extractor/secret facts. Keep `checkpointIntegrity` as the separate allowance used by paid Collapse/Snap recovery. For a non-timer recovery use that allowance; for a granted timer recovery use `max(checkpointIntegrity, recoveryMinimum)`, once, using the [F11 authored checkpoint/difficulty budget](CHECKPOINT_RECOVERY.md). Recompute future drain from persistent living-Extractor and secret facts against the attempt's fixed starting Extractor count and normalized F01 denominator. No ordinary load, death rewind or machine destruction grants time.

Persist pre-boss lock and final gate Integrity together. Reload keeps a locked boss clock locked and uses the already-recorded score; it cannot award a different tier by rerunning the gate. Solved puzzle state and its required access mechanisms must restore together. **N02:** completion/sealed state refers to the mission's repaired-moment/wound ID, explicitly mapped from its stable level/variant ID; it is not an era-wide completion flag. Preserve that scope in the completion record or an immutable versioned mapping. Shared-level and 4A wounds remain independent, so loading/migration cannot reopen a completed repair or auto-complete another mission in the same era. See [sealing scope](NARRATIVE_RESOLUTION.md#n02--sealed-moments-stay-sealed). **V01b:** local Reset Puzzle restores only the selected unsolved puzzle's registered props/mechanisms, overlaid with persistent solved/latch/access facts; preserve player resources, encounters, reward claims and live Integrity. Recheck solved state and apply a reset generation atomically to reject old callbacks. Same-instance restoration retains that generation; ordinary checkpoint reconstruction rebuilds unsolved transient arrangements without replaying the interaction. See [the puzzle-reset contract](CAMPAIGN_VALIDATION.md#v01b--local-reset-for-an-unsolved-puzzle). The F04 Nexus source is never saved armed: an unsolved puzzle restores its available source; a solved gate keeps it disabled.

**F11 Option A:** `recoveryMinimum = max(25, ceil(100 / (parSeconds * difficultyMultiplier) * remainingRouteSeconds * (1 + safetyMarginFraction)))`, with a provisional 20% margin. Use the conservative all-alive rate and measured mandatory retry route, including Tremor/mechanism delays. Values above 100 are invalid budgets; missing measurements remain pending. Save the resolved minimum, budget version and granted gauge in the recovery event before presentation; reload never recalculates that committed event. Actual route sufficiency and scoring impact still require validation. F12 specifies exactly Entry and PreBoss for every 4A variant, both active on every difficulty; see [Legacy checkpoint mappings](LEGACY_CHECKPOINTS.md). Persist actual stable IDs and roles through validated per-variant migration, never inferred suffixes.

## Required schema and ownership

The existing root Story fields remain where specified; add root `attemptState: StoryAttemptState` for the records below. Do not duplicate mutable copies of a root field inside it. The following table completes the abbreviated main/HTML schema.

| Owner / field | Type and meaning | Commit trigger / reset boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Global `seenDialogueIDs` | Set of completed or explicitly skipped presentation IDs across slots | Union after completion/confirmed skip; never cleared by level restart or slot change |
| Story root `currentHP`, `currentUltimateMeter`, `currentLives` | Latest durable HP, meter and death-rewind count, not old checkpoint resource values | Damage/resource/heal changes and recovery; only explicit rules replenish |
| Story root `checkpointIntegrity` | Gauge recorded on checkpoint activation; entrance allowance before first checkpoint | New checkpoint; fresh/restart initializes |
| Story root `levelChronalDust`, `depositedChronalDust`, `gridProgress` | Active wallet, deposited balance, owned grid IDs | F05 claim/loss/completion and purchase/respec transactions; restart clears active wallet only |
| Story root progression | `completedLevels`, `levelIntegrity`, `unlockedLegacyAbilities`, `isCompleted` | Completion/script-effect transaction; preserve on restart |
| `attemptState.attemptID`, `levelID`, `revision` | Unique attempt identity, owning level, monotonically ordered save revision | New ID only on fresh/restart; increment revision per committed change |
| `attemptState.status` | Active, AwaitingHubResume, RecoveryPending, Smothered, CompletionPending, Completed, or LegacyRecoveryRequired | Commit transitions before presentation; choose load destination from this state |
| `attemptState.anchorChargesRemaining` | Integer 0..difficulty cap; zero outside Act III | Spend on Snap; initialize only on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.defyHistoryUsed` | Boolean | Set atomically with prevented lethal hit; clear only on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.checkpointRecord` | Anchor ID/role, baseline encounter IDs/version, activated checkpoint IDs and granted checkpoint-benefit IDs | Checkpoint activation with HP/rewind benefit; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.currentIntegrity`, `startingExtractorCount` | Current gauge and immutable initial machine count | Live timer checkpoints / critical events; initialize on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.destroyedExtractorIDs`, `foundSecretIDs` | Stable sets, including Act III stand-ins | Commit destruction/discovery with source issuance and new drain facts; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.puzzleStateByID` | Solved/latch states and persistent prerequisite mechanisms | Successful puzzle event; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.preBossLocked`, `finalGateIntegrity` | Freeze/score state; final score absent until gate reached | Same transaction as pre-boss activation; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.fontUsesByID`, `collectedHealingIDs` | Remaining Font uses and consumed Feast/Salve/pickup IDs | Commit consumption with HP or pending healing; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.pendingHealing` | Source ID, uncredited heal amount and remaining live duration | Font channel completion spends once; apply remaining healing over its saved duration; never replay credited HP |
| `attemptState.rewardSources` | F05 stable source states: unissued, spawned/uncollected, collected; quantity, pickup type/location, and fixed random-drop outcome where relevant | Issue once and commit claim with wallet/benefit; restore pending pickup once; clear on fresh/restart |
| `attemptState.playerResourceTimers` | Block charges, regen/lockout, ability/Echo Step cooldowns, Rally uncredited meter, Time Freeze remaining cooldown, and D02d Wardenclyffe remaining damage-recharge delay (temporary shield charge is discarded on reconstruction) | Resource event and periodic durable snapshot; no offline progression or load refill |
| `attemptState.recoveryEvent` | Unique event ID, cause, resolved HP/rewind/anchor/wallet/Integrity outcome and remaining recovery entitlement; F11 checkpoint/role, difficulty, budget version and resolved recovery minimum | Resolve death/Collapse/Snap before animation; finish once on resume without another fee/spend |
| `attemptState.sealReadiness` | Committed boss-defeated/ready state and stable sealing-anchor ID, alongside existing pending boss pickup state | N01 boss defeat enables interaction; preserve until accepted sealing, no premature completion/deposit |
| `attemptState.completionTransaction` | ID, retained base, bonus, deposit amount, final score, sealed/local-siphon inactive result, destination and applied marker | N01 accepted sealing; retained until its transition is safely finalized |
| `attemptState.appliedScriptEffectIDs`, `pendingGlobalSeenIDs` | Per-attempt gameplay effects already applied and global presentation updates awaiting acknowledgement | Watched/skipped sequence resolution; progress itself lives in root fields; pending global updates drain by idempotent union |
| `attemptState.presentationFlags` | First Tremor bark, recovery/boss intro viewing flags where not already global | After qualifying presentation; never control gameplay rewards/unlocks |

All sets/maps use stable authored IDs qualified by level and attempt where appropriate. Player-derived max HP and caps come from current legal grid/difficulty; clamp restored resource values without granting a refill. Validate F08 grid prerequisites and its paid-cost refund migration as part of slot migration.

A Font interrupted before its 1.5-second channel completes consumes no use and stores no heal. Once the channel completes, the use decrement and pending two-second heal commit together. Repeated loads resume only the uncredited remainder. Pending healing is cleared by a death recovery that replaces HP with its specified recovery value, without refunding the spent Font.

## Save ordering and crash limits

Capture immutable state on the game thread; serialize/write asynchronously through one ordered writer per slot. Ignore obsolete revisions; never let an older asynchronous write replace a newer one. Store related fields in one authenticated, atomic slot transaction using the existing temporary-file/swap/backup mechanism.

Critical events include accepted resource expenditures, Defy, rewind/Snap/Smothered, checkpoint benefits, healing/pickup consumption, Extractor/secret/puzzle changes, grid spending/refunds, voluntary exit and completion. Commit before acknowledging the event as safely saved or continuing past a recovery/scene-transition boundary. Couple irreversible rewards and expenditures to durable event IDs; resume a committed pending event instead of reissuing it. On write failure, retain the prior valid save and pending event, report that saving failed, and prevent a transition from pretending it saved successfully.

Continuous HP/meter/timer changes are checkpointed at least once per live second and folded into every critical event snapshot. An orderly Save/exit flushes the latest state. A crash can lose uncommitted continuous progress since the last successful write; it does not promise exact crash-frame restoration. No offline clock/cooldown advance and no crash fee. A critical effect cannot be claimed durable until its commit succeeds.

Time Freeze activation commits a recovery cooldown of 45 seconds for any reload during that activation. Background snapshots retain this conservative reload value while the live five-second freeze continues; they do not shorten the live effect. An explicit Save or exit during the active freeze ends it and saves 45 seconds. Once thawed, snapshots store the actual remaining cooldown. Reload never resumes or renews the five-second effect. This clarifies F03's saving-during-freeze rule without turning periodic autosaves into early thaws.

Load one coherent primary or backup revision. Never combine a newer wallet or global gameplay effect with older claims or charges. Backup fallback may lose later progress and must say so; it is not a promise of zero rollback. Global seen-dialogue union can be retried independently because it grants no gameplay value.

## Dialogue, completion, and migration

**N05 ending persistence:** equally count exactly Levels 2–15 (14 IDs; S27), excluding 0/1. Use completed-level PreBoss Integrity at its stored precision; the clean ending requires their sum to be at least 700 percentage points (50% across 14 levels; S27). At Level 15 sealing, commit its final record, completion, selected ending variant and rule version atomically before presentation. Reload/skip resumes that committed choice. Missing/invalid required records need trustworthy migration/backup recovery, never guessed values or a smaller denominator; preserve existing committed endings under their recorded rule version. See [the complete calculation and validation contract](NARRATIVE_RESOLUTION.md#n05--equal-weight-ending-average).

Presentation ID and gameplay-effect ID are separate. Normal completion and confirmed skipping call the same idempotent gameplay-effect resolution: unlock Legacy abilities, latch gates, commit completion or select a destination as authored. Seeing a scene in another slot suppresses only the first-skip confirmation; it never suppresses another slot's gameplay effects. An interrupted unresolved scene resumes its required flow; a resolved scene cannot award its effects twice.

Do not wait for credits to be watched to secure the final boss's rewards, completion and ending state. Complete them before the presentation/transition; credits viewed or skipped selects the same post-completion destination. Crash recovery of a completion overlay does not reopen the defeated level or replay its deposit. A boss reward that is still an uncollected physical pickup remains pending under F05; loading its already-defeated boss must keep the pickup/completion trigger accessible. **N01 Option B:** boss defeat enters AwaitingSeal with no deposit/unlock. The player uses the marked anchor's Interact action to commit sealing and the existing completion transaction before its vignette. Restore readiness and the pending pickup once on a pre-seal load; after commit, resume the completion flow without resealing or duplicate rewards. Local siphon shutdown is not destruction/secret credit and cannot alter locked final Integrity. Normal live-attempt resource/quit/recovery rules remain. See [narrative resolution](NARRATIVE_RESOLUTION.md).

Migration must be versioned and validated, not just default every new field. Derive state from existing trustworthy records where possible. Never infer full anchors, unused Defy, unused healing, or unclaimed sources for an already-active legacy attempt. If critical attempt history cannot be reconstructed, preserve the original save and all balances, mark LegacyRecoveryRequired and explain that the existing explicit Restart Level is needed to continue; only that choice clears the active wallet under the normal restart rule. Do not silently restart, grant resources, or claim the legacy state was recovered. Assign actual schema version numbers during implementation after inspecting shipped formats.

## Required validation

All runtime checks are pending. Test each boundary on Easy/Normal/Hard, including Acts I–II and Act III:

- Save/load after spending a rewind, the final rewind, an anchor, the final anchor, and Defy; interrupt each recovery presentation before/after commit. Each spends once; zero remaining is a valid living state after a successful rewind/Snap.
- Quit/crash while Smothered; repeated loads return to Game Over. Restart creates exactly one fresh attempt and clears only the specified level state.
- Revisit/reload checkpoints; no repeated Mending or rewind refresh. Persist Font partial healing, Feast/Salve claims, and random-drop outcomes without rerolling or refunding use.
- Restore claimed and pending pickups across respawned encounters; validate Extractor/secret overlaps, puzzle access, and an unsolved F04 source. No duplicate reward or unreachable mandatory gate.
- Save/load Integrity with changed machine/secret facts and after the pre-boss lock. Ordinary loading never substitutes checkpoint Integrity; timer-collapse allowance applies once. Validate the authored F11 minimum, upward rounding and safety margin; reject missing or above-100 budgets, and verify interrupted recovery never recalculates an already-committed grant.
- Exercise Time Freeze across background autosave, explicit Save, quit, crash, death rewind and Snap; enforce 5-second live duration and the separate 45-second cooldown.
- Interrupt fee, deposit, grid purchase/respec and Act III transition writes; compare a complete pre-event or post-event state, never mixed balances/claims. Reopening a settled overlay adds no fee or deposit.
- Watch/skip/interrupt every gameplay-bearing sequence, including first-skip confirmation and cross-slot seen IDs; obtain identical unlocks, gates, completion and destination. Verify final rewards survive a crash before credits finish.
- Exercise queued-write ordering, failed writes, primary/backup fallback and incomplete legacy migrations. Preserve original evidence and clearly distinguish tested behavior from this design contract.
