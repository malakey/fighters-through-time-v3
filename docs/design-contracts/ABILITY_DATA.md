# Ability data contract — A15

Design revision: 2026-09-29; **amended 2026-11-03 (gameplay review): Momentum regenerates a pip every 9 s, the running attack, Leonardo's plain synced bolt stops on contact, and nine capstone Majors (end of file).** The user selected Option A of ability review item A15: one table per ability family, collected from the approved GDD kit entries, the construct specifications and the resolved review decisions. It adds no new numbers. A cell reading **Unspecified** marks a value the design has not yet decided; fill it only through an explicit design decision, never by copying an unreviewed build value.

**Authority (P04).** The GDD kit entries and adopted contracts define intended behavior; this table mirrors them in one place. When a value changes, update the GDD kit entry, `characters.html` and this table in the same change. Verified `resources/Abilities/*.tres` values describe a build revision and do not override the design; record disagreements in the [design/build ledger](DESIGN_BUILD_DEVIATIONS.md).

**Shared rules.** Frames are 60 Hz. **Special cooldowns are retired (2026-10-31):** every Special costs one Momentum pip (a four-pip gauge refilled by passive regeneration — one pip every **9 s** since 2026-11-03, 12 s before — and landed basic swings; start 2) and arms a 2 s per-slot reuse delay, and no Special authors a cooldown. The former 6–14 s band (M08) and the Resonance Momentum refunds survive only as the replaced values in the table below. A blocked ordinary Special costs 2 charges; a Shield-Breaker shatters (A01), after its authored startup in both modes (decision 1.3) and even inside the perfect-block window (Q3). Persistent-object hits count as Basic-class (1 charge) and are impulse-free. Venom deals 3 per 1.0 s tick at intensity 1.0, scaling with intensity (C02, raised from 2 by the lingering-kit pass), and while a fighter is poisoned every heal except Defy's is blocked (Rally reclaim, healing orbs, the Restoration Font, the checkpoint Mending heal). **Lingering kit (2026-10-31):** every hero-kit Time Dilation slow is intensity 1.2 (x0.4 run speed and jump) and lingers 1.75 s after its source stops refreshing; a boss's move multiplier from it is floored at x0.7. A construct (Tesla coil, Leonardo turret, Cleopatra serpent nest) keeps a weak, slow attack of its own that spends nothing, holds a fixed number of charges, and fires a stronger synced strike (Basic class, hitstun, 0.05 Momentum pip) each time its owner lands a basic hit or a direct Special hit on a target inside the synced reach (a 2D circle); it fizzles when its charges or lifetime run out. A synced strike, the Lorentz discharge, a Rift collapse, a Vortex final pulse and Golden Ratio carry a combo marker: inside the owner's own Special cast it arms the recovery cancel, and it pays Momentum once (0.2 pip for a detonation, 0.05 for a strike or Golden Ratio); it earns no Rally echo and does not count as a swing landed. Every movement ability shares the universal 5 s cooldown and is provisional under A07. Damage anchors: a special ≈1.5× a full string (band 1.2–1.8×), an Ultimate ≈4–5× a full string (roster band 70–84).

**One Special timing in both modes (2026-10-16).** User decision 1.2: **"Full timing now: every Fighter Special gets its authored startup, active and recovery frames, matching Story."** The startup / recovery column below is therefore the timing of both modes. An accepted press spends a Momentum pip, arms the slot's reuse delay and the ability lockout at once, a whiff included (decision 1.1(2)); the effect fires on the first active frame, and the caster is locked until its recovery ends. A grounded cast brakes to a stop over the 4-frame run stop and an aerial cast keeps its drift (plan question Q1: **"Brake to a stop, both modes"**), except where the kit writes its own motion (Divine Piercing's lunge); an aerial cast falls at Story's half gravity, now one shared constant (in the sim, a cast started in the air, through its startup and active frames). A whiff keeps its whole recovery, with a grounded Block cancel from recovery frame 0 as its only exit. A Special that landed a direct hit may also cancel its own recovery (D3; follow-up 11.1: **"Extend D3 to Fighter: one rule in both modes — a whiff keeps its full recovery; a Special that landed a direct hit cancels into roll/jump from recovery frame 0, into a basic or the movement ability from frame 6."**), never into a Special or the Ultimate; ticks, constructs, the Rift, the Vortex, the Tempest and Cleopatra's two Specials never arm it. Where the column reads Unspecified, both modes now run the build's `.tres` frames, which are build evidence, not design.

## Ultimates

Every Ultimate costs 100 meter, is unblockable (eligible Temporal Aegis and HP barriers still absorb — D03b), bypasses enemy damage reduction and hyper-armor, and earns its caster no meter (D03h). In Fighter Mode each opens with an avoidable activation strike (A02; provisional 20 wind-up / 10 active / 45 whiff recovery); the cinematic plays only on contact. The wind-up shows a steady Unblockable-red tint and the Unblockable X glyph on the caster for exactly its length, with a Critical cue, in Fighter and on the Level 13 Mirror clone (decision 2.1; it is built steady rather than flashing, and whether that matches the ruling's "red flash" is open for the user), and no Ultimate is accepted from the block stance or shieldstun in either mode (decision 2.3). Story Mode keeps the screen-clearing versions; the Story hero's Ultimate is armoured through its own startup, so a hit there deals damage but does not interrupt it (decision 2.4 with plan question Q2: **"Startup only (a Story rule; `.tres` flags stay false)"**). An Ultimate-class hit still interrupts it, and its active frames are not armoured.

## Gameplay review package (2026-11-03)

From the user's gameplay-review rulings of 2026-11-03 (`docs/recon-2026-11-03/DECISIONS_gameplay_review.md`; plan `docs/GAMEPLAY_REVIEW_2026-11-03_PLAN.md`). Every number below the user did not state is provisional.

**Momentum regen (rulings 10a and 10a+, both modes).** One pip every **9 s** (540 frames; 12 s / 720 frames before). The lockout and the reuse delay stay.
