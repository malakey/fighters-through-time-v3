# Shared temporal-state contract

Design revision: 2026-09-12. The user approved the recommended consolidation and Echo Step's exact-destination policy. This contract specifies design; implementation, serialization migration and runtime validation remain pending. It collects the existing F03/F10/F11/F16/F22 decisions. T01a (Death Rewind temporary combat effects) is resolved by user-selected Option A on 2026-09-12. T01b (boss rewind) is resolved by user-selected Option A on 2026-09-12: historical recovery capped at 20% maximum HP, with a safe destination and a 1.5-second suspended-combat presentation.

## State matrix

"Retain" means retain current state, never restore its historical value. Suspended timers resume their remaining duration without catch-up ticks. A menu pause is separate from live simulation. Checkpoint reconstruction follows [Story persistence](STORY_PERSISTENCE.md); Fighter round transitions follow [match rules](FIGHTER_MATCH_RULES.md).

## Echo Step: exact destination, validated twice

1. At the start of each authoritative 60 Hz simulation tick, before evaluating action input and that tick's movement, record the fighter's current position with tick ID `t`. Retain **31 consecutive samples**, including `t`, so `t - 30` remains available after writing the newest sample. Resolve by exact tick ID; do not round to a nearby sample or interpolate. Paused simulation adds no samples; an advancing hitstop tick may record the same position.
2. On legal Block+Roll input during the existing eligible recovery window, read `position[t - 30]` and lock it as the pending destination. The 8-frame wind-up does not move the lookback window. The ghost shows this exact locked destination.
3. Validate the destination using the fighter's full collision shape against the current world: no solid overlap, no authored kill region and no crossing into an unavailable room. One-way platforms follow the normal placement/collision rules. The intervening path need not be clear: this is a teleport. Airborne destinations remain legal; support is not required for Echo Step. A CPU may additionally reject perceived pit/threat risk under F19.
4. If history is insufficient, any normal eligibility/resource check fails, or the destination is blocked, **reject before spending meter or starting cooldown**. Do not start the wind-up or search for a nearby substitute. Leave the existing attack/recovery to its ordinary rules.
5. Accepted activation atomically spends 30 meter, starts the 120-frame cooldown and arms the 8-frame wind-up once. At completion, recheck that same destination against then-current geometry before moving. If now blocked, cancel the teleport, remove the ghost and retain the committed cost/cooldown; no refund, destination search or extra recovery penalty. If otherwise controllable, return to ordinary locomotion at the current position; an intervening hitstun/grab/death state keeps authority.
6. An effective interruption that makes the wind-up illegal cancels it; never fire the pending teleport after hitstun, a grab or death. Existing immunity/armor and interruption rules determine whether a hit actually interrupts. Echo Step grants no new protection. On successful arrival, zero velocity, preserve facing and choose grounded/airborne locomotion from actual destination contact.

Initialize a new history generation at spawn, checkpoint reconstruction, Death Rewind landing and other forced relocation to a new recovery anchor, including Sudden Death setup. Storage may be filled with the spawn coordinate, but only the actual first sample is valid: Echo Step requires 30 subsequent simulation ticks before an exact 30-frame-old sample exists. Never treat filled slots as a fabricated earlier timeline. An ordinary movement teleport, including successful Echo Step, does not by itself reset continuous history.

Snapshot the ring, head, latest sample tick, valid count, generation, cooldown, armed flag, locked destination, activation tick and remaining wind-up frames. Restore them together on rollback, including an already-debited activation; never reapply the cost because a ghost is recreated. This design requirement also applies to local Fighter play. Package 7 remains deferred; this does not claim its broader state inventory is complete.

## Death Rewind: landing clearance and fallback

Search grounded samples oldest to newest within the 300-frame buffer; "deepest" means the oldest **valid** sample, not an unconditional coordinate from the past. Test the complete player shape for solid clearance, present support and exclusion from kill regions. Closed gates, collapsed supports and moving objects can invalidate historical positions.

Validate against the world configuration that will exist at landing, including a PathMovingPlatform's reversed path state. Check again before releasing the player. If a selected landing fails, continue through eligible grounded history, then the last-known grounded fallback, then the last activated physical checkpoint, then the implicit entrance. Validate every fallback; never restore a crumbling platform, open a solved/closed gate differently, or erase progress solely to make a coordinate usable. The camera re-confines to the selected room.

One recovery event spends one rewind and grants the same difficulty HP regardless of which fallback is required. Rechecking or using an anchor does not charge again, grant checkpoint Mending/refresh, charge a Collapse fee or refill Integrity. Zero displacement is valid if that is the only safe landing; safety takes precedence over a visible travel distance.

Each authored persistent-world configuration must retain a valid recovery checkpoint or entrance. If that invariant fails at runtime, do not place the player in a wall/void or invent another death/charge spend: keep the committed recovery pending, report the invalid recovery configuration and offer reload or the existing explicit Restart Level flow. Reload retains the committed recovery outcome under F10; it cannot repeatedly debit resources.

## Death Rewind: temporary combat effects (T01a — Option A)

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12: **clear effects on the hero; preserve surviving world objects**. This applies to a live Story Death Rewind, including lethal falls. It adds no death-rewind cleanup to a successful Defy History proc, Time Freeze or Echo Step, and changes no Fighter stock-loss rule. D04 separately releases the triggering hit's forced reaction/capture on Defy and protects immediately, then for 60 active ticks after normal control returns; this does not run T01a's hero/world cleanup. Pause that window under the shared effect clock. F10 reconstruction discards its remaining phase/timer without replaying the proc or restoring its spent use; see [the defensive contract](DEFENSIVE_EFFECTS.md#d04--defy-grants-a-brief-protected-recovery).

- **Hero cleanup:** remove both status slots, other temporary debuffs/marks, temporary buffs, shields and ability-granted armor/protection. Cancel the hero's current attack/cast, armed Echo Step, grab/throw attachments, attached hitboxes and continuing hero-bound ability effects. Resolve both sides of a broken attachment without a delayed throw/hit. Cancel pending healing without refund. Permanent purchased grid benefits, unlocked abilities and derived maximum HP remain; ordinary block charges and their regeneration/lockout state are resources, not temporary shields.
- **Rally:** clear the Echo Pool and its drain timer without restoring HP. Settle only still-uncredited damage-taken meter at the normal 0.25-point-per-HP rate, capped at 100, once in the recovery transaction. Never replay credit already earned at hit time or from earlier drain; reclaimable HP is not added on top of the difficulty recovery HP. A kill-boundary fall creates no new echo or damage-taken credit, but any pre-existing outstanding pool follows this settlement. Settlement occurs after the lethal outcome has been resolved; reaching full meter here cannot retroactively fire Defy or cancel the committed rewind.
- **Surviving world objects:** retain deployed constructs, independent zones and already-emitted player projectiles with their current positions, HP, remaining lifetimes, velocities and action/tick state. Preserve spent ammo, hit-target records, deploy-limit membership and per-instance used allowances (including Vortex Step); death cannot refresh or duplicate them. Effects already applied to surviving enemies retain their remaining state unless an authored death/removal rule clears them. Independent deployed effects survive; effects that require the hero's ongoing action or attachment end with that action.
- **Existing exceptions:** clear active enemy projectiles on screen when rewind starts. Preserve Tesla's rule clearing Conductive marks when their target or source Tesla dies, even while his coils survive. Break Siphon tethers on either actor's death; no reattachment or refunded drain. Clear the F04 Nexus authorization and its puzzle-only effects on death, preserving the available-source/solved-gate rules. Other explicit death, source-removal or room-departure rules still apply; this is not an override of authored lifetime conditions.
- **Presentation and thaw:** suspend all surviving combat effects, their status/lifetime timers and tick schedules during the rewind. The authored PathMovingPlatform reversal remains a spatial exception. Resuming does not replay elapsed damage, shots, healing or triggers. Cleanup is removal, not a successful block, shield break, kill or natural expiry that earns a reward or fires an offensive effect. At landing, return control for the 60-frame Post-Landing Hold with the world still suspended (R03); at the thaw, grant the existing 2-second recovery protection as a new entitlement after cleanup; do not restore the hero's old temporary protection. Surviving zones can reapply effects only on their next ordinarily legal contact/tick, respecting that protection and their existing cadence.
- **Resources and persistence:** retain the already-approved HP restoration, rewind expenditure, cooldowns, block state, Defy-used flag, Integrity and world-progress rules. Save Rally settlement and its cleared pending credit with the same unique F10 recovery event as the resulting HP/meter and charge spend. Duplicate callbacks or reload cannot settle it again. Uninterrupted live rewind preserves surviving world objects; loading reconstructs a checkpoint under F10 and discards those transient objects. It does not save or recreate a combat encounter snapshot.

## Post-Landing Hold (R03 — 2026-09-26)

Every Story recovery (death rewind, the checkpoint placement resuming a Collapse, Anchor Snap and the Level 0 scripted rewind) ends with a 60-frame hold: the hero has full control while the Time Freeze frozen set stays frozen, including the hero's own constructs and zones.

- **No credit:** frozen actors are invulnerable; connecting hits are discarded with no damage, stagger, grab, status/mark, meter, Rally reclaim or objective credit.
- **Actions:** movement, jumping, blocking, attacks and Echo Step are legal. Time Freeze, checkpoint strikes, pickups, the Font, puzzle controls and the sealing anchor wait for the thaw.
- **Clocks:** live play. Integrity drains; the Time Freeze cooldown, hero cooldowns, block regeneration and hero status timers run. Frozen actors' schedules stay suspended with no catch-up.
- **Protection:** the 2-second recovery protection starts at the thaw.
- **Thaw:** the Time Freeze thaw warning plays before resumption (kept under Reduced Temporal Effects); the recovery music duck ends at the thaw; `OnRecoveryLanded` and `OnRecoveryWorldThawed` bracket the hold.
- **Boss ordering:** the hold is part of the recovery; a still-valid pending boss transition plays after the thaw.
- **Persistence:** transient. A save during the hold stores the landed state; reload reconstructs the checkpoint without regranting the hold.

## First Unbound: capped historical recovery (T01b — Option A)

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. This is a once-per-encounter Phase 2 transition at the authored **66% HP** threshold. The boss rewinds its own position and recovers toward its HP from **180 simulation frames (3 seconds)** earlier, healing by at most **20% of its current difficulty-scaled maximum HP**. The cap is provisional pending balance validation. Phase 3 remains the existing Borrowed Legacies transition at **33% HP**.

### Trigger, HP and phase ordering

Resolve the triggering damage normally before checking the transition; do not clamp the hit to a phase boundary or discard excess damage. A lethal result defeats the boss and cancels pending phase work: this rewind cannot resurrect it. For a living boss first crossing from above 66% to at/below 66%, capture the historical sample and post-damage HP, set `phase2RewindUsed`, and commit one transition event before presentation. Repeated contacts/callbacks cannot create a second event.

Let `H` be post-trigger damage HP, `P` sampled historical HP and `M` current maximum HP. Resolve `heal = max(0, min(P - H, floor(0.20 * M), M - H))`, then `resultHP = H + heal`. It never lowers HP if the sample is lower, adds a flat 20% regardless of history, or exceeds max HP. For a 60%-HP trigger with 95% historical HP, the result is 80%. At the authored 3,000 base HP, the cap is 600 HP before difficulty scaling. Healing itself grants no damage credit, Rally, meter or reward; subsequent actual hits follow normal combat accounting.

Phase progression is latched, not recalculated backward from healed HP. Consuming the rewind enters Phase 2 even if healing raises HP above 66%. If the same nonlethal damage resolution also crosses 33%, latch that Phase 3 crossing and process it once after the rewind; healing cannot erase the crossing or force the player to earn it again. Do not reset any phase-used flags, thresholds, cooldowns or resource allowances from the historical sample.

### History and safe destination

Record boss position and HP once per authoritative 60 Hz simulation tick, at tick start before movement/input/damage resolution, retaining **181 consecutive samples** with tick IDs. At trigger tick `t`, lock `t - 180`; if younger than 180 ticks, use the oldest real sample from this encounter. Seed one actual sample when combat begins, not fabricated pre-fight history. Use the same locked sample for position and HP even if spatial fallback is required.

Validate full boss collision-shape clearance, arena bounds, kill regions and suitable support against the current arena; airborne placement is legal only where supported by the boss's authored locomotion. Try the historical coordinate, then the boss's current coordinate, then a stable authored safe anchor. Do not move platforms, restore destroyed geometry, search arbitrary nearby points or move the player to make the destination fit. Recheck before arrival; fallback changes only destination, never the sampled heal or once-only event. If no destination is valid, retain the pending transition and report the invalid arena configuration without spawning into danger; use the existing reload/Restart flow. Arena authoring must guarantee a valid fallback.

### Presentation and remaining state

Play a **1.5-second (90 presentation-tick) rewind** with combat suspended: player, boss, other actors, projectiles, constructs, hazards and platforms cannot act or deal damage. Pause statuses, lifetimes, cooldowns, Rally drain and gameplay clocks; the already-locked pre-boss Integrity remains locked. Menu pause also pauses presentation. This is a scripted boss transition, with no Time Freeze activation/cooldown, player rewind spend, Death Rewind projectile clear, platform reversal or hero-effect cleanup.

Cancel the boss's current attack through its normal transition cleanup, removing its attached attack hitboxes and pending emissions; do not reset spent resources or remaining cooldowns. Retain independent emitted attacks/objects and existing statuses/buffs at their remaining state. Other actors' interrupted-by-presentation actions are suspended, not recast/refunded. New combat commands during the presentation are discarded; visual reverse playback cannot fire animation gameplay callbacks.

At completion set the boss's resolved position/HP once, zero its relocation velocity and retain its facing. Preserve the player's position, velocity, HP, resources and action progress. Resume surviving world state without catch-up ticks, replayed hits or duplicate projectiles; ordinary contact and hit-eligibility rules still apply. The boss selects its next legal attack with a **full normal telegraph**, never midway through the canceled attack. The existing transition rest-cooldown bypass grants no ability-cooldown refill. Show actual HP recovery and a distinct once-only rewind cue; do not display a player charge cost.

Serialize competing presentations: first resolve the complete damage event and any player death/recovery outcome, then present a still-valid pending boss transition after the Post-Landing Hold thaws (R03). Do not nest its pause with a player Death Rewind or continue a discarded encounter's event after checkpoint reconstruction, boss defeat or level departure. A live player Death Rewind preserves the boss encounter and its consumed phase flags; it cannot rearm this ability.

### Encounter ownership and verification

Own the history, phase flags, pending Phase 3 crossing and transition event in the current boss encounter. The event holds a unique ID, locked sample/tick, resolved HP, selected/fallback destination, remaining presentation time and applied marker. Re-entering the same live event applies its result once. F10 ordinary loading reconstructs the authored encounter baseline; it does not resume an exact boss snapshot. A newly reconstructed, undefeated boss encounter starts new encounter-local history/phase flags, while persistent reward claims and completed-boss facts remain protected. Never revive a completed boss or reissue a reward by resetting this encounter record.

Runtime and balance validation remain pending: cover each difficulty; exact 180-frame history, short history and ring wrap; above/equal/below-cap healing and lower historical HP; lethal hits and one-hit crossings of both thresholds; healed HP above 66% without phase regression; duplicate callbacks; live player Death Rewind versus reconstructed encounter; blocked historical/current positions, fallback and no valid anchor; active player attacks/Ultimates and boss projectiles at suspension; paused statuses, lifetimes, cooldowns and Rally; no catch-up damage, replayed emission or timer refill; full next-attack telegraph; and ordinary save/load with existing reward claims. Compare burst and sustained-damage characters before confirming the 20% cap.
