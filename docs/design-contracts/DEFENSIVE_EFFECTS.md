# Defensive-effect contract

Design revision: 2026-09-12. D01 is resolved by user-selected **Option B: temporary protection before block**. This contract records design decisions; implementation, migration, runtime and balance validation remain pending. D02a is resolved by user-selected **Option A: refill a repeated HP-shield grant to its normal cap and restart its authored duration, without stacking copies**. D02b is resolved by user-selected **Option A: eligible projectile immunity, then Temporal Aegis, then HP barriers, then ordinary block**. D02c is resolved by user-selected **Option A: Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward last 8 seconds of live gameplay or until depleted, with a valid repeat grant refilling capacity and restarting the timer**. D02d is resolved by user-selected **Option A: Wardenclyffe protects/recharges only in an owned coil radius; 15%-max-HP cap, 3-second delay after shield/HP damage and 2.5%-max-HP recharge per live second**. D02e is resolved by user-selected **Option A: one active Temporal Aegis, no time expiry; a collected duplicate is consumed without another charge or benefit**. D03a is resolved by user-selected **Option A: full HP-barrier absorption prevents that hit's attached hitstun, stagger, knockback and status/mark application**. D03b is resolved by user-selected **Option A: unblockable damaging hits, including Ultimates, bypass ordinary block but remain eligible for HP barriers and Temporal Aegis**. D03c is resolved by user-selected **Option B: ongoing damaging status ticks and external damaging hazards can be absorbed by eligible HP barriers and Temporal Aegis**. D03d is resolved by user-selected **Option A: a legal primary throw bypasses finite HP barriers and Temporal Aegis for damage/launch without consuming them**. D03e is resolved by user-selected **Option A: Aegis prevents damage, hitstun/stagger, knockback and statuses/marks attached to its absorbed hit**. D03f is resolved by user-selected **Option A: use actual HP removed for damage-based meter, echo generation and eligible Rally reclaim, excluding absorbed damage and overkill**. D03g is resolved by user-selected **Option A: direct hits reclaim Rally; persistent zone, construct and attached damage-over-time ticks do not**. D03h is resolved by user-selected **Option A: Ultimate-origin damage earns its caster no damage-dealt meter, including delayed effects**. D04 is resolved by user-selected **Option A: immediate hit protection, release from the triggering hit's stun/capture, then 1 second of hit invulnerability once control returns**. Current defensive decisions are resolved in design; different-HP-shield coexistence remains an unauthored future combination noted below.

## D01 — Temporary protection before block

For an incoming hit, check existing hit eligibility and active invulnerability first. An ineligible or invulnerability-rejected hit spends no temporary protection or block charges. For an otherwise valid hit, resolve **eligible temporary protection before the ordinary charge-based block**. This is shared resolution wherever those effects exist; Story grid perks are not added to Fighter Mode.

Temporary protection includes authored projectile immunity, a one-hit shield such as Temporal Aegis, and finite HP-absorbing barriers such as Wardenclyffe Shield and the shared `ResonanceBarrier` status granted by Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward (A11). Each must satisfy its own active-state and attack-eligibility rules. D01 establishes priority relative to block. D02a below specifies repeated grants of the same HP shield; D02b below orders projectile immunity, Temporal Aegis, HP barriers and block. D02c below sets the three granted HP shields to 8 seconds; D02d specifies Wardenclyffe recharge/range; coexistence between different HP shields remains pending.

- **Fully absorbed attack:** stop before block resolution. Spend only protection actually used. Do not consume a block charge, cause block shieldstun/shatter/daze/lockout, or award a successful-block perk. Holding Block does not turn a shield absorption into a successful block. D03a also prevents the attached hit effects when an eligible damaging hit is fully absorbed by an HP barrier; shield feedback is not the ordinary block response.
- **Partially absorbed attack:** subtract the amount absorbed by the finite barrier and pass only the remaining damage to ordinary block/hit resolution. If the remainder is positive and the block is legal, consume charges using the original attack classification, regardless of how little damage remains. Basic remains one charge, Story Guard-Crush retains its existing two-charge rule, an ordinary player Special consumes two charges, and a Shield-Breaker special still consumes all remaining charges (A01, 2026-09-29). A fully blocked remainder deals no HP damage.
- **No legal block:** rear attacks, airborne/locked/zero-charge states and attacks that bypass block pass the remaining hit to the ordinary damage/response rules. Protection-first priority does not make these blockable.
- **Block-dependent effects:** Shield of Orléans rewards only a real block with charge consumption after protection. Henry's Bastion is created only after that successful block; it cannot absorb the hit that created it. Once present, it takes priority over a later block under this decision. An existing guard that absorbs an entire hit therefore cannot summon or refresh itself through a false block event. Preserve the existing distinct-attack reward/refund accounting.
- **One attack, one resolution:** retain the same source execution/contact identity as damage passes through defenses. Do not create another attack for overflow, replay full original damage at block/HP, or process duplicate collision callbacks twice. Later genuinely distinct multi-hit contacts use then-current protection and block state under their normal hit cadence.

For example, a 30-damage Basic hit meets a barrier with 10 absorption remaining. Consume the 10, then a legal block absorbs the remaining 20 for one charge. The same remainder from an ordinary Special costs two charges; from a Shield-Breaker it still causes the full shatter. If the barrier had 30 remaining, it absorbs the entire hit and block spends nothing.

## D02a — Refresh the same HP shield without stacking

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. When an ability legitimately grants the same finite HP shield again, **set its remaining absorption to that effect's normal current cap** and **restart its authored duration**, if it has one. Keep one active instance per recipient and shield-effect ID; never add capacities, append another copy or add durations together. A full shield may refresh its duration. A broken or expired shield receives a fresh instance on the next legitimate grant.

For example, a 10-HP shield with 4 HP remaining returns to 10 HP on a new grant, not 14 or 20. Refresh grants no actual HP, Rally or damage-based meter. The cap uses the effect's existing max-HP basis and legal modifiers; this decision adds no new shield-strength bonus.

- **Henry's Bastion:** a qualifying real block grants or refreshes its 10%-max-HP guard only after that block resolves. D01 still prevents a fully shield-absorbed hit from triggering it. No same-contact recursion or retroactive absorption.
- **Royal Aegis:** a valid Desert Mirage shield grant refreshes Cleopatra's 10%-max-HP shield. Treat the decoy as a separate recipient under its existing inherited-shield rule, never another stack on Cleopatra or a shared drainable pool.
- **North Star Ward:** an accepted North Star Leap activation grants or refreshes Tubman's 10%-max-HP shield (it replaced Pocahontas's Leaf Barrier in the 2026-09-29 roster swap). A Star Guide mid-flight redirect is not a new activation. Preserve all existing movement eligibility and cooldown limits.
- **Wardenclyffe Shield:** retain its separate 15%-max-HP continuous-recharge design. Being within a coil radius, overlapping two coils or repeatedly entering that radius is not a repeat discrete shield grant and does not instantly refill it under D02a. D02d below specifies its rate, damage delay and range-exit behavior.

Use a unique grant event identity tied to the actual accepted activation or qualifying block contact and recipient. Refused input, polling the active condition, duplicate animation/collision callbacks, recreating a visual, or restoring the same event grants no refill or duration reset. Store/reconstruct instance state and processed grant identity consistently wherever that instance is actually restored. Existing mode restrictions, suppression and Time Freeze action locks still apply.

This is a rule for repeated grants of the **same HP shield**, not an approval to stack different HP shield types or queue multiple Temporal Aegis charges. D02b below resolves mixed protection-type priority, and D02c sets Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward to 8 seconds. D02d below resolves Wardenclyffe recharge/range; D02e specifies one active Aegis with no timer or banked duplicate charges. Time Freeze pauses the refreshed duration; T01a death cleanup and F10 checkpoint reconstruction still remove temporary hero shields without granting them again.

## D02b — Projectile immunity, one-hit shield, HP barrier, block

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. After the existing hit-eligibility and invulnerability checks, resolve each eligible layer in this order:

1. **Projectile immunity:** an active protection that rejects this projectile type stops it before any finite shield or block resource is spent. Respect the source's filter: Rest Shield's physical-projectile coverage is not automatically widened to every ranged attack. Overlapping immunity effects do not create extra absorption charges or duplicate a rejection.
2. **Temporal Aegis:** if the hit remains eligible for Aegis, consume the one-hit protection and negate that hit's damage and knockback under its existing rule. Leave HP-barrier capacity and block charges unchanged. Do not first subtract damage from an HP barrier, even if that barrier could absorb the whole hit.
3. **HP barriers:** if no earlier layer stopped the hit, apply eligible finite HP absorption. Fully absorbed damage never reaches block. Pass only overflow to the next layer under D01; coexistence/order between multiple different HP-barrier types remains separate pending design.
4. **Ordinary block:** apply the existing legal-stance/direction/class checks and block costs to any remaining hit. Subsequent HP damage and response follow their existing rules and the pending D03 clarifications.

For example, with a 10-HP barrier and active Aegis, an eligible 8-damage hit consumes Aegis and leaves the barrier at 10. If active projectile immunity already rejects that same projectile, retain both Aegis and the full barrier. If an earlier layer does not cover the attack, skip it without spending its resource.

Apply this order per distinct eligible hit contact, not once for every entire multi-hit attack execution. Aegis protects its consumed contact; later legitimate contacts use then-current protection and their ordinary cadence. This grants no new protection window against the rest of the attack. Preserve source execution/contact identity so duplicate callbacks cannot consume another layer or replay the stopped hit. Resolve a contact once and stop at the first layer that fully prevents it; do not spend unused layers simultaneously.

This priority applies in both modes wherever the relevant effects exist, including Story orbs with the shared Aegis effect. It grants no Story grid perks to Fighter Mode. Invulnerability, non-hit lethal falls, Siphon Snare's specific rules and authored attack-type eligibility remain as stated above/below; ordering does not establish a new bypass or protection category. D02e below resolves Aegis re-pickup capacity/lifetime; D03a below resolves full HP-barrier absorption of attached hit effects. D03b–D03d resolve the specified attack coverage, and D03e resolves Aegis attached effects; meter/accounting questions remain separate decisions.

## D02c — Eight-second granted HP shields

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. **Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward each last 8 seconds of live gameplay (480 active ticks at 60 Hz), or until their absorption reaches zero, whichever happens first.** Their existing 10%-max-HP capacities and grant conditions remain unchanged.

Start the lifetime when the valid shield grant takes effect. Under D02a, each legitimate repeat grant refills capacity and resets remaining lifetime to 8 seconds; never add 8 seconds to the old remainder. A full shield may refresh its timer. An ordinary absorbed hit reduces capacity but does not refresh or shorten the time remaining beyond normal elapsed time. A broken shield disappears immediately and requires a new valid grant; expiry discards remaining absorption without healing, meter credit, a block event or an offensive shield-break/expiry proc.

Use the same suspended-effect clock as the temporal contract: Time Freeze, menus, world-frozen recovery and boss-rewind presentations do not consume lifetime. Normal travel, attacks and blocking during live play do. Do not substitute wall-clock/offline time or award catch-up ticks. Track authoritative remaining capacity and lifetime together wherever a live instance is actually restored; reconstruction of visuals or a repeated callback cannot reset either. This does not change F10's removal of temporary effects on checkpoint reconstruction or T01a's hero cleanup on Death Rewind.

Royal Aegis on a sand decoy has its own recipient lifetime and can end earlier when that decoy is removed. The shield never extends the decoy's existence, transfers unused capacity/time to Cleopatra or persists as a detached object. Henry's valid block trigger and North Star Ward's accepted-leap trigger retain D02a's deduplication rules. Generic movement/ability-duration upgrades do not extend these shield timers unless a modifier explicitly targets that shield effect; none is granted by this decision.

This eight-second rule does **not** set a lifetime for Wardenclyffe's recharging shield, Mozart's condition-based projectile protection or Temporal Aegis. Keep their source-specific conditions and cleanup rules; D02d resolves Wardenclyffe recharge, and D02e gives Aegis no time expiry.

## A11 — One shared Resonance Barrier status

User selected Option B on 2026-09-29. **Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward are grant sources of one shared status, `ResonanceBarrier`**, not three separate shield effects. The status carries the rules those three already shared: **10%-max-HP absorption**, the D02c **8-second live lifetime**, D02a **refill-not-stack** on a valid repeat grant, and the D01/D02b/D03 resolution order. A recipient holds at most one `ResonanceBarrier` instance; a valid grant from any source refills it to its cap and restarts its 8-second lifetime. Record the granting source on the instance for presentation (phantom guard, sand shield, leaf shield) and for source-specific eligibility, but never as a separate stack.

Source-specific rules stay with their grant, not the status: Henry's Bastion is granted only after a real block and cannot absorb its triggering hit; Royal Aegis also grants a separate instance to the sand decoy, which ends with the decoy; North Star Ward is granted on an accepted North Star Leap activation. Each source still lives on a different hero's Story grid, so a hero never has two sources today; the shared status makes any future overlap a refill, not a stack.

**Wardenclyffe Shield stays a separate effect** (15% max HP, continuous recharge, coil-radius condition — D02d), as do Temporal Aegis (one hit, no expiry — D02e) and authored projectile immunity. The D02b order is unchanged: projectile immunity → Temporal Aegis → HP barriers (`ResonanceBarrier` or Wardenclyffe) → block. Fighter Mode grants none of the Story grid barriers.

## D02d — Wardenclyffe Shield: protection within coil range

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. Retain the purchased Story Major's **15%-max-HP absorption cap**. Both protection and recharge require Tesla to be inside the authored radius of **at least one of his own active Tesla Coils**. Coil ownership and actual radius come from the existing coil data; a linked fence by itself is not an extra shield radius. Another Tesla's coils cannot power this shield. Overlapping owned coils still supply one shield and one recharge rate.

- **Damage delay:** whenever damage actually reduces Tesla's HP or Wardenclyffe absorption, set the recharge delay to **3 live seconds (180 ticks at 60 Hz)**. Count it down in ordinary live play, inside or outside coil range. New qualifying damage restarts it. A hit fully rejected by invulnerability/projectile immunity, absorbed by Temporal Aegis, or blocked without reducing this shield or HP does not restart it. One damage contact reducing both shield and HP still starts one delay.
- **Recharge:** while in range and the delay has elapsed, restore **2.5% of Tesla's current maximum HP per live second**, capped at 15%. Retain fractional progress so tick rounding cannot alter the rate. An empty shield takes **6 seconds of eligible recharge** to fill; after a damaging hit that empties it, uninterrupted in-range recovery takes 3 seconds of delay plus 6 seconds of recharge. Damage resolves before recharge for that update; no refill on the same update as qualifying damage or catch-up credit for time spent outside range.
- **Leaving range:** immediately disable absorption and recharge, but preserve the existing stored charge. Incoming damage outside range uses the remaining defense layers and can restart the HP-damage delay. Returning re-enables only the charge actually retained and permits recharge only if the delay has elapsed. No exit/re-entry refill, charge loss, timer reset or per-coil charge pool.
- **Coil removal:** recompute eligibility when a coil is destroyed, expires, is replaced or explodes during Tesla's Ultimate. If no owned coil still covers Tesla, the shield becomes inactive with stored charge retained. Moving into another owned coil's radius or replacing a coil does not create another grant. Continuous radius membership is a condition, not a repeated D02a refill event.
- **Initialization and cleanup:** initialize charge to zero with no initial damage delay on fresh entry or first legitimate acquisition of the perk; it builds at the normal rate when eligible, never starts full. T01a Death Rewind and F10 checkpoint reconstruction discard stored temporary shield charge. Preserve the remaining damage delay as a player resource timer through same-attempt recovery/load, preventing reload from accelerating recovery; fresh entry/full Restart resets it. Respec removes the shield, and reacquisition cannot clear an existing same-attempt damage delay. Existing purchased-perk/mode restrictions remain.
- **Clock and state:** freeze charge and the remaining delay whenever the existing temporal rules suspend combat-effect/resource timers, including Time Freeze, menus and rewind presentations. No offline progression. Track charge, remaining damage delay and perk eligibility; recompute range from restored coil ownership/positions before absorption. Persist the delay with F10 player resource timers; ordinary loading does not recreate stored charge or live coils. If an actual live instance is restored, restore remaining charge/delay without topping up.
- **Presentation:** show protection as active only while a valid coil powers it; a dormant stored charge outside range must not look like an active protective bubble. At zero charge, no hit is absorbed until real recharge has supplied capacity. No eight-second expiry or natural charge decay is added; this shield uses range, damage delay, depletion and existing cleanup.

The 3-second delay and 2.5%-max-HP rate are design values pending playtest validation. This decision changes no coil damage, lifetime, range or deploy limit, and adds no actual HP healing, Rally or damage-based meter from charging.

## D02e — Temporal Aegis: one hit, no timer

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. **Temporal Aegis has no time expiry and at most one active protection per recipient.** It remains until the next eligible hit consumes it under D02b, or an existing death, reconstruction, recipient-removal or round/scene cleanup rule removes it. No eight-second timer applies.

A successful pickup while unprotected grants one active Aegis. A successful pickup while already protected consumes that pickup but leaves the existing Aegis unchanged: no second charge, reserve, duration, HP, meter or replacement reward. This rule applies to the shared Story/Fighter orb effect. Display one bubble; no stacked pips or timer. Existing pickup eligibility still applies, including Time Freeze's pickup prohibition.

Commit the pickup's stable claim and the resulting active flag together through the existing pickup transaction. Duplicate collision callbacks or loading an already-claimed pickup cannot grant another Aegis. A second genuinely distinct pickup can grant protection after the first Aegis has been consumed, but it must actually be collected then; earlier duplicates never become a banked entitlement.

Represent protection as an active flag tied to its source/instance identity, not a growing charge count. Restore the consumed/active state with contact and pickup accounting whenever an actual instance is restored, without replaying the absorption or pickup benefit. In Story ordinary load, F10 discards the temporary shield but preserves the consumed pickup claim; no uncollected replacement appears. T01a Death Rewind and F22 Sudden Death cleanup still remove it, and normal death/stock/scene cleanup is not bypassed by "no timer."

D02b still checks eligible projectile immunity before Aegis and spends Aegis before HP barriers/block. It prevents one distinct eligible hit contact, not a whole multi-hit execution, with no additional immunity window. Rejected/ineligible hits do not consume it; later contacts follow ordinary hit eligibility and remaining defenses. D03a separately resolves HP-barrier response; D03b–D03d resolve the specified attack coverage and D03e resolves Aegis attached effects.

## Different HP-barrier types: current authoring boundary

The currently authored finite HP barriers belong to separate kits: Tesla's Wardenclyffe, Shakespeare's Henry's Bastion, Cleopatra's Royal Aegis and Tubman's North Star Ward. Repeated same-type grants are covered by D02a, the decoy is a separate recipient, and Aegis is a separate one-hit layer covered by D02b. No current kit specifies a way for one recipient to acquire two different finite HP-barrier types.

Do not infer additive stacking, strongest-only selection or an overflow order for such a future combination. Record any new content that grants multiple different HP-barrier types to one recipient as requiring an explicit coexistence decision before authoring/implementation sign-off. This boundary is not a new shield-sharing mechanic or a claimed resolution of that future design choice.

## D03a — Full HP-barrier absorption prevents attached hit effects

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. When an **eligible damaging hit is completely absorbed by an HP barrier**, prevent the hit's attached hitstun, stagger, knockback/launch and status or mark application, as well as HP damage. This applies to Wardenclyffe Shield, Henry's Bastion, Royal Aegis and North Star Ward under their existing ownership, active-state and capacity rules. D03a determines the response to an absorbed hit, not which attack categories a shield covers.

Resolve absorption before applying victim hit reactions or attached statuses. A hit exactly exhausting the remaining shield is still fully absorbed: remove the depleted shield, but do not apply that hit's effects to the unprotected actor afterward. A later distinct contact can hit normally; no extra invulnerability window is granted. Do not queue rejected hit reactions/statuses for when the shield expires or breaks.

If damage exceeds barrier capacity, pass only the remainder to ordinary block/HP resolution under D01. A legal block of the remainder retains its existing block response. If the remainder reaches HP, apply normal hit-response and status eligibility, including existing hyper-armor, immunities and lethal/Defy ordering. Partial absorption alone does not proportionally reduce a status duration or grant an additional hit-response immunity. Hyper-armor keeps its existing role on an unabsorbed hit; this choice neither turns it into damage reduction nor removes its authored bypasses.

Examples for a 20-damage poisoned hit: a 30-HP barrier ends at 10, with no HP loss or new poison/stun/knockback; a 20-HP barrier breaks but still prevents that contact's effects; a 10-HP barrier passes 10 damage onward, with normal effects if it reaches HP. D02d still starts Tesla's recharge delay when his shield actually loses capacity, even though that hit caused no HP loss or attached effect.

This does not cleanse or pause an existing status, break an unrelated tether, erase a zone, or suppress an independent environmental effect. Existing poison continues its ordinary tick schedule. D03c makes subsequent damaging DoT ticks eligible for finite-shield absorption; absorbing a tick prevents only its damage and does not remove or refresh the status. Grabs, Siphon Snare, non-hit kill boundaries and other standalone control effects retain their separate rules. Decompose an attack's authored attached effects and independent effects explicitly rather than applying a status merely because a collision callback fired.

Preserve one source execution/contact identity through absorption and response resolution. Duplicate contacts cannot consume another shield, apply a rejected status or create another effect event. Emit appropriate shield-impact/break feedback without a victim hurt/launch reaction for a fully absorbed hit. D03f defines the actual-HP-loss basis for Rally/meter and excludes overkill; D03e resolves Temporal Aegis attached effects, and D03a grants no new shield-damage rewards.

## D03b — Unblockable bypasses ordinary block, not finite shields

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. The **Unblockable** classification on a damaging attack, including player Ultimates and authored boss unblockables, means it bypasses the ordinary charge-based block. It does **not** by itself bypass HP barriers or Temporal Aegis. Preserve existing hit eligibility, invulnerability and source-specific projectile-immunity filters, then apply D02b's protection order.

An eligible Temporal Aegis consumes itself to stop one contact while leaving barrier HP and block charges intact. Without Aegis, an active eligible HP barrier absorbs normally. If absorption is complete, D03a prevents that HP-barrier-absorbed contact's attached hit effects, including at exact depletion. If damage remains, skip ordinary block and resolve only the remainder against HP and normal hit-response defenses. Do not spend block charges, enter block shieldstun/shatter, or grant a successful-block perk for an unblockable contact, whether fully absorbed or not.

For example, an unblockable 20-damage hit against a 10-HP barrier consumes the barrier and passes 10 damage to HP even if Block is held. With eligible Aegis active instead, that one contact is absorbed. A later distinct contact of a multi-hit Ultimate may consume barrier HP or damage the actor; the Ultimate's entire execution is not canceled, refunded or rendered harmless by the first absorption.

The existing **player Ultimate bypass of enemy damage-reduction defenses** remains a separate authored rule: Tech Enforcer's reduction bubble, Shock-Shield Legionnaire's frontal reduction and equivalent `FrontalDamageReduction` fields do not reduce player Ultimate damage. These reduction defenses are not the finite HP-absorption pools or Aegis resolved here. Keep the defense types explicit; a shared cosmetic word such as "shield" must not silently make one inherit another's bypass policy.

Likewise, keep the existing Ultimate bypass of hyper-armor for a hit that reaches normal hit-response resolution. A fully HP-barrier-absorbed contact does not reach that response under D03a. This decision does not change invulnerability, grab/throw capture rules, source-specific projectile immunity, non-hit lethal falls, meter drains or D03e's Aegis attached-effect rule. No general finite-shield-piercing flag or new attack category is added.

Retain the red/X Unblockable telegraph as the promise that ordinary block will fail. Explain finite-shield protection in the defense reference; do not relabel the attack Basic or Guard-Crush because a temporary shield absorbed it. The attack keeps its original class/identity through all layers and later contacts. No enemy ability placement, attack damage, timing or cooldown changes are part of this decision.

## D03c — Shields absorb ongoing DoT and external hazard damage

User selected Option B on 2026-09-12. **Actual HP-damaging ticks from an already-applied status, such as Venom, can be absorbed by Temporal Aegis and active HP barriers.** Ordinary external damaging hazards, such as spike impacts, lava contact and stage-hazard ticks, can also consume eligible shields. Preserve the existing hit/damage eligibility and invulnerability checks before finite protection.

- **Tick resolution:** route each scheduled damage occurrence through the shared damage resolver once, with its source/status instance ID and tick index. After any eligible projectile immunity, Aegis consumes itself to prevent that single tick; otherwise an eligible HP barrier absorbs up to its remaining capacity. Pass only unabsorbed damage onward. A status tick is not a projectile, so projectile-only immunity does not reject it.
- **Status lifetime:** an absorbed tick still counts as that scheduled occurrence. The source status remains active on its original duration and cadence; no cleansing, duration refresh, delayed damage debt or immediate retry tick. Later scheduled ticks may consume the remaining barrier or reach HP. D03a can prevent the original damaging hit's poison application, but a shield gained after application handles future ticks rather than removing the poison.
- **Ordinary block:** finite-shield coverage does not create a front-facing block opportunity against an already-attached DoT. Unabsorbed status-tick damage reaches HP under the normal damage/Defy resolver. External stage-hazard ticks retain their authored Basic-class block rule: after finite absorption, a legal block of remaining damage consumes one charge. Preserve explicit source-specific hazard block classifications elsewhere; do not reclassify a boss attack because its visual resembles a hazard.
- **Hit effects and timing:** DoT/hazard/zone ticks keep zero hitstop; absorbing one does not create an impact freeze. Preserve the source's authored reaction rules rather than inventing stun or knockback for poison. A fully HP-barrier-absorbed hazard hit loses its attached impulse/status application under D03a; Aegis prevents that tick/contact's damage and attached hit effects under D03e. Finite absorption does not turn off the hazard or grant immunity to the next tick.
- **Boundary cases:** zero-damage warnings, non-damaging statuses and harmless overlaps consume no Aegis or barrier HP. Radiant Burn remains a vulnerability modifier with no damage ticks; do not create a DoT because it occupies the damage slot. Bottomless-pit kill boundaries, Integrity loss, Siphon Snare and other non-HP meter drains retain their separate rules and do not spend finite shields to prevent them.
- **Recovery and replay:** Time Freeze and suspended presentations pause these scheduled effects under the temporal contract, with no thaw catch-up ticks. T01a/F10 cleanup remains unchanged. If an actual live instance is restored, restore remaining shields and the status/hazard tick schedule/processed occurrence together; duplicate callbacks cannot spend another layer, repay prevented damage or restart status duration.
- **Tesla:** a tick that reduces Wardenclyffe charge or Tesla HP restarts the D02d three-second recharge delay. A tick consumed entirely by earlier Aegis protection does not. Recharge grants no offsetting heal or refund for poison damage.

For a 2-damage poison tick with Aegis and 10 barrier HP, consume Aegis and retain the 10 barrier HP; the next normally scheduled tick can reduce that barrier to 8. With only 1 barrier HP, consume it and pass 1 damage to HP, without removing the poison.

D03f defines damage credit using actual HP removed. D03g resolves Rally source eligibility and D03h excludes Ultimate-origin damage from caster meter gain. This choice authorizes absorption, not shield-damage meter farming or a new reclaim source.

## D03d — Primary throws bypass finite shields

User selected Option A on 2026-09-12. **A legal grab is not prevented by temporary HP barriers or Temporal Aegis. Its resulting primary throw deals normal damage and applies its normal launch without consuming those protections.** This is an explicit coverage exception to the finite-shield order for ordinary damaging hits. Apply it to Forward, Up and Back throws in every mode where those throws are already legal; it adds no new enemy grab behavior.

Preserve existing capture eligibility: grounded grabber, range/startup/active requirements and victim exclusions for hitstun, daze, shieldstun, airborne/rolling/invulnerable states and grab-immune enemies. A finite shield is not invulnerability and does not itself make a target grab-immune. The grabber's ordinary block stance remains disabled throughout the grab; that restriction does not erase a temporary shield.

At the paired throw's scheduled damage/launch event, skip Temporal Aegis and HP-absorption pools as well as ordinary block. Do not decrement charge/capacity, trigger an absorption or shield break, grant a block perk, or treat skipped protection as partial damage reduction. Retain 1.0× BasicAttackDamage, authored directional impulses, weight and low-HP scaling, no DI, no added statuses and the existing hold/animation/throw-immunity timings. The normal paired throw still delivers its own scheduled hit during its protected 12-frame animation; that animation's protection prevents unrelated interference rather than canceling the paired hit.

Resolve normal HP damage, eligible Defy, death and existing throw/Rally/meter accounting once. A legal lethal throw can still be defied under the normal rule; finite-shield bypass does not bypass Defy. If the victim survives, its unspent temporary protection remains with its ordinarily elapsed lifetime and active-state conditions. Do not refill or refresh it, pause its timer merely because the actor is grabbed, or restore it after a death cleanup. Throw HP damage restarts Tesla's D02d damage-recharge delay even though Wardenclyffe capacity was not spent. T01a and mode-specific KO cleanup still remove effects when appropriate.

Mark the resolved contact as the primary hit of a validated paired grab/throw event, with paired actor IDs and the existing damage-applied guard. Do not infer this bypass from a projectile's visual, from a broad Unblockable flag, or from all hits sharing the attacker's execution. Story's secondary crowd-bowling collision from a thrown mob remains its separately authored projectile hit and uses normal eligible defenses; it does not inherit primary-throw bypass.

For a 15-damage throw against 20 barrier HP, the surviving victim loses 15 actual HP and retains 20 barrier HP, subject to ordinary lifetime/range rules. With Aegis instead, the throw deals normal damage/launch and leaves the unspent bubble available for a later eligible hit. No stored shield is converted to health or compensation.
