## Column requirements

- **Movement gate:** exactly one required traversal gate that the hero's *baseline* movement ability clears. It must never require a purchased grid node (Wings Refresh, Rail Breaker, Vortex Step and similar are Story purchases), and must not depend on meter.
- **S1 / S2 puzzles:** one required puzzle per Special. Declare the accepted puzzle props and actions under V01c (designated props, plate weights, beam networks). Zero-damage or non-projectile Specials need a non-damage target: for example, The Tempest acts on a movable or wind-driven prop, and Divine Piercing's in-place thrust needs a target placed within its reach. Constructs may operate only mechanisms that explicitly accept them.
- **Ultimate set-piece:** the designated target resolved through the Nexus Resonance Source (F04) at any meter value, reward-free, retryable, and disabled once solved.
- **Boss:** either a figure from the hero's own history bent by the Unbound, or an Unbound Overseer wielding a stolen artefact of the hero's era. Two phases under the standard boss rules and the "never just a speed multiplier" phase rule; `phaseTrigger` per M19. State an HP band relative to the Level 4 and Level 5 bosses, before difficulty scaling.
- **Mob roster:** reuse the shared-era roster where the home era is a shared level. For Princeton, Vienna and Tidewater, name the era roster to author or an approved reuse. The single scripted Eraser encounter (F12) is additional.
- **Status values:** *To author* → *Drafted* → *Approved* (user decision recorded with date) → *Prototyped* → *Validated*. Only *Approved* rows are design of record.

## Acceptance rules for every row

1. The route order follows F12: Entry → kit gates and the independent Eraser encounter → late Font approach → PreBoss → boss. Exactly two checkpoints.
2. Each of the four kit requirements is used once and is completable with zero meter and any legal grid allocation. Spending meter or recoveries before a gate cannot soft-lock it (F04, V01).
3. The nexus outcome, wound and siphon mapping are distinct from the shared level's sealed outcome (N02). The larger personal homecoming lives here, not in the shared level (N03).
4. Economy uses the fixed 4A ledger row: 15 required encounter dust, 25 boss dust, 10 optional dust (F05). Integrity par follows V01a with its own benchmark per variant.
5. Production records its reuse and new work in the P01 ledger before an estimate is accepted.

## Flags raised by the backfill (not resolved here)

Reported only; none of these was fixed, and none changes a row's status.

1. **Joan's setting may conflict with N02.** The shipped Joan 4A is set at **Orléans' fortifications**: the 1429 assault on Les Tourelles, the fortified bridgehead, across its boulevard and curtain wall (`Level04AJoanController`, `legacy_joan_room_boulevard` "The Tourelles Boulevard", boss "The Tourelles Overseer"). The design places Joan's nexus on "the vanguard field, not Orléans' battlements". [N02](NARRATIVE_RESOLUTION.md#n02--sealed-moments-stay-sealed) also says that "a different room alone is not the explanation" and that "a location change alone does not define the separation". The variant shares Level 2's city, year, episode, palette and boss kit (Siegemaster Duke). Its dialogue gives no separate wound ID and no distinct threatened outcome beyond "the morning can arrive the way it was promised". Level 2's Joan branch already has the banner restored to the vanguard (`dlg_l02_exit_3_joan`), so the two missions may currently resolve overlapping moments. This needs a setting and wound ruling before approval.
2. **Princeton, Vienna and Tidewater ship generic rosters.** Einstein, Mozart and Pocahontas each ship 4× `chrono_slasher` + 2× `hologram_drone`, the era-agnostic Future Cultist mobs. They use no era roster and no recorded approved reuse. The column rule requires naming an era roster to author or an approved reuse. Joan, Leonardo and Lincoln also ship this generic pair instead of their shared-era rosters (Levels 2, 1 and 11). That is a second breach of "reuse the shared-era roster". Only Tesla (`voltaic_shock_drone`), Shakespeare (`holo_page`) and Cleopatra (`plasma_spear_ward`) reuse their era's ranged standard. Einstein's code comment names `tech_enforcer_drone`, but the code spawns `hologram_drone`.
3. **Boss HP is a flat 700 for all nine, with no band.** Level 4's boss (`revolutionary_tribunal`, per `Level04Controller`) is **850**. Level 5's boss (`tidal_eraser`) is **640**. The shipped 700 sits between them, and the Act I curve currently descends from Level 4 to Level 5. The contract requires an HP band relative to those two bosses. All boss-HP rows are **held under D13/D10** until the user rules, alongside the open `VERIFY-BOSS-HP` ledger entry. No value was changed.
4. **Phase rule.** Tesla's and Shakespeare's legacy bosses unlock no ability in phase 2 (`AbilityMinPhase` all 0). Their second phase differs only by a speed multiplier (×1.2 / ×1.3), which conflicts with "never just a speed multiplier". No resource carries an M19 `phaseTrigger`. Every phase is a plain 50 % HP threshold.
5. **Boss origin is undeclared.** No variant authors either permitted origin: a bent historical figure, or an Overseer with a *named* stolen artefact of the era. Each boss is a renamed duplicate of an existing era boss's kit and sprite. Mozart's Impresario and Shakespeare's Understudy King share the same Tragedy King kit and sprite. Pocahontas's Tidewater Reaver reuses the Level 5 boss kit, which the route puts immediately after 4A.
6. **N02 wound mapping is missing everywhere.** No variant declares a stable wound ID, local anchor or linked siphons. Leonardo (Florence 1503 = Level 1), Tesla (Chicago 1893 = Level 3), Cleopatra (Alexandria 30 BC = Level 8), Shakespeare (London 1599 = Level 10) and Lincoln (Gettysburg 1863 = Level 11) share city and year with their shared level. They also reuse that level's boss kit (and, for Tesla/Shakespeare/Cleopatra, its ranged mob). Acceptance rule 3 is therefore unverified for all six shared-era heroes. The suggested briefing line ("The victory you restored still holds…") appears in no 4A dialogue.
7. **Stand-in gate resolvers — retired by Package 12 W8.** The five S1/S2 gates that used variant-local stand-ins now use declared gate configuration: `LegacyGateMode.CastWatch` (appended, = 4) for the Tesla Coil, Serpent Nest and The Tempest gates; a `Zone` gate with `ZoneAcceptsOwnedConstruct` for Vine Snare (the hero's own live snare from its authored scene); and `Strike` gates whose surface also sits on `EnemyHurtbox` for Divine Piercing and Clockwork Turret. `LegacyCastGateWatcher`, `VineSnareGateResolver` and `LegacyResonantEffigy` are deleted. Each gate still requires only its canonical kit slot and refuses during Time Freeze and the Post-Landing Hold. The V01c prop/action declaration should still be confirmed before approval, and nobody has played any variant to verify it.
8. **Optional dust is unreachable.** No 4A variant builds a `SecretCache` or an Extractor, so the 10 optional dust cannot be earned (`VERIFY-SECRET-CACHE`). This conflicts with acceptance rule 4.
9. **Retired vocabulary in comments.** Several controller doc comments still say "the Archive" (for example "the Archive's containment lens", "the Archive's Princeton detail"). This is comments only; the shipped `en.csv` strings use Unbound/Overseer vocabulary.
10. **Kit names.** The build names Einstein's S1 "Mass-Energy Conversion" (the table said "E=mc²") and Tesla's S1 "Tesla Coil" (the table said "Tesla Coil (Chain Lightning)"). The table now shows the build names, keeping the old label in parentheses for Einstein.
