# Implementation status

Last audited: 2026-08-08 against `design-godot.md` and `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

This is the concise execution ledger for the gap-closure roadmap. `AGENTS.md` is the durable architecture/context file, while `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` remains the ordered delivery plan.

Package 6 (ten-stage local Fighter Mode completion) closed on 2026-08-08. The CPU is complete at all three bands (off-stage recovery, orb pursuit, per-band hazard avoidance, pulsed rather than latched buttons). The rollback-readiness gate — Package 7's entry criterion — **passes**: 165 delayed-input convergence runs across all eleven authored geometries, and a worst case of 0.058 ms median / 0.104 ms p95 depth-7 resimulation against an 8 ms budget with a largest snapshot of 2,699 bytes and zero breaches over 3,000 corrections (`docs/PERFORMANCE_BASELINE.md` carries the per-stage table). 907 tests pass. Production art, music, VFX, cinematic KO presentation, and the `ParallaxBackground` -> `Parallax2D` migration are deferred to Package 8 by design; a human playthrough of all ten stages has not been performed.

Package 8 (product-wide UI, audio, visuals, controls, localization) closed on 2026-08-08, run as four parallel framework agents, seven parallel application agents, and a serial closeout against `docs/PACKAGE8_PRESENTATION_PLAN.md`. Package 7 (online) was deferred by user decision beforehand, so the network select / online pause / network error screens, the Chronal Jitter warning and netplay reverb tuning were explicitly out of scope and those three manifest rows deliberately stay `Planned`. Localization is now gated in both directions by three permanent tools. **Everything Package 8 shipped is a framework plus placeholder content — production art, music and VFX assets are Package 10 — and no human has visually judged any of it.** 1293 tests pass.

## Implemented systems

- The deterministic Fighter authority now includes eight-frame run acceleration, phased evasive roll (the universal dash was removed 2026-08-09), fixed-point pushbox jostling, attacks, block, meter, statuses, cooldowns, match modes, projectiles, hazards, Chronal Orbs, persistent constructs, bottom-zone KOs, snapshots, hashes, prediction, corrected-input rollback, and resimulation.
- Story combatants use lower-torso soft pushboxes rather than solid hurtboxes. Normal movement body-blocks; roll travel crosses ordinary enemies, while opt-in immovable bosses block it.
- The Test Arena uses `FighterSimulationDriver`; Godot player nodes and 112 prewarmed entity proxies are presentation only. Story-only Resonance values are excluded at the Fighter loadout boundary.
- Local Fighter setup supports a human or deterministic CPU opponent, two character choices, CPU Easy/Normal/Hard reaction bands, Stock/Time/Hybrid rules, stocks, timer, items, and hazards. Match completion supports results, rematch, change fighters, menu return, and global/character statistics.
- Story rewind records 300 frames at 60 Hz, searches backward for a safe grounded state, retains a grounded fallback, replays backward at 4x, uses the physical checkpoint as a final fallback, and applies the Easy/Normal/Hard restore/refill contracts.
- Rewind now freezes enemies and persistent simulation participants, clears enemy projectiles, restores/reset constructs according to explicit policies, grants 120 invulnerable frames, and emits typed visual/audio presentation phases. Timeline Collapse applies the 20 percent Dust penalty and offers anchor/full-level restart choices in the Hub.
- The complete Section 6 roster is authored: 27 `EnemyData` (era standards and elites, plus the cultist mobs) and 15 `BossData` resources with 76 `EnemyAbilityData` kits across eight archetypes. `EnemyController` runs patrol/chase/telegraph/attack/stunned/dead with seeded difficulty-scaled reaction delays, flying and wall-phasing behavior, frontal shields, item-drop multipliers, a death window, and checkpoint rewind. `BossController` executes its selected ability with `RangeClass` distance filtering, a no-deadlock fallback, phase gating and invincibility, and interruption rules; `BossEncounterController` owns spawn-on-trigger, HUD bar, defeat/dust, and rewind policy, and drives Florence's boss. Mirror Paradox reuses the real Hard Fighter CPU decision engine through a Story-side observation adapter without adding Godot dependencies to `scripts/FighterSim/`.
- Enemy warm-up is data driven per level: `resources/Pools/level_pool_configs/level_02..15_pool_config.tres` warm `standard_enemy`, `elite_enemy`, `enemy_projectile`, dust, items, VFX, damage numbers, and constructs inside validated budgets. All fourteen are registered in `scene_pool_catalog.tres` as of the Package 5 closeout. The Package 6 closeout added ten `resources/Pools/fighter_stage_configs/fighter_stage_*_pool_config.tres` entries mirroring the Test Arena budgets — Florence had warmed no pools at all — and `ScenePoolConfigTests` asserts every campaign scene *and* every Fighter stage scene resolves to its own `ConfigID`, which matters because eight era names are shared between a campaign level and a Fighter stage.
- Story projectiles/zones, current enemy instances, placeholder VFX, persistent constructs, Chronal Dust, and helper items use stable-ID pools with configured Grow/Recycle/Reject policies. Difficulty-driven drops implement 50/25/10 healing and +50%/15s, +25%/10s, or no buff drops.
- The reusable Story toolkit now includes stable puzzle state, levers/rotating platforms, weights/plates/counterweights, power routing, extractors, destructibles/chests, room transitions, crumble/cyclic hazards, and Chronal Rifts. Florence contains representative placeholder instances.
- Story saves use schema version 3, AES-256-CBC encryption, encrypt-then-HMAC-SHA-256, independent derived keys, a random per-install key file, atomic verified writes, backup recovery, corruption preservation, legacy migration, puzzle completion persistence, and future-schema rejection.
- Story slot flow supports three slots, locked-character creation, difficulty selection, resume, autosave at checkpoints/level completion, and restoration of Florence position, HP, Influence, dust, and rewinds.
- All nine characters have complete baseline 3x3 Resonance data resources with prerequisites/costs plus repository deposit/unlock UI. Generic resolved stats and character-scoped modifier queries apply only when Story characters are built; character-specific perk behavior remains to be connected to bespoke abilities.
- Fighter stage select loads a localized ten-stage catalog. Florence Workshop now has its own authored scene (`scenes/fighter/FighterStage_Florence.tscn`), a `FighterStageGeometry` fixed-point definition (unique walls, two one-way gear platforms, authored hazard/orb anchors), and a `FighterStageController` that spawns fighters at authored markers with camera midpoint/zoom. The other nine entries still use the shared Test Arena scene with distinct presentation colors and deterministic hazard identities.
- Campaign scenes share `StorySceneBootstrapper`: a resource-driven `DialogueManager` (typewriter, confirm/advance, portraits, emotion tags, gameplay suspension, `DialogueSequenceData`/`DialogueSetData` resources), a reusable `StoryHUD` (HP/meter/rewind/dust, objective, boss bar, checkpoint toasts), `LevelResultsPanel`, a pause menu, and the rewind manager/presentation overlay.
- The Tutorial implements all three parts: fracture presentation, calibration (movement, combat on the training dummy, special-cooldown and rewind steps), advanced mobility gates (double jump, roll — the dash gate left with the universal dash, 2026-08-09), and a two-wave combat trial with checkpoints.
- Florence gates its four-room progression with a linked `RotatingGear` train puzzle, trigger-spawned enemy waves with difficulty scaling, a boss encounter with HUD bar and intro/exit dialogue, dust accumulation, results overlay, autosave, and hub return.
- The hub restores the locked campaign character, auto-deposits carried dust with a toast, and offers the Calibration Bay return anchor, a Holodeck human-vs-CPU practice loop that returns to the hub, and Sarah briefing dialogue.
- Campaign levels 2-15 are authored on `StoryLevelControllerBase`: per-level room graphs with `RoomTransitionTrigger`/`StoryCameraConfiner` bounds (re-applied on a checkpoint resume), three checkpoints each, wave/proximity encounters at the locked S/E/B/X counts, authored Chronal Extractors, a boss arena wired through `BossEncounterController` and sized against the authored boss resource's ranged band, entrance/boss/exit dialogue resources, and the completion/auto-deposit/sequential-unlock tail. Fourteen era-mechanic components back the per-level identities: searchlight zones, rising water, gravity fields (static and cycling), pendulum anchors, path-moving platforms, trapdoors, escape sequences, movement dampeners, sequence locks, rescuable NPCs, and shield-generator towers with forcefields. Level 13 runs the CPU-driven Mirror Paradox instead of a scripted boss; Level 15 ends the campaign through the Temporal Core insertion, ending dialogue, credits, the `IsCompleted` save write, and the Main Menu, with the hub portal standing down afterwards.
- `StoryDifficultyTuning` centralizes Easy/Normal/Hard multipliers for enemy HP/damage applied at spawn in Story controllers only; Fighter loadouts never read them.
- LAN groundwork uses exact binary input packets and UDP, while a deterministic in-memory transport exercises latency and rollback convergence. Steam/public matchmaking deliberately reports unavailable until its SDK is integrated.
- Settings persist audio buses, display mode/resolution/VSync, complete InputMap binding overrides, haptics, damage numbers, HUD opacity, and screen-shake scale, and apply them at boot. SFX playback uses 24 prewarmed reusable players with oldest-steal.
- Florence now includes an original generated parallax background asset at `assets/environments/florence/florence_far_background.png` while most character/environment presentation remains prototype art.
- Every production UI surface is an authored scene against one shared `Theme` (`resources/UI/ftt_theme.tres`) and one `UIPalette`, with authored focus order (`FocusChainBuilder`, including `SpinBox` internal editors), a shared `ConfirmModal` that replaced three ad-hoc patterns and both native `ConfirmationDialog` windows, a `PauseMenuBase` that owns `SceneTree.Paused` for both Story and Fighter and hands it back in `_ExitTree`, and per-transition themed loading screens chosen from the destination path.
- `AudioManager` owns an authored bus layout (Master → Music, SFX → Combat/Movement/Environmental, UI), a `StemDirector` that plays a `StageAudioSet`'s three stems synchronized and additively with authored crossfades, and an `AudioSnapshotMixer` layering pause/low-health/ultimate/rewind volume offsets and low-pass cutoffs (cutoffs stack by minimum, not sum). All 27 sets are authored; `StoryAudioDirector` drives Story intensity from engaged enemies and boss reveals, and `FighterAudioRules` drives the Fighter last-stock climax.
- `GlowPresentationController` is the single owner of a combatant sprite's material and tint state, arbitrating hyper-armor/spawn-invulnerability > status > slot colour over the shared `outline_glow.gdshader`, with base tint, tint override and outline stack as three independent channels. `VfxEmitter` + `RosterVfxMap` + `VfxLibrary` resolve all 36 ability cast/impact assignments and all 78 roster presentation events onto six reusable family scenes within a shared 500-particle budget, and `PresentationVisibilitySuspender` suspends off-screen presentation nodes.
- Localization is validated in both directions against the **compiled** `en.en.translation`: `ScriptTranslationKeyTests` (every `Tr("...")`/`TranslationServer.Translate("...")` literal in `scripts/`), `SceneVisibleTextTests` (every visible `text` in an authored `.tscn` is a real key, an empty runtime-filled string, or a letter-free glyph), and `UnusedTranslationKeyTests` (fails on a new orphan; twelve pre-Package-8 orphans recorded).

5. **Two known content gaps.** Florence's three budgeted Chronal Extractors are unauthored (`docs/DUST_ECONOMY.md` §6), because Package 5 deliberately did not retrofit the pre-existing Tutorial/Florence controllers; and `scenes/templates/ChronalOrbTemplate.tscn` is authored and tested but wired into no pool config, awaiting whoever adds Story orb drops.
6. **Release.** Export presets, Windows/macOS/Linux/SteamOS/Steam Deck validation, and release documentation.

Two exit criteria stand outside every automated gate and remain outstanding: a human end-to-end playthrough of levels 0-15 at each difficulty and of all ten Fighter stages, and a human **visual** review of the Package 8 presentation layer — the shader's rendered output, the parallax at the camera's 1.4× zoom, each era's lighting tone, and the dialogue glass panel have never been looked at.

## Scope warning

The architecture and functional foundations are substantially farther along, but the initial-release game is not content-complete or production-ready. Do not describe LAN as production online play, authored Resonance data as complete bespoke perk behavior, or the prototype Florence/shared-template Fighter presentation as final assets.
