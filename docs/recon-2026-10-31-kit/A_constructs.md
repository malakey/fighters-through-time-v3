## 2. Where "the hero's hit landed on target X" is known

### Story
- Basic hits and every Hitbox-delivered Special/projectile: `Hitbox.OnAreaEntered`, `scripts/Combat/Hitbox.cs:285-312`. After `hurtbox.TakeDamage` it has `payload.ContactLanded = hurtbox.DidLand(payload.ContactId)` (`:285`), the struck `hurtbox`, `Delivery`, `HitboxID`, `SourcePlayer`. **Recommended hook**: a new `SourcePlayer?.NotifyConstructStrikes(payload, hurtbox)` right after `:285`, next to `NotifyStoryHitLanded` (`:300`). It is the only place with the target hurtbox, the D1a landed stamp (includes full shield absorption) and the delivery together.
- Shape-query / ground-wave Specials never pass a Hitbox: `BaseSpecial.Credit(Hurtbox struck, ...)`, `scripts/Combat/BaseSpecial.cs:524-529` stamps `landed.ContactLanded` from `struck.DidLand` (`:526`). Add the same call there (second hook). `ConfirmAbilityHit` (`:369`) has no hurtbox, so it is not the place. `PlayerController.OnMeleeHitConfirmed` (`PlayerController.cs:1731-1749`) knows `ContactLanded` and basic-vs-not but has no target.
- Filter: `payload.Delivery == HitDelivery.DirectHit`, `ContactLanded`, `AttackerIndex` is the owner. Construct hits already stamp `Delivery = Construct` (nodes use `WithAbilityContract(..., HitDelivery.Construct)`; the turret's pooled bolt via `ConfigureConstructHit`, `Hitbox.cs:~205`); zone ticks are `Tick`. So construct strikes and ticks cannot loop. Hand-exclude Tesla's Lorentz Pulse and its chain arcs: they are `DirectHit` (`TeslaAbilities.cs:~260-320`, HitboxIDs "pulse", "chain_lightning").
- Time Freeze / world hold: `Hitbox.IsDiscardedByTimeFreeze` / `IsDiscardedByWorldHold` return before this point; the nodes also stop on `_rewindFrozen`.
- Each landed string hit is its own Hitbox contact, so "up to three charges per string" falls out. A cleave through two mobs triggers per body; decision 18 should say whether that is intended.

### Fighter sim
- Every hit resolves through `FighterDamageRules.ResolveFighterHit` (`FighterEntitySystems.cs:537`, returns `Landed` at about `:871`) which has **no Frame**, only component refs. The "hero hit" sites:
  - basic string, up-attack, down-air, air strike, ledge attack: `ApplyBasicSwing` -> `OnSwingResolved` (HEAD `FighterSimulationSystems.cs:2462`, callers `:2331/2345/2359/2441`). It already tests D1a (Landed or Absorbed, damage > 0) and pays Momentum once per swing. One resolved swing = one landed hit: this is the basic hook.
  - melee Special intents: `ApplyIntent` (HEAD `:3140-3200`) -> `FighterSpecialCancelRules.NoteLanded` (`FighterSpecialCancel.cs:124-152`).
  - Special projectiles `FighterEntitySystems.cs:2176-2210` (NoteLanded `:2206`); Special zone pulses (Golden Ratio, Lorentz Pulse, Requiem) `:3320-3350`; reach-kit thrusts `:1488-1507`, `FighterKitRules.cs:176-193`; Lorentz chain arcs `FighterKitRules.cs:584-595`.
  - `NoteLanded` is already the single funnel for "a DirectHit-delivery Special landed" (it requires `contract.Delivery == DirectHit`, so Tick zones and constructs never pass), so one new call beside it covers Special direct hits and `OnSwingResolved` covers basics. Construct hits (`ApplyFighterHit` `:2295`, fence `:2373`, construct bolts) pass neither, so no loop by construction. Rift/Vortex are Tick contracts (no trigger, consistent with decision 18).
- System order (`FighterSimulation.cs:62-71`): Movement, AbilityEntity (Update); Pushbox, **Combat**, **Projectile**, **PersistentObject**, **Zone**, Hazard, Orb (PostUpdate). Basic, melee-Special and projectile hits resolve before `FighterPersistentObjectSystem`, so a same-tick synced strike works. Zone-pulse hits (Golden Ratio, Lorentz) resolve after it, so they are seen next tick. Recommendation: hit sites only record an owed-strike count on the attacker (no Frame there); `FighterPersistentObjectSystem.Update` consumes it at its top and always clears it so nothing goes stale. Evaluate reach at consumption: positions do not move on the hit tick (launch velocity is applied by the movement system next tick and DI-resolved after hitstop).
- Where to store the owed count: `FighterVerbComponent` (310) `SpareInt1` is free at HEAD (`FighterSimulationComponents.cs:760`); the other session consumes `SpareInt0` (WIP `GrabConnectFrame`), so do not take it. Or 323 (44 B, pinned by `FighterActionStateTests.cs:21`; its assertion must change if grown). 1v1, so a per-owner count suffices and the target is always the opponent.
- **Hitstun conflict**: `ResolveFighterHit` sets `target.HitstunFrames = hitstunFrames` (replace, not max) and a stunning non-launching hit forces `targetVerb.Tumble = None` (CM-FIXB-SIM B1). A synced strike after the hero's finisher would cancel the launch's tumble/DI and could shorten hitstun. Use max(existing, strike) and consider skipping the tumble reset for synced strikes. The target also already carries the hero hit's hitstop.

## 5. Risks

1. **Hash / rollback.** A new 303 field or 310/323 state moves every Fighter hash. Consume in `FighterPersistentObjectSystem` (after Combat and Projectile). Use FP64 and the deterministic filter order (oldest-ID pattern at `FighterEntitySystems.cs:1583`). Strike presentation must key on a counter edge or a rollback correction replays it.
2. **Hitstun / launch / block.** See section 2 (replace-not-max, tumble reset, existing hitstop). Decide whether a strike is blockable and Foresight-catchable (`FighterForesightRules.TryCounter`); a blocked or refused hero hit is not Landed, so it does not trigger (recommended, per D1a).
3. **Loops.** Safe if triggers are only `OnSwingResolved` / `NoteLanded` (sim) and `DirectHit` + `ContactLanded` (Story). Traps: Story's Lorentz Pulse and chain arcs are `DirectHit`; the turret's pooled bolt must stay `Construct`; Cataclysm detonation should not spend charges.
4. **Ability-damage composition.** Nodes already use `StoryAbilityDamage` / `StoryAbilityDamageScale` / `StoryAbilityHitboxDamage`. `KitAlignmentContractTests` (`tests/ContentValidation/`, regexes `:62`, `:100-101`) fails on a bare `new HitPayload {` outside the stamping helpers and on direct reads of `StorySpecialDamageMultiplier` / `StoryTemporaryDamageMultiplier` / the AbilityDamage lane under `scripts/Characters/Abilities/`. Build strikes via `WithAbilityContract`/`Stamp`; credit via `BaseSpecial.CreditDealt`.
5. **Momentum / D1a / D3.** Decision 3 says synced strikes count as landed and earn Momentum; today construct hits never do, and `StorySpecialRecoveryCancelTests` / `FighterSpecialRecoveryCancelTests` pin "constructs never arm D3". Making a strike count needs a new path, test edits, and care that it cannot re-arm hit-confirm. `NoteLanded` requires the busy cast's slot. Rally: constructs never reclaim (D03g, `StoryRallyTests`): keep Construct delivery / `collectsEcho:false`. Influence credit stays.
6. **Reach rule per mode.** Story 2D circle vs sim x-only; turret 30 u vs 10 u; nest 60 px vs 1.0 u. The nest growing to about 2.5 u touches `KitReachRules.SerpentNestWidthUnits` shared by both modes, `CleopatraContentTests` and the design master's "2.0 units wide".
7. **Lorentz discharge** ("near a coil") is ambiguous: Tesla near the coil, the coil inside the 2.5 u pulse radius, or the target in the coil's reach. Story pulse: circle on Tesla (`TeslaAbilities.cs:200`); sim pulse: zone type 82 centred on Tesla (`FighterEntitySystems.cs:1760-1790`, chain consumer `:3358-3365`). The mark-based T01 chain (8 u, 8 dmg per coil) overlaps; say whether it is replaced.
8. **Turret extras** tied to the 4-bolt budget (section 3).
9. **Pool contract.** Story nodes re-enter the tree per spawn but `_Ready` runs once; reset new fields in `Initialize` and `OnDespawn`; keep hurtbox subscription on the enter/exit pair (audit H-3).
10. **WIP collision.** The grab pass edits `FighterSimulationSystems.cs` (`OnSwingResolved` region), `FighterSimulationComponents.cs` (310 `SpareInt0`), `PlayerController.cs`, `EnemyController.cs`, `FighterSimulationDriver.cs`; hook insertions there will conflict until it merges.
