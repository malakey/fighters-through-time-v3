## 2. A lingering slow under stronger-wins

`StatusRouting.ShouldReplace` (`StatusSlots.cs:94-103`; raw sibling `:113-122`) replaces a same-type occupant when `incomingIntensity x incomingDuration >= currentIntensity x currentRemaining`. Refreshing every frame with the full linger duration at the **same intensity** always replaces (the occupant's remaining only shrinks), which is how the Rift's 0.5 s linger works today in both modes. A weaker-intensity or shorter-total application is a silent no-op; a different type (Root, StaticCharge) always replaces the slow.

Recommendation: keep the Rift pattern (refresh while inside, linger = StatusDuration, now 1.5-2.0 s). No new state, no exit detection. Costs: (a) in Story each refresh clears and re-adds the slot and raises `RaiseStatusEffectApplied` every physics frame (`StatusController.cs:183-198`; enemies re-run `_glow.SetStatus`), already true for the Rift; (b) all slow sources should share one intensity and differ only in duration, because the product rule lets a lower-intensity long application (Yorick 0.6 x 2.5 = 1.5) replace a heavier short one (1.2 x 1.0 = 1.2) and then slow less.
**x0.4 needs no formula change**: intensity 1.2 gives max(0.1, 1 - 0.6) = 0.4 in Story and the same in the sim (both clamp at 0.1, so intensity up to 1.8 is valid). Do not touch the formula: about a dozen enemy/boss ability `.tres` (`temporal_blades`, `dilation_field`, `holo_dagger`, `cold_gas_vent`, `gravity_well`, `inverted_gravity_field`, `sandstorm_veil`, `soliloquy_shard`, `chronal_collapse`), `ChronalRiftZone.cs:46` (3600 s at 1.0), `MovementDampenerZone`, Level 10 daggers, the Paris sink (`FighterEntitySystems.cs:2717`, 1.0) and the Vesuvius pool (`:2705`, 0.5) all use it. "Lower jump" is the same multiplier; a separate jump factor needs a second constant (Story already has `StatusJumpMultiplier` as its own property, `StatusController.cs:31`; the sim reuses `statusMoveMultiplier` for jumps, `FighterSimulationSystems.cs:1272,1279`, so a split is an extra FP64 local, not state).
The Vortex needs the slow applied **on entry** (a per-frame refresh like the Rift), or the pull outruns the slow for the first 0.4 s in Story.

## 5. Golden Ratio "all three connect"

Ticks at t, t+18, t+36 frames; the Story radius grows 40 -> 120 px (a victim in tick 1 stays inside). Applied hitstun must be at least 18 frames after every rule below.
Story rules that eat it (`EnemyController.ApplyStun` `:2157-2233`, `StoryEnemyCombatRules`, `EnemyStaggerRules`):
- `resisted = duration x (1 - StunResistance)`, resistance up to 0.65 (`:2165`).
- **Diminishing special stun**: any Special-class stun within 4.0 s of the previous one is x0.5 (`:2174-2181`; `EnemyStaggerRules.cs:62-66`). Ticks 2 and 3 are halved. Authored H gives H, H/2, H/2, so H >= 36 frames for tick 2 to hold 18 (up to about 103 against 0.65 resistance).
- `_stunTimer = stun` **sets** rather than maxes (`:2208`): a halved refresh can shorten the remaining stun.
- Standard poise: budget 139.5 frames; the first Special charges x1.0 but the budget is sized for at most 36 frames in total (`FirstSpecialStunAllowanceFrames`, `StoryEnemyCombatRules.cs:199`; `StoryFirstSpecialTaxTests` recomputes every Special). H=36 charges 36+18+18 = 72, so string + Golden Ratio (102+72) trips Armored Recovery and the committed counterattack. Elites: 120-frame budget, one-for-one.
- Natural stun expiry arms 36 frames of getup armor (no stun/knockback): if a hold runs out before the next tick, later ticks lose their stun.
- S2 proration is fine: all ticks share the AttackID (role FirstSpecial, untaxed, `ClassifyChainSpecial` `:147-157`).
Per-tick hitstun alone therefore breaks the poise contract. Options: (a) exempt later ticks of the same execution from the x0.5 diminish and from the standard-poise charge, then raise the allowance; (b) one long stun on tick 1 (>= 48 frames) and later ticks re-stun without charging; (c) a short held/Root-like control status. Sim: set `pulseHitstunFrames` for zone 21 from the contract (the .tres says 12; needs >= 20), non-launching. `blockCancelableHitstun` defaults true for pulses, so a grounded victim holding Block escapes the hold (hit-2 escape), and blocked Special pulses cost two charges each (a shatter after two ticks).

## 6. Vortex pull vs run speeds

Run speeds (u/s): Joan 6.2, Cleopatra 5.8, Einstein/Mozart/Tubman 5.4, Leonardo/Shakespeare 5.2, Tesla 4.8, Lincoln 3.8. Story pull is a positional horizontal 3 u/s through `PullThroughCollision` (`BaseSpecial.cs:617`: walls and lock barriers stop it, `StoryKitPullWallTests`); sim 0.05 u/frame. Today only a slowed Lincoln (3.8 x 0.6 = 2.3) loses to it. A pull "stronger than walking" needs more than 6.2 u/s (at least 0.11 u/frame), updated by hand in the sim literal (not in `KitReachRules`).
Interactions: (1) with x0.4 a slowed victim walks 1.5-2.5 u/s, so even 3 u/s holds them once the slow is on; the new pull matters in the unslowed first moments. (2) **Escapes fail geometrically**: radius 2.5 u (Story circle of 150 px, sim 2.5 half-extent box) against a x0.16 jump (about 0.5 u) and a roll whose travel is `run x 1.875 x StatusMovementMultiplier` (`PlayerController.cs:2419`) under a faster pull; only i-frames survive, not leaving. Decide whether the pull skips an invulnerable roll/air-dodge/Defy body (the sim `ApplyVortexPull` already skips `InvulnerabilityFrames > 0`, `FighterEntitySystems.cs:3434-3440`; the Story pull has no such check) or the zone is a tall box instead of a circle. (3) Story pull ignores armor and knockback immunity: bosses and **dormant encounter-lock members** are pulled (the query uses `StoryShapeQuery.DeliveryMask` with no dormancy filter; the tick hit is refused for them but the position write is not). (4) The sim pull is horizontal only and needs AABB overlap. (5) Open stages: a 2.5 u pull beside a pit can carry a fighter off-stage (Paris, Vesuvius, Nassau). (6) The throw clamps the center to the radius from a wall (`CleopatraAbilities.cs:207`), so it follows the new constant.
Final launch: `tick.Launches = finalTick && Data.Launches` (`CleopatraAbilities.cs:288`); Tick delivery never arms D3 or Momentum.
