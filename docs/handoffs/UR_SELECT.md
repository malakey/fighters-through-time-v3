# UR-SELECT handoff (pulp UI redesign, Phase B)

Branch `ur/select`, from `main` at `22219329`. The Fighter character select as CARD-B trading cards (5 x 2), its stage phase, and the Holodeck console panel.

## What shipped

- **Scene** `scenes/menus/CharacterSelect.tscn` rewritten: `select_split.png` with `halftone_screen.png` at 0.80 behind everything, `StageScrim` (shown with the stage phase), the banner on `Title` / `StageTitle` (`BannerHeading` role on the `banner_select` plate, 844 x 90, tilted -1 degree and re-applied after every container sort), `Grid columns = 5`, footer buttons `PulpButtonCompact` 316 x 58, stage buttons `PulpButton`, `RulesRow` an `HFlowContainer` (same path), the old gold Fight override gone.
- **Cards** (`scripts/UI/CharacterSelectScreen.Cards.cs`, new partial). The grid is **built in code**, one `Button` per roster member plus `RandomTile` last (the scene carries no tiles), so the roster growing adds cards; `CardGridRules` (pure) gives 5 columns, `RowsFor`, and `ScaleFor` (1 up to two rows, then `2 / rows`, floor 0.5; applied as a `Scaler` inner node and the button minimum size and grid separations). Tiles stay `FocusMode.None`, skip-group, cursor-driven. Each tile: `Scaler` > `Tilt` (rotation and -10 lift live here, never on the button) > `FrameBack` (the P2 frame peeking out when both players hold the card) / `Frame` (`CardPlate` / `CardPlateP1` / `CardPlateP2`) / `Photo` (`card_field`, `card_halftone`, `Portrait` at 132 %; Random: `Silhouette` ColorRect with the `hatch_random` tile and a `CardQuestion` "?") / `CharacterName` (`CardName`, upper-case) / `CharacterDates` (`CardDates`) / `Stamp` (`StampPlate`, `CardStamp`, -10 degrees, on a locked card) / `Badge1`, `Badge2` (50 px ink discs with a paper ring holding the `slot_p1` / `slot_p2` icon in `UIPalette.P1` / `P2`). `TokenBadge` (the glyph text `TileBadgeText` reads) survives as a hidden Label.
- **Player panels.** Each `P{n}Panel` now holds the star (`star_badge_sh/fill/ink`, fill tinted `UIPalette.P1` / `P2`, "P1" / "P2" in `StarBadgeText`), the "HMM..." bubble (`P{n}Thinking`) and the READY bubble (`P1Ready` / `P2Ready`: the `bubble` plate as the Label's own box plus `bubble_tail`, idle one modulated #D8CDB2), the join prompts, and the paper stat card `P{n}Card` (`PulpTaggedPanel` tag = the style key > `PaperPanel` > `Inset` > `Col`): `P{n}Name` (`SideCardName`, full name), three rows (`StatLabel`, a 118 x 14 `ProgressBar` on the `bar_track_h14` plate with `PulpBarDecor`, `StatValue`) and four 50 px `PulpAbilitySlot`s (`AbilityIconLibrary`: Special 1, Special 2, Movement, Ultimate). `P{n}Stats` is a hidden Label carrying the reduced stat line (new key `fighter_stats_card`: HP, Speed, Weight, Style) for tests and screen readers. The bar maxima are `CharacterSelectScreen.CardMaxima`, computed from every roster member's `CharacterData` (`CardStatMaxima.FromValues`), never literals.
- **Footer** `PulpButtonDecor` (compact) on the three buttons; a `PulpPromptBar` (`ui_accept` / `common_confirm`, `ui_cancel` / `common_cancel`) at the end of `Root`. Footer focus rules, token polling, countdown, join prompts, and `FighterFlowRoutes` are untouched.

- **Holodeck console** (`HolodeckConsolePanel.cs`): `Center/Tagged` (`PulpTaggedPanel`, tag = the screen title key; the old title Label is gone) > `Panel` (`InkPanel`, 720 min) > `Layout`; `PulpButtonCompact` + decor on Launch / Drills / Back; `PulpToggleDecor` on both toggles; `MenuSafeAreaFit` now attaches to the tagged panel. ## Provisional values and choices
- **Life dates** exactly as the brief lists them (Joan and Tubman with "c."; Cleopatra "69-30 BC"), en dash U+2013. Card names are new `character_<id>_card_name` keys (short names), because `hero_name_leonardo` is "Da Vinci".
- **Stat line** = HP, Speed, Weight, Style: the rows show the first three, the tag shows the style; the one-line `fighter_stats_card` text is hidden. Weight format `0.##`, speed `0.##`, HP `0`.
- The bubble is 237 x 74 (idle 214), the tail overlaps the plate by 5; both nominal from the spec. The "HMM..." idle bubble is also shown for a joined player who has not locked, as in the mock.
- Tilt: P1 -1.6 degrees and -10 px, P2 +1.6; a shared card draws `FrameBack` offset (16, 12) rotated 4 degrees. Badges: P2's moves 56 px left of P1's when both are on one card.
- Four ability slots (the brief), where the mock draws three.

## Kit and spec gaps
- No hero sprites on the side margins (UI_SPEC layer 3), no bubble pointing at a hero's head: the brief did not ask for them; the bubbles sit under the stars.
- Button labels draw mixed-case until UR-WIDGETS step 3 (`PulpButtonDecor` upper-casing) lands.
- `PulpAbilitySlot` at 50 px shows no key tag; nothing names the slot (the mock has none either).
- The stat bar is the theme's `PulpBar` with its background duplicated onto `bar_track_h14` (content margins from the theme's h30 plate); I did not verify those margins against UI_SPEC 3.3.1 pixel for pixel.

## Capture comparison (`D:\Projects\ftt-scratch\ur-captures\select\`)
Against `mix_select.png`: the split background and halftone, the gold banner (tilt, Cinzel heading), the five-column cards (field, halftone, portrait crop, name plate, dates, P1 / P2 frames with tilt and lift, badges with the slot shapes, READY stamp), the stars, the bubbles with tails, the paper stat cards with tags, bars, values and four ability icons, the footer banners, the hint line and the key-cap prompt bar read as the mock. Differences seen: the stat bar's segmented look differs a little from the mock (more ticks visible); "SHAKESPEARE" is as wide as its plate (as in the mock); the footer labels are mixed-case; the `RemotePlayNotice` label draws a focus outline (it holds focus by design) and sits between the hint and the prompt bar, where the mock has no such line; when the countdown shows, the whole centred block moves up by the label's height (the label is shown and hidden, not reserved). Stage phase and Holodeck captures have no mock; they read cleanly (toggles on / off, banner buttons, the tagged ink panel).

**At 140 % UI scale** (`select_140`) the screen stays on the screen but the fixed-size card text overflows: names such as "LEONARDO" and "CLEOPATRA" touch their neighbours, "JOAN OF ARC" and "NIKOLA TESLA" are clipped on the stat cards, and the dates crowd the names. Cards are fixed art at 196 x 276; I did not shrink the text (no font-size overrides). A real fix needs a per-role scale rule for card plates, or shorter names, and is left for a decision.
