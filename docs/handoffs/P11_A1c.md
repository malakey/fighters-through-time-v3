## 1. What shipped

### The 31-sample Echo Step ring (V7.6 temporal contract)

The retired ring held **5 samples written every 6 frames** and read the oldest, so the destination
was somewhere **24–30 frames back depending on the phase the chord landed on**. It is replaced by
**31 consecutive per-tick samples** and the **exact `t − 30`** one.

- **Components 313–317** (`FighterEchoStepRing0..4Component`), 6/6/6/6/7 samples. Klotho component
  **311 is retired**. `FighterEchoStepRing` (in `FighterEchoStepRules.cs`) is the only place that
  knows how a whole-ring slot maps onto a slice.
- Slice 0 also carries `Head`, `LatestTick`, `ValidCount`, `Generation`, `LifeEpoch`, `Armed`,
  `ActivationTick` — **not** A1b's component 312 (§9 item 1).
- **Sampling is hoisted above the hitstop short-circuit** in `FighterMovementSystem.Update`, so the
  ring really is one sample per authoritative tick and an advancing hitstop tick records the same
  position, as the contract specifies. Nothing else in the loop moved.
- With 31 slots, `head` (the next slot to overwrite) **is** the `t − 30` slot — no search, no
  rounding, no interpolation.
- **Generations** open at spawn, at a stock loss (through the `LifeEpoch` mismatch the sampler
  detects) and at Sudden Death setup. Spawn-filled storage is never counted as history:
  `ValidCount` gates activation, so a fighter genuinely must live 30 further ticks.
- Story mirrors all of it in `PlayerController` (`_echoStepRing[31]`, `ResetEchoStepHistory()`),
  with generation resets wired into `CompleteStoryRewind` and `RestoreStoryCheckpoint`.

### Exact-or-no-teleport destination policy

`FighterEchoStepRules.IsDestinationClear` is the sim-side predicate — the mirror image of the four
position-level rules the movement system actually applies (wall clamp, ceiling clamp, blast zone,
floor snap). Story's equivalent is a `PhysicsShapeQueryParameters2D` sweep of the player's real
collision shape against the Environment layer.

- **Validated before anything is spent.** An invalid or unavailable destination refuses the
  activation at **no meter and no cooldown cost** and does not start the wind-up. Previously
  `TryStartEchoStep` spent the meter and armed the cooldown unconditionally, and `AdvanceEchoStep`
  wrote the destination straight into `fighter.Position`: a target inside terrain or outside the
  stage teleported there.
- **Rechecked at wind-up completion.** A destination that became blocked **cancels the teleport with
  the committed cost retained** — no refund, no nearby substitute, no added immunity.

### Dedicated inputs (C01c) and protocol v3

- `gameplay_grab` and `gameplay_echo_step` in `project.godot`, **Unbound by default** on both device
  kinds; `InputManager.Actions.Grab` / `.EchoStep`.
- Wire bits: **`EchoStep = 1 << 12`, `Grab = 1 << 13`, `DirectOrigin = 1 << 14`**. Bit 15 is free and
  the reserved Dash bit 11 is untouched. `PlayerInputFrame.SerializedSize` (12 B) and
  `RollbackInputPacket.SerializedSize` (47 B) are **unchanged**.
- `InputManager` raises each verb bit from **either** route — the direct bind, or the preset chord
  while that chord is enabled for the device — and the sim deduplicates to **one request per actor
  per tick** (`FighterGrabRules.Requested`, and the Echo Step path). Neither route synthesizes a
  component press; a direct bind grants no extra priority, cancel, leniency or resource bypass; a
  refused direct action does nothing and spends nothing.
- **`DirectOrigin`** is the normalized instruction to a peer: *my chords are off, do not read my
  component bits as a chord.* That is what stops a peer re-recognizing a remote chord with its own
  shortcut settings. **`RollbackProtocol.ProtocolVersion` → 3.**

### Same-frame chord priority

`BasicComboRules.SelectRecoveryVerb` is the single table both modes read. Previously the ordering was
**incidental**: the sim called `TryStartEchoStep` eight lines before it evaluated the roll cancel, so
Echo Step won by accident, the grab chord was resolved in a different system entirely, and Story
called all three from three separate sites. The rule: **the chord beats the single input, and the
priced verb beats the free one.** A chord whose priced verb is unavailable falls back to the
block-cancel rather than eating the input.

### `CharacterState.Thrown` (F23)

Appended **after** `Grabbing`; `Dashing` keeps its reserved ordinal. A thrown victim rides out its own
state — no landing tech, no block escape, fixed trajectory. `PlayerController.ApplyStun(duration,
thrown)` is the transition, routed from the hit pipeline by
`BasicComboRules.ThrowHitboxID`. Sim-side, `FighterVerbComponent` gained a packed
`GrabPartnerPlayerID` + `ThrowDamageApplied` (the partner used to be resolved positionally, which no
snapshot could describe; the throw had no once-only damage guard beyond the phase machine).

### F21 mode consolidation

- `MatchMode` values are explicit; `Hybrid = 2` is `[Obsolete]`, reserved, and **removed from both
  selectors**. `FighterAudioRules.ModeUsesStocks` no longer answers true for it, and
  `FighterMatchSystem.Update`'s silent `_ =>` default arm is an explicit Stock fallback.
- **Time mode is decided by fewest stocks lost.** `FighterMatchComponent.PlayerOneKOs`/`PlayerTwoKOs`
  counted the KOs that player *scored* and the winner test picked the larger — the opposite
  bookkeeping to the contract. They are now victim-side `PlayerOneStocksLost` / `PlayerTwoStocksLost`,
  fed from `KnockoutsSuffered` through the single chokepoint `ApplyStockLoss`, so every cause counts
  once with no attacker credit and duplicate callbacks cannot repeat it. Equal totals, 0-0 included,
  enter Sudden Death with **no HP tiebreak**.
- `SavedMatchSettings.Normalize()` — idempotent, run before loaded settings are displayed or the next
  match is created, never against a running match. Legacy Hybrid → timed Stock keeping a valid timer
  and stock count; an Off/missing/nonfinite Hybrid timer → 480 s flagged `TimerRepaired`; stock counts
  clamp to 1–5 defaulting to 3; **unknown values are not Hybrid**. Switching untimed Stock → Time
  selects 480 s and writes it to the spin box before launch.
- `fighter_mode_hybrid` deleted from `en.csv` (A1c owns that row); no orphan is created because the
  row is removed in the same change.

### F22 Sudden Death

`EnterSuddenDeath` is rewritten as a shared round reset. Retired: the **1-HP clamp**, the
**forced-hazards-on** override of the house rules, and the **pre-marked Defy flag**.

Living fighters keep their exact current positive HP (unequal included); a fighter dead or
respawning at the end of regulation gets its normal respawn HP **once**, recorded on component 319 so
a duplicate transition cannot grant it twice. Hazards respect the toggle — **Off stays Off**; On gets
twice-normal cadence and a full first warning, never re-multiplied by Overtime. Meter 0, every
cooldown ready, block refilled, both status slots / Rally pool / marks cleared, every regulation
projectile, zone, construct and hazard swept, orbs disabled, old invulnerability cleared. Defy is
disabled by **derivation** from `SuddenDeathActive`, so the spent flag is preserved and entry consumes
no use. Regulation stocks-lost totals are frozen on **component 319** and reach `MatchResults`. Entry
runs the shared ready countdown, freezing controls and sim clocks for both players together.

## 5. AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md edits required (Phase C — not made here, per §5.1)

1. **`MatchMode`**: drop the third playable mode. Fighter Mode is Stock or **Time**; Time is decided
   by fewest stocks lost with unlimited respawns and no HP tiebreak.
2. **Echo Step** (Combat / Fighter sections): "5 samples every 6 frames, ~30 frames back" → **31
   consecutive per-tick samples, exact `t − 30`**, validated at activation (refusal costs nothing) and
   rechecked at wind-up completion (cancel, cost retained).
3. **`FighterVerbComponent` (310) has no headroom left** — the "4 bytes of headroom" note is stale.
   Component **311 is retired**; **313–317** are the Echo Step bank and **319** is
   `FighterSuddenDeathComponent`.
4. **Networking**: "protocol v2" → **protocol v3**, carrying the logical combined verbs and the
   direct-vs-preset origin flag. Packet sizes are unchanged.
5. **`GameplayButtons`**: bits 12/13/14 are `EchoStep`/`Grab`/`DirectOrigin`; bit 15 is the only free
   one; the reserved Dash bit 11 is still never reused.
6. **`CharacterState` canonical list**: append `Thrown` after `Grabbing`.
7. **Sudden Death** (Combat section): the "respawned both at 1 HP, hazards accelerated, first hit
   decides, Defy pre-marked used" description is retired — living HP is retained, the hazard toggle is
   respected, the **next actual death** decides, and Defy's spent flag is preserved.
8. **Test baseline**: +38 from this workstream.
