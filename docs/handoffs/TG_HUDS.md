## Provisional values and agent choices

1. **The D11 floor is 22.8 px on screen** (`BossNameFloorPixels`): 0.6 of 38 at 100 %, 0.43 of the canvas size at 140 %. Taken literally (0.6 of the canvas size, 31.9 px on screen at 1.4) the floor would cut seven names at 1.4 (the band is 363 canvas units there; every name over 552 units at full size: The Thunderbomb General, The Chronal Inventor, The Borgia Inquisitor, The Governor's Guard, The Vulcan Decimator, The Siegemaster Duke, The Gravity Overseer). With the pixel floor none is cut; the spec expected only The Thunderbomb General to be. The fit then drops the phase label under the name (right-aligned, 4 px below the name's descent, the tube 8 px below it) and only then ellipsizes.
2. **Both clusters scale about their own screen corners** (the HUD's `UiScaleBinder`, unchanged): the left stack's 32 / 24 margins scale with it (44.8 / 33.6 at 1.4, right edge 912.8). 4.4 says "about (32, 24)" but its own band formula, min(840 s, 1920 - 1008 s), only holds with the stack scaling about the screen's corner (with (32, 24) fixed the band would be 1888 - 976 s, 521.6 at 1.4). I kept the formula.
3. **The status chips keep fixed positions** (HUD_CONTRACT: hiding or changing one never shifts the other): each slot is as wide as its widest status name in the current language, so the control chip sits 30 px after "RADIANT BURN" (x about 312) rather than 30 px after the shown name (4.4 row 3.1e.2 gives x 226.4 for "VENOM"). Re-measured on a language change.
4. **The Integrity caption follows the spec, not the render**: its right edge 14 px left of the dial (the CSS rule) and centred on it (D3). The render draws it 39 px further left and 12 px lower, both artefacts of the leaking `.cap` grid rule D3 names.
5. **The checkpoint toast**: fade-rise 0.22 s, hold 2 s, fade 0.3 s (9.2 leaves the hold and fade to the agent). It is a `Label` wearing a duplicate of the theme's `PanelTagPlate` box with the flag's margin added. It stays at the top centre unless that would cover a cluster, and otherwise sits at the near edge of the boss bar's free band (only at large UI scales; at 1.4 it starts at x 946). TG-PAUSE's first-use tooltip shares the top-centre y 140 position and may meet the same stack at 1.4 (not checked here; not my file).
6. **The boss intro card**: the name shrinks to fit the canvas less 40 px a side (only reached at 1.4); it fades out at the end of its beat (MOT-A row 5, no sink, since it is centred).
7. **The wedge's alphas** ride its `SelfModulate` (0.85) so `FillColor` stays the semantic colour R8's test reads; the remainder is `WedgeRemainder` at 0.55 overall.
8. **Hero name**: shrinks about its baseline when wider than 380 px, never below 18 px, then ellipsizes ("WOLFGANG AMADEUS MOZART" fits at full size).
9. **Phase label**: cross-fades in over 0.22 s when the phase changes (a fade, so the same under Reduced Temporal Effects); the notches are static.
10. Kept from the build: low HP at 25 % or less (`LowHpFraction`), the status time in tenths (`hud_seconds_short`: "4.0s" where the mock writes "4s"), the HUD's own `+N` float timings.
