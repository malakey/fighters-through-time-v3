## What shipped

### The burst-on-terrain projectile primitive (owned by W7a, both modes)
- **Data.** `AbilityData.ProjectileBurstRadius` (px; 0 = single-stage shot), `ProjectileContactDamage`, `ProjectileBurstsOnTerrain`. When a burst radius is authored, `BaseDamage` is the **burst** and `ProjectileContactDamage` the contact stage. Projected loadout-only into `FighterAbilityHitData.BurstRadius / ContactDamage / BurstsOnTerrain / Bursts` (no snapshot field, no component).
- **Sim.** `FighterProjectileSystem(contracts, geometry)` now takes the stage geometry. A bursting Special projectile's contact deals `ContactDamage` with no knockback, launch or status; then `FighterProjectileBurstRules.Burst` applies the projectile's own damage/status with the slot's contract (signed vector, hitstun, launch) to the opponent inside the radius (`FighterKitGeometry.CircleOverlapsBox`). With `BurstsOnTerrain` it also bursts at the side walls, ceiling, the floor plane where the stage has floor, and at lifetime expiry (max range); non-bursting projectiles keep the legacy `|x| > 12` / lifetime despawn.
- **Story.** `PlaceholderProjectile.BurstsOnTerrain` (an `Environment`-layer ray along each step; bursts at the contact point, or at lifetime), `StopsOnTerrain` (vanish at terrain, no burst — the turret's line of sight), `MakeMinorContactStage()`, `ConfigureDirection(Vector2)` (straight shot along any direction), `ConfigureConstructHit()`. All cleared on despawn.
- **For W7b (Mozart's Requiem Chord):** author the three fields and branch on the projectile type inside `FighterProjectileBurstRules.Burst` if the burst needs pulses rather than one hit.

### Einstein
- **E=mc² (E05):** `special_1.tres` speed 720 (12 u/s), startup 14 / recovery 20, `ProjectileContactDamage 7`, `BaseDamage 20` burst in `ProjectileBurstRadius 72` (1.2 u), `ProjectileBurstsOnTerrain` — straight, no range cap (lifetime 5 s outlasts any wall-to-wall crossing). The sim now deals the burst (it dealt 20 on contact only).
- **Relativity Rift (E03):** thrown up to 5 u ahead, stopping at a wall; ground-snapped when cast grounded (sim: `FighterKitGeometry.GroundBelow` — platform or floor under the point), mid-air when airborne; 1.5 u (Story 90 px, sim box half-extents 1.5); 3 s; `StatusDuration 0.5` is the linger — the slow is refreshed every frame an opponent overlaps (`FighterRiftRules.RefreshLinger`; Story `EinsteinRelativityRift.RefreshLinger` through `IStatusEffectTarget`) and lasts 0.5 s after leaving; re-entry refreshes through stronger-wins.
- **Rift Collapse (E01), both modes:** an E=mc² burst inside Einstein's own open rift ends it (ticks and speed buff stop) and pulls every caught opponent to its centre over 6 frames (`KitMotionRules.RiftCollapsePullFrames`), then launches upward (`RiftCollapseLaunchUnits` 6, provisional) with a 0-damage Special-class hit: no meter, no Rally. **Who is caught is decided at the detonation instant, before the burst resolves** — so a blocker that the burst then shatters is still not pulled; a target who raises the stance mid-pull is not launched. Sim state is on the rift's own zone entity (`FighterZoneComponent.CollapseFramesRemaining`, `CollapseTargetMask` — new fields on component **309**, no new ID). Story: `EinsteinRelativityRift.TryCollapse(point)`, `IsCollapsing`, called by `EinsteinEmc2Blast.Detonate`; bosses (immovable), roll/post-rewind/Defy-protected players and blockers are not caught.
- **Relativity Warp (E04):** a spacetime fold. `movement.tres` startup 10 / active 1 / recovery 1, `DistanceMoved 240` (4 u), `MovementDuration 0`. Sim: kit phase **30** `FighterKitMotion.WarpFoldStartup` in component 305's universal-movement slot — holds in place for 10 frames (hittable; hitstun cancels it through the existing cancel sites, cooldown already spent), then relocates instantly by `FighterKitMotion.WarpFoldDestination` (held 8-way direction, clamped so the binding axis lands exactly on the wall/ceiling/floor plane), then 60 float frames. Story: `EinsteinRelativityWarp` shows a placeholder ghost (`ColorRect`) at the destination through the startup, resolves clearance with a `CastMotion` of Einstein's own body shape against `Environment`, relocates on the active frame, floats if the fold ends in the air, and hides the ghost on `Interrupt`.

### Leonardo
- **Golden Ratio (L02):** `DamageTickIntervalFrames 18`, `Lifetime 0.7` → exactly three ticks (0 / 18 / 36 frames) in both modes; the sim box is 2.0 × 2.0; 12/18 unchanged; the light outward shove is still the expiry pulse.
- **Clockwork Turret (L03):** placed at his feet (Story; the sim already was). Bolts are real straight projectiles at 12 u/s (`ProjectileSpeed 720`, `ProjectileLifetime 2.5`) aimed at the nearest target, fired only while one is in range — Story also requires line of sight (`Environment` ray) and stops the bolt on terrain; the lifetime is the idle cap and 4 bolts the ammunition. Sim: `FighterTurretRules.TryFireBolt` spawns a construct bolt (`ProjectileTypeID = character*10 + 5`, `FighterTurretRules.ConstructBoltSlot`) that hits Basic-class, impulse-free, non-launching, without Rally or hitstop. Story bolts are `PlaceholderProjectile`s with `ConfigureConstructHit`; `Hitbox.CreatePayload` now stamps `ExemptFromHitstop` for `HitDelivery.Construct` (no other `Hitbox` used that delivery). `BaseSpecial.SpawnStoryProjectile(parent, position)` is the pooled spawner for non-cast callers. No pool → the historical instant strike is the fallback.
- **Glide bolt in the sim (D14):** `FighterTurretRules.TryCommandGlideBolt` — a basic-attack press during Leonardo's Ornithopter glide (`FloatFrames > 0`, airborne, not in hitstun/daze) fires his turret's next bolt now, once per flight (`FighterPersistentObjectComponent.GlideBoltSpent`, new field on component **303**, cleared by `BeginFlight`), spending one of the four. The press still starts his aerial swing in the sim (Story's glide consumes it).

### Shakespeare
- **Yorick's Lament (S03):** thrown straight at 600 px/s (10 u/s) for 0.8 s (8 u), `ProjectileContactDamage 5`, `BaseDamage 16` wave in `ProjectileBurstRadius 90` (1.5 u), bursting on a fighter, terrain or max range; the slow rides the wave only (applied once). The sim gains the wave through the primitive.
- **The Tempest (S01/S02) is a windbox, not a hit, in both modes:** 8 startup / 12 active / 16 recovery, `Lifetime 0.2`. An opponent the storm touches is caught (sim: `CollapseTargetMask` reused as the Tempest's caught set; Story: `_caught`) and pushed 0.25 u (15 px) outward every remaining frame — ~3 u — by a positional shift (sim `FighterTempestRules.Push`; Story `MoveAndCollide`, so walls stop it). No `ApplyFighterHit`/payload at all: no block charge, hitstun, hitstop, shieldstun, DI, tumble, meter or Rally. Grab states are never moved (both modes); Story also refuses an Ultimate cinematic (`UsingUltimate`) and a knockback-immune boss (`ShakespeareTheTempest.CanBePushed`). Lift ~2.5 u: sim launch `√(2·30·2.5)`; Story drives −750 px/s through the 12 active frames, then a normal fall. The sim's old Tempest pulse branch and `TempestHitstunFrames` are deleted.
- **Prospero's Flight (A08) is a gust burst:** `MovementType.Gust = 6` (appended), `DistanceMoved 240`, `ActiveFrames 20`, `MovementDuration 1/3`. Sim: kit phase **31** `FighterKitMotion.GustBurst` — 12 u/s forward and 7.5 u/s up for 20 frames (gravity suspended), then velocity zeroed and an ordinary fall; no glide. Story: `ShakespeareProsperosFlight` gust for its active frames, then `Velocity = 0`.
- **Midsummer Gust** (node `shakespeare_midsummer_glide`, perk key `midsummer_glide` and translation keys retained): 20 % more gust distance and 8.0 damage to enemies the gust carries him through, once each per gust.
- CPU: `CpuRecoveryProfile.MovementKindGust = 6` — provides lift, horizontal reach = the loadout's `MovementDistance`, lift = `KitMotionRules.ProsperoGustRiseUnits`; faced like a glide (not directional).
