## 1. The order-dependent flake — root cause and fix

**Symptom.** `EnemyControllerTests` failed about one full run in three, a different case each time (`StandGuardChasesNormallyAndReturnsToItsPostOnDeAggro` "expected Patrol got Returning", `FlyingChaserHovers…`), and passed 41/41 in isolation. The coordinator reported the same class in `BossControllerTests.RestWindowTrackingHolds…` and `StoryFsmM04TransitionTests.TheUltimateCancelsASwingAtAnyPoint` ("setup: Airborne … pos=(0,-3.08) vel=(0,600)" — velocity at terminal, position barely moved).

**Root cause.** These suites step bodies by calling `_PhysicsProcess(Step)` directly. Every state timer in that step uses the 1/60 the test passes, but `CharacterBody2D.MoveAndSlide()` takes no delta: Godot reads `get_physics_process_delta_time()` only while the engine is inside a physics frame and **otherwise falls back to the idle frame's `get_process_delta_time()`** — the wall-clock length of whatever runner frame the test landed in. A diagnostic `[BeforeTest]` hook across one full run measured it from **0.0003 s to 0.0174 s** (58×), decided by what the runner did before. At 0.0003 s a 252 px/s knight covers 0.08 px per step, so a 300-step walk-back stops short ("Returning"); a 400 px/s fall covers 0.12 px per step, so the M04 setup never lands. A probe reproduced it on demand (time scale 0.03 → delta 0.00020 → the stand-guard walk-back ended `Returning` at x = 16.31).

**Ruled out** (same diagnostic): no leaked `Players` member, grouped node, static registry (`SiphonTetherChannel`), `StoryDifficultyTuning` state, Time Freeze / world-hold state or leaked pause — at every `EnemyControllerTests` case the tree held only autoloads and pooled presentation nodes.

**Fix (two layers).**
1. **Harness:** `.runsettings` now launches the GdUnit Godot child with `--headless --fixed-fps 60`, which pins every idle frame's delta to exactly 1/60. Verified in a full run: all 41 `EnemyControllerTests` cases saw 0.01667. It fixes every out-of-band stepping suite at once (including the M04 and boss rest-window cases I did not edit), and the full suite also runs faster (1 m 02 s vs 1 m 40 s).
2. **Suite:** the nine movement-sensitive `EnemyControllerTests` cases now run their synchronous body inside a real `physics_frame` emission through `tests/Unit/OutOfBandPhysicsStep.RunInPhysicsFrameAsync`, where `MoveAndSlide` reads the fixed physics step regardless of harness flags. GdUnit requires such cases to be `async Task`. The helper's doc comment carries the full diagnosis.

## 2. M18 — the Borgia Inquisitor

- `borgia_inquisitor.tres`: `PhaseSpeedMultipliers` → `[1.0, 1.0]`; a third ability `after_image_dash` (ChargeDash, `RangeClass` Any, 900 px/s over 22 frames) gated `AbilityMinPhase = 1`.
- New `EnemyAbilityData.LeavesAfterImages` (additive). While such an ability's active phase runs, `BossController` sheds an `AfterImageGhost` (a tinted, fading snapshot of the current frame) every fourth frame. It is decorative, so it is **suppressed by Reduced Temporal Effects** (`ComfortSettings.GhostTrailsAllowed`).
- `Level01Controller` Phase 2 hook (`BossEncounterController.PhaseEntered`): both scaffolding platforms are now `CrumblingPlatform`s that run the standard beat (**0.8 s shake + 1.2 s collapse**, verified against the class defaults, which also pin the 5 s respawn) and never return (`RespawnDuration` 1e9, `RewindPolicy = PreserveCurrentState`). Two burning barricades (`AnimatableBody2D`) slide in from the outer walls over 1.6 s (inside the 2 s transition window) to inner faces 600 px apart (> the 420 px ranged band). A rewind landing outside the closed span is moved inside (`OnRecoveryLanded`). The build is hopped off a physics flush when the phase change comes from a killing hit.
- Level 1 still authors no Crumbling Platforms over a pit elsewhere; that layout line and lethal pits are W8/D11.

## 3. M19 — the Revolutionary Tribunal squad

- `BossData`: `enum BossPhaseTrigger { HpThreshold, MemberDefeat }` (default `HpThreshold`, so every other boss is unchanged), `SquadMemberCount`, `AbilityMember` (parallel to `BossAbilities`; negative = shared), and derived `IsSquad`, `MemberMaxHP` (= `MaxHP / SquadMemberCount`, so 850 is still authored once), `GetAbilityMember`.
- `BossController`: `SquadMemberIndex`, `IsSquadMember`, `AbsorbSquadMember(int)`, `EnterPhase(int)` (ordinary transition window; never regresses), `PhaseEntered` (per body), `Died`, `RaisesDefeatPayload`. A squad member fights with its own plus the shared abilities; the kit is rebuilt through the existing composite-kit path (the Borrowed Legacies one), and **cooldowns follow the ability, not its index**, so an absorb neither refunds nor re-arms anything. A `MemberDefeat` boss ignores `PhaseThresholds` for progression.
- `BossEncounterController`: spawns one body per member (240 px apart), `Members`, `CombinedCurrentHP`/`CombinedMaxHP`, one **shared HUD bar** holding the sum (with even shares the authored 0.5 notch sits exactly one member's pool down), `PhaseEntered` (deduplicated per encounter), `FallenMemberCount`. A member's death hands its abilities to the survivors and enters Phase 2; only the last death raises the EventBus defeat payload, so the **25-dust award is paid once**. `SetStoryRewindFrozen` and the intro hold freeze every body.
- `revolutionary_tribunal.tres`: `PhaseTrigger = MemberDefeat`, `SquadMemberCount = 2`, `AbilityMember = [-1, 0, 1, -1]` (Call the Mob and Tribunal Levy shared; Verdict Bolt = member 0, the prosecutor; Guillotine Drop = member 1, the executioner), and the design-table drift fixes **`IsKnockbackImmune = false`, `RestCooldown = 1.0`**. `MaxHP` 850 untouched (D10).
- `Level04Controller`: the courtyard floor is authored in three pieces; the centre (9200–9660) is a lid over a pre-built **non-lethal** trapezoid pit (120 px deep, 140 px ramps ≈ 40.6°, under the 45° walkable limit), so the player, the survivor and the rioters can all walk in and out. Phase 2 removes the lid through the safe shape setter. No kill boundary is authored (D11).

## 4. M20 — the Rift Phantom's Teleport line

- `EnemyData.SecondaryAbilities` + `SecondaryAbilityCooldown`: an **opt-in standard-tier secondary path** (not a promotion to Elite — no stagger budget, no elite dust). Derived `HasSecondaryAbilities`, `ActiveSecondaryAbilities`, `ActiveSecondaryCooldown`; `EnemyController.SelectNextAttack` alternates through the tier's list.
- A standard's Teleport is the **engage blink** (`EnemyController.TryEngageBlink`): when the target is beyond 1.5× its striking distance and inside aggro, it blinks to just past the target (the executor's far-side rule) and returns to Chase **without arming the primary's cooldown**, so the ambush swing follows. The elite's reactive Phase Skip is unchanged.
- `ResolveGuardCrush`'s elite implicit is now tier-gated (`Tier == Elite`), which is behaviour-preserving for every existing resource.
- `rift_phantom.tres` carries `enemy.rift_phantom.rift_step` (Teleport, 60–110 px, 20-frame telegraph, 6 s cooldown). Enemy abilities have no manifest rows; the pins are `RiftPhantomTeleportTests` (3) and `EnemyRetroSpriteAssetTests`' count.
- **The drone-carrier is not authored.** The GDD names "an Unbound drone-carrier variant" for the SummonMinions mob line but gives no stats, tier, era or roster slot; recorded as `DEFER-DRONE-CARRIER`.

## 5. GAP-07 — the three confirmed bosses

- **Jackal Priest** (`TeleportDecoyMinPhase = 1`, `DecoyLifetimeSeconds 6`, `DecoyBurstRadius 150`, `DecoyBurstDamage 14` on `jackal_priest.tres`). From Phase 2 every Teleport leaves a `BossDecoy` at the vacated spot: a sand-tinted copy of the priest's frame with an `EnemyHurtbox`. It never telegraphs (the real priest's cast tint and glyph are the tell). A **player** strike releases one Basic-class burst through the victim's own hit intake (block, Defy and every protection layer apply) and credits nothing; an enemy hitbox never sets it off. It is `time_freezable` and crumbles after its lifetime. No level edit was needed.
- **Tragedy King** (`GuardedSummonMinPhase = 1`, `GuardedSummonSceneSeconds = 10` on `tragedy_king.tres`). Entering Phase 2 casts his summon as soon as the transition window closes; every Phase 2 summon is a **guarded scene**: he refuses all damage and holds (performs, does not fight) until every actor has fallen or the scene runs out, when the survivors "bow" (are released off-stage). Presented with the shared gold armor glow. `Level10Controller` holds the two arena trapdoors still through Phase 1 and sets them cycling at Phase 2. The actors are still the existing `curtain_call` Holo-Page summons.
- **Chronal Inventor** (`ArenaGuardianMinPhase = 1`, `ArenaGuardianHP = 60`). `Level03Controller` builds two `ArenaGuardian` siphon coils at the Court of Honor's corners at Phase 2 and registers them with `BossController.RegisterGuardian`; he refuses all damage while either stands. A coil is struck through an `EnemyHurtbox` (only player hits count; it credits no meter or Rally).
