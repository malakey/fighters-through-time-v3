# Package 12 W1 — Story recovery and world suspension

Branch `p12/W1`. Items: R02, R03, M17, GAP-06, GAP-11. Closes ledger `VERIFY-05`.
Story-only: **nothing under `scripts/FighterSim/` changed**, so there is no deterministic hash impact.

## What shipped

### R02 — the Story revive edge
- `PlayerController.CompleteStoryRewind(Vector2 landing, int hp, bool? grounded = null)` now revives through an explicit
  Story edge `Dead -> Idle` (grounded) / `Dead -> Airborne` (ungrounded). The edge is open only for the duration of the
  placement (`_storyReviveEdgeOpen`); `TransitionTo` otherwise still refuses to leave `Dead` for anything but `Respawning`.
- `Respawning` is Fighter-only. Its ordinal and `ProcessRespawning` are kept.
- New observer: `PlayerController.StateTransitioned` (`Action<CharacterState, CharacterState>`), raised on every accepted
  transition. Presentation/test use only.
- `ChronalRewindManager` passes the landing sample's own `RewindFrame.IsGrounded` (the checkpoint fallback is grounded).

### R03 — the Post-Landing Hold
- `ChronalRewindManager.PostLandingHoldFrames = 60`. `PostRewindWorldFreezeFrames` is kept as a second *name* for the same
  constant; `IsWorldFreezeLingering` aliases `IsPostLandingHoldActive`.
- Applies to all four recoveries:
  - The death rewind and the Level 0 scripted rewind start it at the landing.
  - The Collapse resume and the Anchor Snap start it through `BeginPlacementHold(StoryRecoveryHoldCause)`.
  - `StoryManager` arms that placement hold in memory only:
    - `BeginAnchorSnap` arms it.
    - `RestartCollapsedLevel(resumeFromTimelineAnchor: true)` arms it, but only after an in-session `BeginTimelineCollapse`.
    - The arm is stamped with the destination scene path. The new scene's manager consumes it on its first tick via `TryConsumeRecoveryPlacementHold(scenePath, out cause)`.
    - It is cleared by `ResumeCampaign` (a save load), by the fresh-entry branch of `BeginLevelRun` and by `ResetCampaignState`.
    - So a reload never grants a hold (plan D3(a)).
- **Frozen set.** `ChronalRewindManager.PostLandingHoldGroups` is the union of `FrozenSimulationGroups` and `TimeFreezeController.FrozenTimeGroups`, so it includes `chronal_extractor`, the Mirror and `persistent_construct`.
  - Pooled projectiles and zones are parked through the Time Freeze `ProcessMode.Disabled` fallback.
  - A room revealed mid-hold is frozen: `TimeFreezeController.FreezeActivatedRoom` now also calls `ChronalRewindManager.RefreshWorldHold`.
  - A private `WorldSuspension` class owns the freeze and release bookkeeping.
- **Suspend path.** The Snap and Collapse holds, and every non-rewind group, use the Time Freeze path (`IStoryTimeFreezable.SetTimeFrozen`: latch only, nothing cancelled or cleared).
  - The death and scripted rewinds keep `SetStoryRewindFrozen` for the four rewind groups at initiation, for these reasons:
    - The T01a rewind start legitimately cancels in-flight enemy attacks and clears enemy projectiles.
    - The Mirror clone's invulnerability during the presentation rides `SetRewindSuspended`.
    - Existing pins depend on it.
  - Its release is a pure latch on every implementer (enemy, boss, extractor), so the hold has no catch-up.
- A live Time Freeze is ended at rewind start through the new `TimeFreezeController.EndFreezeForRecovery()`.
  - It hands its parked projectiles to the rewind rather than restoring them.
  - Before this, both freezes latched the same per-actor flag, so a freeze thawing a tick later released the world mid-rewind.
- **No credit.** The single shared query is `ChronalRewindManager.IsWorldHeld(SceneTree)`: hold active, or a GAP-06 boss suspension.
  - `Hitbox.OnAreaEntered` consults it next to the Time Freeze gate.
  - `Hurtbox.TakeHit` also consults it, because many kit abilities deliver through shape queries straight into `TakeHit`.
  - Result: no damage, stagger, status, mark, meter, Rally, checkpoint strike or extractor credit.
- **Blocked until the thaw:**
  - `TimeFreezeController.CanActivate(state, isFrozen, cooldownRemaining, alive, worldHeld = false)`, which `TryBeginTimeFreeze` passes the query to.
  - `InteractionArea.TryInteract`, via the new `PlayerController.IsRecoveryWorldHeld`. This covers the Restoration Font and puzzle controls.
  - Pickups, because `story_loot` is frozen.
  - Checkpoint strikes and extractors, through the Hurtbox gate.
- **Clocks.** The hold is live play:
  - Integrity resumes at the landing.
  - The Time Freeze cooldown runs. `IsPresentationHolding` deliberately excludes the hold.
  - The hero is never `TimeFrozen`.
- **Protection.** `CompleteStoryRewind` no longer arms it.
  - `PlayerController.BeginRecoveryHold(cause, frames)` zeroes any leftover protection.
  - `EndRecoveryHold()` arms the 120 frames at the thaw.
  - `ArmRecoveryProtection()` is public.
  - `CancelRecoveryHold()` drops a hold after a death inside it.
- **Events** (additive, `EventBus`):
  - `OnRecoveryLanded` and `OnRecoveryWorldThawed`, both carrying `RecoveryHoldPayload { PlayerIndex, Cause, HoldFrames, Position }`, published by `PlayerController`.
  - New enum `StoryRecoveryHoldCause { DeathRewind, ScriptedRewind, CollapseResume, AnchorSnap }`.
  - `RewindPresentationPhase.Thawed`, appended.
- **Thaw cue.** At `PostLandingThawWarningFrames` (30) before the thaw:
  - `AudioManager.PlayCriticalCue(ChronalRewindManager.ThawCueStream)` plays; the stream is null under the silent placeholder kit.
  - The reused `hud_time_freeze_thaw` string is posted over the hero via `EnvironmentNotice`.
  - Neither is gated on Reduced Temporal Effects. Counter: `ThawWarningsIssued`.
- **Music duck.** `CreateLandedHoldPayload` carries -12 dB.
  - `RewindPresentationOverlay` holds the duck on `Landed` and releases it on `Thawed`.
- **Boss ordering.** At the thaw, a boss still in `BossState.HistoricalRecovery` resumes its world-wide suspension for its remaining beat. It was frozen through the rewind and the hold.

### M17
- `RewindPresentationPayload.TransmissionLineKey` (additive), chosen by `ChronalRewindManager.CollapseTransmissionKeyFor(actIII, cause)`:
  - `""` in Act III (`StoryManager.IsActIIILevel`).
  - The era-lost key for a timer collapse.
  - `collapse_transmission_line` otherwise.
- `RewindPresentationOverlay.TransmissionLine` is hidden for an empty key.
- The timer collapse's separate `EnvironmentNotice` post was removed: the line now rides the beat.
- `dialogue_collapse_sarah_era_lost` value → `"Sarah: The era's slipping away — pulling you out!"`. The key name is unchanged and no new keys were added.

### GAP-06
- `ChronalRewindManager` subscribes to `EventBus.OnBossHistoricalRecovery` and suspends the world through the Time Freeze path for exactly the boss's own beat. The recovering boss is excluded, since its beat is the clock.
- The hero is suspended through `PlayerController.SetWorldSuspended(bool)`: the process mode is disabled, so velocity, action progress and every hero clock are preserved.
- The Time Freeze cooldown is held.
- `IsBossSuspensionActive` / `IsHoldingWorld`.
- Level 15 authors `Level15Controller.HistoricalRecoveryAnchor` at `HistoricalRecoveryAnchorPosition` (10400, FloorY − 50), on the rotunda floor, and binds it to the First Unbound.

### GAP-11
- `Level10Controller.TickAudience` returns early while `Player.TimeFrozen || Player.IsRecoveryWorldHeld`. While frozen:
  - no idle build-up;
  - no telegraph countdown;
  - no throw;
  - no catch-up. The position baseline follows the hero.

## What did not ship / open
- **Sealing anchor.** It does not exist yet (`DEFER-SEALING-ANCHOR`, W8), so "sealing waits for the thaw" has nothing to gate.
- **Pressure plates.** `PressurePlate` is not an `IStoryTimeFreezable`, so a weighted plate still reacts during the hold. This is the same as under Time Freeze today.
- **Thaw cue and HUD.** The thaw cue audio is a null placeholder stream (Package 10), and there is no dedicated HUD widget for the hold: the warning reuses the Time Freeze string as a world-space notice.
- **`PostLandingThawWarningFrames` = 30 is provisional.** The design says "just before" and gives no number.
- **Integrity during the GAP-06 beat.** It is not paused explicitly. It is already locked at PreBoss in Level 15, which is the only level with a T01b boss.
- **Pooled projectiles.** A pooled enemy projectile whose pool release is deferred inside a physics flush is not parked on the death-rewind path; it is cleared instead, which avoids reviving pooled nodes at the thaw.
- Nobody has played any of this.
