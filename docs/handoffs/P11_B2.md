### Reconciliation with B1/B3 (orchestrator, 2026-09-13)

Both upstream fixes are the orchestrator's at merge; B2 changed nothing in A12's files and its
variants are already consistent with B1's approach.

- **`LegacyKitGate.BuildStrikeSurface` gains the `EnemyHurtbox` layer.** B2's two construct gates —
  **Cleopatra's Serpent Nest** and **Tesla's Tesla Coil** — are declared `Strike` precisely so they
  become natively resolvable the moment that layer lands (both constructs already emit
  `AttackID = <ability ID>` with `AttackerIndex = 0` against `EnemyHurtbox`-layer hurtboxes, and
  `EnvironmentHurtboxAdapter` is a `Hurtbox`). **Their `LegacyCastGateWatcher` instances become
  redundant then and should be removed** with the rest of the consolidation — drop
  `SerpentNestWatcher` / `CoilWatcher` and the `TheCastRecognisedGateHasAWatcherBoundToItsOwnAbility`
  case from those two suites.
- **Shakespeare's The Tempest is *not* covered by that fix.** It lifts the caster and pushes
  `CharacterBody2D` targets directly; it raises no hitbox and calls `TakeHit` on nothing, so no
  hurtbox layer can observe it. That gate still needs `LegacyGateMode.Cast = 4` — fold
  `LegacyCastGateWatcher`'s poll into `LegacyKitGate` and keep the Shakespeare suite's watcher case,
  retargeted at the enum member. Never loosen `Strike`/`Zone` to cover it.
- **`ParSeconds` hiding** — corroborated by B1 and B3; fixed upstream (see the table above).
