# KL-SZONE handoff (Phase B of the lingering-kit pass, 2026-10-31)

Branch `kl/szone` from `kl/integ` (Phase A). Story zones only; nothing under `scripts/FighterSim/` was touched.

## What shipped

- **Rift** (`EinsteinRelativityRift.cs`). The collapse now also fires when the owner's landed `combo_3` hits a target inside the open Rift: it subscribes to `PlayerController.OwnerHitLanded` while the Rift is open (unsubscribed on collapse, expiry and `_ExitTree`), and runs the collapse directly, or deferred when raised inside a physics callback. The collapse hit now deals `LingeringKitRules.RiftCollapseDamage` (8) through `StoryAbilityDamage`, carries `CountsAsCombo`, and Momentum Detonation (0.2 pip) is paid once per collapse after the 6-frame pull, only if some body took landed damage. `TryCollapse` gained optional `sourceCast` / `sourceSerial`; `EinsteinEmc2Blast.Detonate` passes itself, and the collapse landing calls `sourceCast.ConfirmAbilityHit` so D3 arms for that cast (a finisher collapse has no cast and pays Momentum only). The caught `PlayerController` manual launch now runs only when the hit dealt nothing (damage now takes the normal path). The 2.5 u radius and the 1.2 / 1.75 s refresh-and-linger already worked from Phase A; tests pin them.
- **Vortex** (`CleopatraSandstormVortex` in `CleopatraAbilities.cs`). Pull is `LingeringKitRules.VortexPullUnitsPerSecondStory` (7 u/s, 420 px/s). `IsPullable(body)` exempts a hero in roll travel or an air dodge, a knockback-immune boss, and a dormant encounter-lock member. The per-tick slow is gone from the tick payload; the 1.2 / 1.75 s slow (from the `.tres`) lands once when a body leaves the area (diffed each frame against the previous occupants) and on everyone still inside when the Vortex ends. The final launch tick has `CountsAsCombo` and pays Detonation once.
- **Golden Ratio** (`LeonardoGoldenRatio` in `LeonardoAbilities.cs`). Every tick pulls each caught target `GoldenRatioPullPercent` (50 %) of the remaining distance, capped at 1.5 u, through `PullThroughCollision` (`PullStepPixels` is public for tests). Ticks carry `CountsAsCombo`; HoldCast (0.05) is paid once per cast on the first landed tick. Tick 1 keeps its normal hitstun. **Master Stroke's old 30 px pull is removed** (the base pull subsumes it; the perk keeps its +15 % damage). The pull reuses `CleopatraSandstormVortex.IsPullable`, so a roll, a knockback-immune boss and a dormant member are not drawn in.
- **Yorick**: no code change; the burst already applies `.tres` status (now 1.2 / 1.75 s). Pinned by a test.
- **Wrath of the Nile kept at 78**: `cleopatra/ultimate.tres` Venom `StatusDuration` 3.0 to 2.0 (3 x 2.0 x 2 s = 12), intensity unchanged. The sim reads the same `.tres`: **KL-SIM's sim Ultimate tests will see the 2 s duration** (Venom ticks twice, 12 total). Pins restored to 78 / 12: `UltimateActivationContentTests` (row), `CleopatraUltimateContentTests` (duration 2, comments), `CleopatraUltimateTests` (Determinism: `VenomTotal` 12, fixture `StatusDuration` 2 s, test renamed `StormLandsSeventyEightCountingTheFinaleVenom`).

## Bug found on the way
`IStatusEffectTarget` is implemented by `PlayerController` only; `EnemyController` and `BossController` are not. The Rift's per-frame `RefreshLinger` therefore refreshed the slow on heroes only, and mobs got it from the zone's 0.5 s ticks. New `FTT.Combat.LingeringZoneStatus` (`FindStatusNode`, `TryApply`) names the mob and the boss, and the Rift's refresh and the Vortex's exit slow use it. Bosses go through their own `ApplyStatusEffect`, so Phase A's capped boss slow applies.
