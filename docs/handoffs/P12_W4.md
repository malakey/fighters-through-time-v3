## 2. What shipped, per item

- **Tesla Lightning Blink (both modes).** `tesla/movement.tres`: `StartupFrames 6`, `ActiveFrames 12`, `RecoveryFrames 10`, `DistanceMoved 180` (3.0 u), `MovementSpeed 900`; `MovementDuration 0.2` and `CooldownDuration 5.0` unchanged. The design's "1 second as the hard cap on the whole action" is the action length (28 f), not the cooldown, so the 5 s cooldown stands. Story (`TeslaLightningBlink`): hovers through startup, translates over `ActiveFrames`, zero momentum at recovery; **Long Blink is now wired** (`LongBlinkPerkKey = "long_blink"`, +30 px). Sim: `ApplyMovement`'s Blink branch starts the kit phase instead of teleporting; startup hovers (no gravity), the translation flies straight at `distance / 12 frames` (diagonals normalised), recovery bleeds x under gravity. Hits cancel it through the existing universal-movement cancel sites. **Projectile pass-through during the translation only**, derived from the phase: `FighterProjectileSystem` skips a target in `BlinkTravel` (the shot is not consumed).
- **Mozart Extra Note (Story only).** `MozartSonataDrift`: `MaxCharges` 2 with the node, `AvailableCharges = (timer idle ? 1 : 0) + banked`; a cast while the recharge runs spends a banked charge without restarting it; a completed recharge with the stock short banks the charge and restarts the timer (`RearmCooldown`) — one at a time; landing on one of his own staffs halves the **remaining** recharge, **once per platform** (`SonataPlatformNode.TryConsumeLandingRefund`, reset on spawn/despawn). The staff refund is also the baseline kit rule and applies without the node. HUD: the restarted recharge raises the ordinary `CooldownStarted` event, so the cooldown radial follows it; **there is no charge-count widget** on the Story HUD (not added).
- **Pocahontas Second Glide.** The re-entry now neither checks nor restarts the movement cooldown (`TryExecute(armCooldown: false)`).
- **Pocahontas glide attack (Story).** `ProcessUsingMovementAbility` lets a basic attack start while `PocahontasBreezeGlide.AllowsBasicAttackMidGlide`; the glide keeps steering through the swing and reclaims `UsingMovementAbility` when the swing ends in the air; any other state ends the glide. The sim's glide is a float window an aerial swing never cancels, so it already met the rule.
- **Spirit Strike (both modes).** `pocahontas/special_1.tres` `ActiveFrames 21 → 15`. Story: 45° up-forward carry at 720 px/s over the active window; the eagle hitbox rides `EagleOffsetPixels(frame)` ahead of her and dives. Sim: Pocahontas's Special 1 is **no longer a range-gated melee intent** — `FighterAbilityEntitySystem` arms the cooldown and starts `SpiritStartup` on every legal press (target in range or not); `ResolveSpiritEagles` strikes once per cast (`UniversalMovementDirection` ±2 marks "struck") for the authored Special 1 damage/knockback/status as a Special-class direct hit with the sim's 18-frame special hitstun.
- **GAP-10b.** `CpuRecoveryProfile.MobilitySpecialFor` is now public and returns `SpecialOne` for Pocahontas, `None` for the other eight. `RecoveryMobilitySpecialPercent` is live for her on Medium (85) / Hard (100); Easy never casts a Special.
- **Cleopatra sand decoy (GAP-10c), Story.** `SandDecoyNode` (one reusable node under `CleopatraDesertMirage`, no per-cast churn): every accepted Mirage arms a 60-frame decoy at the cast origin; `EnemyController` aims its chase and attacks at a live decoy of its target (`TargetAimPosition`); the decoy has a hurtbox on the owner's hurtbox layer so a hit aimed at it lands on the sand. **Royal Aegis** grants the decoy its own shield (same 10 %-max-HP capacity, own depletion, own 8 s lifetime, grant ID = the cast ID, a repeat refused) as a **separate recipient** — closing Package 11 A1b's recorded D02a deviation. A hit its shield cannot absorb disperses it. Freeze-safe (both latches, `persistent_construct` group). **Bosses do not aim at it** (`BossController` untouched). Fighter: not implemented (`DEFER-FIGHTER-SAND-DECOY`).
- **GAP-10a.** The sim's Lorentz zone (type 52) no longer adds chain damage on Static Charge. After a pulse tick that **landed**, `TryResolveLorentzChain` consumes this Tesla's Conductive mark through `FighterConductiveRules.TryConsumeChain(mark, owner, zone.EntityID + 1)` — once per pulse per target, replay-safe — and deals `CoilArcDamage (5) × live coils` (Special class, no Rally, no hitstop, 6-frame hitstun). Another Tesla's mark, Static Charge alone, a blocked pulse or no coil never chains; with no coil the mark is left to expire. **Story parity:** `TeslaLorentzPulse` now chains only when the pulse dealt damage and a coil stands, and consumes the mark (`ClearConductiveMark`).
- **M03 cleanup.** `LeonardoTurretNode.BaseBoltLimit` 3 → 4; the "third bolt" / "instead of 3" comments now say four.
- **VERIFY-VORTEX-RECLAIM.** Closed: Vortex ticks are stamped `Tick` and credit off the payload.
- **Kit payloads (W3 follow-up).** 19 ability-script payload sites plus the four construct nodes and Tornado Lift now stamp through `WithAbilityContract`/`Stamp`. Behaviour notes: Einstein's Relativity Rift and Shakespeare's Tempest (authored `Delivery = Tick`) no longer reclaim Rally in Story — D03g; Tesla's chain arcs are stamped `Construct`; **the Cataclysm coil detonation is now Ultimate-origin (D03h, no meter)** — it used to credit meter.
- **GAP-14.** Every hit of a Nexus-authorized Ultimate is `PuzzleOnly` and rejected at `Hurtbox.TakeHit`; the puzzle target resolves through `NexusResonanceSource.ResolvePuzzleTarget` exactly as before. Residual: positional pulls (`VERIFY-NEXUS-DISPLACEMENT`).
- **Leonardo mid-glide bolt (Story).** During the Ornithopter glide a basic-attack press commands a deployed turret to fire its next bolt now (`LeonardoTurretNode.TryCommandBolt`, own budget, own targeting), once per flight; a refused command spends nothing. Not in the sim (`DEFER-FIGHTER-KIT-RULES`).
