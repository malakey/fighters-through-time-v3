## 1. Shipped

### The standalone route (Main Menu → picker → drill list → drill scene)

- **`MainMenu`**: a `CalibrationDrillsButton` authored into
  `RootScreen/Center/Panel/Layout` **between `FighterButton` and `SettingsButton`** (the design's
  option order), retro `TemporalGlassButton` variation, raw key `menu_calibration_drills`. Available
  on a **first boot with no Story save** and never gated on one. The handler is split into
  `MainMenu.ArmCalibrationRoute()` (session write, returns the destination) plus the `LoadScene` call.
- **`scripts/UI/CalibrationDrillPicker.cs` + `scenes/ui/CalibrationDrillPicker.tscn`** — one player,
  one token. All nine characters on the Fighter select's tile treatment (portrait layer over a name
  layer), a live name/stat preview driven by focus **or** hover, and the shared
  `MoveListButton` / `SystemsCardButton` / `BackButton` footer. **No** Ready gate, countdown,
  CPU-difficulty row, local-human toggle or stage phase — asserted by name-absence, not by eye.
- **`scripts/UI/CalibrationDrillList.cs` + `scenes/ui/CalibrationDrillList.tscn`** — the six rows are
  built from `CalibrationDrillCatalog` (the catalog is the single source of drill identity; six
  authored buttons would be a second copy of the order), numbered, each showing its objective as the
  one-line lesson, over an authored title / hint / character line / Back / Exit Calibration frame.
- **`scenes/ui/HolodeckDrill.tscn` + `scripts/Combat/CalibrationDrillRunner.cs`** — a
  `FighterSimulationDriver`-backed sandbox on the **Sealed** `florence_workshop` geometry with a
  scripted dummy on player 1 through `InputManager.SetInputSource`, a top objective panel and a
  pass/fail result card offering **Retry / Next Drill / Drill List / Exit Calibration**.

### The retained hub entry

`HolodeckConsolePanel` **keeps** its CPU practice bout and gains a `CalibrationDrillsButton` beside
it. The hub route **skips the picker** (a campaign save locks its character), records the hub as the
Exit Calibration destination, and changes nothing else in the session — slot, difficulty, campaign
level, dust, rewinds and the player's saved Fighter match settings all survive the detour.

### Isolation (the through-line)

- Both drill fighters are built `applyStoryProgression: false` → **normalized Fighter kits**: no
  Resonance stats, no `StoryAbilityPerks`, no Legacy Unlock gate, no Time Freeze, no death rewinds.
- Nothing on either route calls `SaveManager`, writes a slot, or moves `ActiveSaveSlot`.
- The drill index lives only in `SessionData` and is never persisted, so **quitting or relaunching
  can never resume a drill as a Story attempt**.
- **No campaign exit fee**: A3 already deleted `SessionExitGuard.ApplyAbnormalExitFee` (ruling 2.B),
  and the route additionally never writes, clears or re-slots the session marker.
- The drill sandbox builds its own `MatchSettings` (Stock, 9 stocks, 3600 s, **items off, hazards
  off**) rather than reading or writing the player's saved Fighter rules.

### The six drills, as shipped

Catalog order is the design's order and the "Next Drill" order.

| # | `DrillID` | Name (`*_name`) | Dummy script | Pass condition (observable) | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `drill_block_string` | Hold the Line | walks in, three-hit string on a loop | two non-shatter blocked hits — the rising edge of `ShieldStunFrames`, twice | three connected hits with nothing blocked |
| 2 | `drill_landing_tech` | Catch the Ground | same string (its finisher launches) | `TechLockoutFrames` rises from 0 — nothing but a successful landing tech arms it | 3 tumbles that end grounded with no tech |
| 3 | `drill_launch_di` | Steer the Launch | same string | a direction held while `PendingLaunchActive` is set, at the frame it resolves | 3 launches resolved with nothing held |
| 4 | `drill_grab_throw` | Break the Guard | stands in range holding Block, never drops it | `GrabPhase` reaches 5 (throw animation) — the throw, not the seize | the 60 s attempt budget |
| 5 | `drill_echo_step` | Fold the Recovery | idle bag, no retaliation | **beat 1** meter ≥ `BasicComboRules.EchoStepMeterCost`; **beat 2** `EchoStepWindupFrames` rises from 0 | the attempt budget |
| 6 | `drill_rally_reclaim` | Reclaim the Echo | walks in, lands exactly one hit, then stands still | student HP rises above its post-hit floor while an Echo pool is live (only a reclaim raises HP) | 3 pools drained without an answer |

Every drill carries a localized name, objective, pass coaching and fail coaching; drill 5 carries a
second objective line for its second beat. Attempt budget is 60 s (3600 frames) and the miss
tolerance is 3, both in `CalibrationDrillCatalog`. **The final drill hides Next Drill** (F18).

| **A drill-specific HUD** | Deliberate | The driver's production `FighterHUD` is attached as on any stage; it already shows HP, meter and shield pips, which is exactly what the lessons are about. |
