## 1. Shipped

### F01 — Timeline Integrity is the level timer

- **`scripts/Core/TimelineIntegrityRules.cs` rewritten.** The whole V7.1/V7.3 siphon model is
  deleted: `MaxSiphonSharePercent`, `SiphonGraceSeconds`, `ExtractorDestroyRestorePercent`,
  `GenericSecretRestorePercent`, `SpecialSecretRestorePercent`, the old
  `DrainPerSecond(Difficulty)`, and the entire `ChronalRatingRules` class. Tier lines moved
  90/70 → **50/20**; `EndingThresholdPercent` 85 → **50**.
- **New drain model.** `DifficultyTimeMultiplier` (Easy 2.0 / Normal 1.5 / Hard 1.2),
  `ExtractorDrainWeight = 0.2f`, `SecretDrainReduction = 0.1f`, `MinimumRawFactor = 0.9f`,
  `InitialDrainFactor(starting)`, `CurrentDrainFactor(living, secretFound)`,
  `DrainPerSecond(par, difficulty, living, starting, secretFound)`,
  `AllAliveDrainPerSecond(par, difficulty)`.
- **The clock lives on `StoryManager`** and ticks in its own `_PhysicsProcess` (see §4 for the API).
  Drain is global from level load — no engagement, no room entry, no grace. Denominator fixed at
  load from the authored population, never recalculated from survivors. Nothing adds a point back.
  Clamps at zero.
- **`ChronalExtractor`** lost its whole drain block, `SiphonEngageDistancePixels`, `SiphonEngaged`,
  `SiphonGraceRemaining` and `DrainedSharePercent`. `OnDestroyed` now calls
  `StoryManager.NotifyExtractorDestroyed()` (rate down, gauge unmoved) and posts the E01 notice.
  `RecordExtractorDestroyed` and `IStoryRewindSimulation` are **kept**.
- **`SecretCache`** no longer restores integrity; `IsSpecialSecret` is retained as authoring metadata
  only. `StoryManager.RestoreTimelineIntegrity` is **deleted** along with both call sites.
- **Collapse at zero on every difficulty.** `StoryManager.FireIntegrityCollapse` →
  `ChronalRewindManager.BeginTimerCollapse()` → the ordinary fracture beat →
  `BeginTimelineCollapse(id, TimelineCollapseCause.Timer)`. Fires exactly once.
- **E01 drain feedback:** `integrity_drain_slowed`, posted once on a real first destruction while the
  clock runs, via the new `FTT.Environment.EnvironmentNotice`. No time-gained figure, no percentage,
  no numeric readout. Untimed levels and a locked PreBoss clock post nothing.

### F12 — authored checkpoint roles

- New `FTT.Environment.CheckpointRole` enum (`Entry = 0 | Middle = 1 | PreBoss = 2`), declared in
  `LevelManager.cs`. `CheckpointTrigger` gained `[Export] CheckpointRole Role` and
  `[Export] bool Inert`.
- **`BuildCheckpoint(x, y, id, CheckpointRole role)` — the `role` parameter is required.** All 42
  call sites across `Level02Controller`–`Level15Controller` were updated (Entry / Middle / PreBoss in
  authored order). No suffix inference anywhere.
- PreBoss activation calls `LockIntegrityAtPreBoss()` (freeze + bank final Integrity) before the
  committed half of the checkpoint event pair; Entry and Middle call `BankCheckpointIntegrity()`.
- Hard's middle fracture is **inert in Acts I–II only** (`(int)Level >= CampaignLevel.ChronalVoid`
  is Act III). An inert anchor activates nothing, anchors no respawn, registers no F11 budget, and
  reads dead grey.
- ID→role alias map on `StoryManager`; an unregistered ID resolves to **Entry** (safe — Entry can
  never lock the boss clock). Registering a PreBoss role for an already-activated checkpoint
  **re-locks** the gauge, which is how a resume past the strike keeps its lock.

### F11 — recovery minima

`RequiredRecovery` / `RecoveryMinimum` / `TimerRecoveryIntegrity` on `TimelineIntegrityRules`,
computed in `double` with a 1e-6 rounding epsilon. **A budget above 100 throws** — never clamps.
`StoryManager.ResolveTimerRecoveryIntegrity` runs only for `TimelineCollapseCause.Timer`, catches an
invalid budget with `GD.PushError` and falls back to the banked gauge. The resolved value is written
into `LevelIntegrityPercent` **and** `CheckpointIntegrityPercent` before the save, so an interrupted
recovery loads the already-resolved result and never grants twice.

### Collapse Tremor

- New `scripts/Environment/CollapseTremorController.cs` (with `CollapseTremorRules`) and
  `scripts/Environment/FallingDebris.cs`, plus `scenes/vfx/falling_debris.tscn`.
- Stage 1 < 20 %: continuous shake 0.1, jolt 0.3 × 0.4 s every 6–8 s, debris ~every 4 s.
  Stage 2 < 10 %: jolts every 3–4 s, debris ~every 2 s.
- Debris: 0.75 s telegraph, **5 % of max HP**, small knockback, blockable as a Basic-class hit,
  routed through `PlayerController.ApplyEnvironmentalDamage(damage, appliesHitstop: false)`, never
  more than **2** airborne, never inside a Restoration Font radius or on an activated fracture,
  **enemies unaffected**. Pooled (`falling_debris`), every field reset in `OnSpawn`/`OnDespawn`.
- `CrumblingPlatform` gained `[Export] bool FractureEligible` (opt-in) plus
  `StoryLevelControllerBase.BuildFracturePlatform(...)` with a 5 s respawn.
- Attached by **`StoryLevelControllerBase`** on timed levels only — Tutorial and Florence do not
  extend it, so they opt out with no special case.
- Stops whenever the clock pauses; retires permanently at the PreBoss lock. First crossing plays a
  one-per-level non-blocking Sarah bark. All shake goes through `CameraShake`, so the Settings
  screen-shake scale (0 = off) already applies and Tremor **timing** is never reduced.

## 2. Not delivered, and why

1. **No authored kill boundaries.** A sweep of all sixteen campaign controllers found no lethal
   opening: every gap is explicitly documented as non-lethal (L04 boss pit "deliberately not a
   hazard", L05 flood "never becomes a kill floor", L07 sea "not a rising water zone and not a kill
   plane" — it rescues the player onto the deck they launched from, L06 lava "never a kill",
   L12/L13/L14/L15 all state they have no pits; L10's "Yard" is a floored recess). The §3 A3 scope
   ruling excludes exactly this case. Recorded as **`VERIFY-STORY-PITS`** in
   `docs/design-contracts/DESIGN_BUILD_DEVIATIONS.md`. The chokepoint, the type and the authoring
   helper all ship, so adding a boundary later is content work.
2. **The Tremor's decorative half is not implemented** — crack lines across background layers, inward
   palette desaturation, prop time-ghosting, the vignette pulse. A8 owns the shared crack-glow
   overlay and the C01a Reduced Temporal Effects preset; A3 publishes `OnCollapseTremorChanged` for
   it. The Tremor's *gameplay* is complete.
3. **Tremor audio is not wired** (rumble bed, stone groan, music-bus detune, HUD clock tick). Under
   the standing silent-placeholder rule there is nothing to add until Package 10 supplies stems.
4. **`DustBonusPercent` is still authored and unapplied** — A10 applies it.
5. **The Act III collapse branch is a hook, not an implementation** —
   `StoryManager.ActIIICollapseOverride`, left `null`. A3b fills it with Anchor Snap / Smothered.
6. **Par values and F11 route budgets are provisional, not measured** — `VERIFY-PAR-SECONDS`.
