## 1. Shipped

### The V7.5 Legacy Unlock Schedule
- **`scripts/Core/LegacyUnlockSchedule.cs` (new, engine-free).** Milestone table — Movement Ability
  after Level 1, Special 1 after Level 2, Special 2 after Level 3, Ultimate **cast** after Level 4.
  Milestone-gated, never dust-gated; the 975-dust grid economy is untouched. Everything else (basics,
  Block, Rally, the death rewind, the meter and Defy History) is never gated.
- **Save (additive, plan §2.6):** `StorySaveData.UnlockedLegacyAbilities`
  (`Dictionary<string, List<string>>`, per character, keys `movement`/`special1`/`special2`/`ultimate`)
  plus its `Normalize()` entry. Preserved on Restart Level.
- **The grant:** `StoryManager.OnLevelComplete` → `GrantLegacyUnlockMilestone(levelID)` inside the
  completion transaction, before `RecordLevelResultToSave` so the same save write persists it.
  Idempotent; backfills from `CompletedLevels`.
- **The gate:** `PlayerController.IsAbilityUnlocked(AbilitySlot)` consulted at the four cast sites
  (`CheckSpecialInput` × 2, `CheckUltimateInput`, `CheckMovementAbilityInput`). Installed by
  `CharacterFactory` on the `applyStoryProgression: true` path only.
- **Slot lock states:** `EventBus.OnAbilitySlotLockChanged(AbilitySlotLockPayload{Slot, State})` with
  `Dormant | Suppressed | Clear`. A5 publishes `Dormant`/`Clear`; **A1 publishes `Suppressed`**;
  **A8 renders**. Locked slots are shown, never hidden.
- **Resonance Restored beat:** `LevelResultsPanel` renders `results_resonance_restored` naming the
  restored ability's authored `DisplayNameKey`. The Ultimate's beat is deferred to Level 4A entry.
- **Move List Dormant badges:** `MoveListScreen.Populate(characterID, MoveListMode)`; a Story row for
  an unrestored slot shows `movelist_dormant_until` and suppresses `movelist_ability_stats`. The Story
  pause opens `MoveListMode.Story`; the Fighter pause and character select keep the full kit.

### The rebuilt Level 0 calibration (V7.6)
New step order: `BasicHits → RallyReclaim → Block → **Grab** → **HitstunDI** → **LandingTech** →
**MeterAndDefy** → UseRewind → UseManualRewind → Done`. `UseSpecial` and `UseUltimate` deleted.
- **Grab Calibration** — a frozen standard-tier `hologram_drone` holds a guard that restores its HP
  on every swing (absorbed, re-prompted) until the player grabs and throws it; the triangle is stated
  both ways in `tutorial_step_grab_complete`.
- **Hitstun Agency Calibration** — one scripted launch with a 36-frame tutorial-only hitstop for the
  ±15° DI read (the beat proceeds with no input held), then a mandatory landing tech that repeats
  until it lands, with a 24-frame "slowed approach" freeze on the first attempt. The launch is free:
  no HP, no Rally accounting, no meter, no rewind charge.
- **Meter & Defy History** — fills the meter, publishes the F13 seal `Ready` (lit), lands a scripted
  lethal-tagged hit through `ApplyEnvironmentalDamage`, publishes `Spent` (broken).
- **Part 3** — the movement-ability corridor is deleted; traversal is double jump → roll on base jump
  reach.

### Dialogue mechanisms (plan §2.10, verbatim)
- `DialogueSequenceData.HeroConditionCharacterID` + the `<baseID>@<heroID>` convention, with
  `VariantID` / `IsVariantID` / `BaseIDOf` / `BaseDialogueID` helpers.
- `DialogueSetData.FindSequence(baseID, activeHeroID)` — prefers the hero variant, falls back to the
  base, **never** to another hero's variant. `DialogueManager.FindSequence` and every
  `StartSequence` path route through it, selecting on the **saved** hero ID.
- `FTT.Core.CampaignCaptiveRoster` — manifest-backed (`ContentManifest.ForCategory(Character)`), never
  a literal roster. `For(heroID)`, `NamedExamplesFor(heroID)`, `SpokenNameKey(id)`. Authored priority
  Leonardo → Cleopatra → Tesla, intersected with the manifest.
- `{CaptiveName1}` / `{CaptiveName2}` substitution at render time, resolving through the new
  `captive_name_*` key family with a `character_*_name` fallback.
- **Hold-to-skip for every sequence** — the `save.IsCompleted` + already-seen gate is gone.
- **First-viewing confirm** on the shared `ConfirmModal`, keyed on the new global
  `GlobalSaveData.SeenDialogueIDs` (additive). A sequence seen on any slot skips with no prompt.
- **Idempotent effect ledger** — `ResolveDialogueEffects(sequenceID)` split out of `EndSequence()`,
  called from both the watched and confirmed-skip paths, guarded by the per-slot
  `StorySaveData.ViewedDialogueIDs`. `LastViewedDialogueID` is demoted to a presentation cursor.
