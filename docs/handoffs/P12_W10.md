# Package 12 W10 — Presentation hooks

Branch `p12/W10`. Items: H03, H04, M15, the glow stack order, and the `StatusController.ClearSlot` payload.
Presentation only: **nothing under `scripts/FighterSim/` that the simulation runs changed** — the only
FighterSim file touched is `FighterSimulationDriver.cs`, and only in its read-only presentation region. No
deterministic hash moves. No Klotho component ID was allocated. No save field was added.

## What shipped

### Glow stack order (HUD_CONTRACT "Ownership and effects")
- `GlowLayer` is now `SlotIndicator = 0, DamageStatus = 1, ControlStatus = 2, HyperArmor = 3, SpawnInvulnerability = 4`, so the resolved order is **spawn protection > armor > control status > damage status**. The ordinals are presentation-only and never serialized.
- `GlowLayer.Status = 5` survives as an alias meaning "both status layers":
  - `GlowStateStack.Clear(Status)` clears both.
  - `IsActive(Status)` is true when either is active.
  - Nothing pushes it, which keeps `EnemyController` and `BossController` (W9's files) compiling and correct without an edit.
- `GlowPalette.Status(type)` returns the type's own layer, through the new `GlowLayers.ForStatus` / `ForSlot` helpers.
- `GlowPresentationController`:
  - `SetStatusSlot(StatusSlot, type)` paints exactly one layer; `None` clears it.
  - `SetStatusSlots(control, damage)` sets both.
  - `StatusOn(slot)` is a test seam.
  - `SetStatus(type)` keeps its single-glow meaning for enemies and bosses: it clears both layers, then paints the type on its own layer.
  - The Venom lerp reads the damage layer.
- `FighterSimulationDriver` paints both authoritative slots (`runtime.StatusType` / `DamageStatusType`) instead of the single `PresentedStatusType` collapse.

### `StatusController.ClearSlot` — per-slot event payload
- `StatusEffectPayload` gained two additive fields:
  - `Slot` (`FTT.Combat.StatusSlot`) and `SlotScoped` (bool).
  - Helpers: `ResolveSlot()` and `ClearsAllSlots`. `ClearsAllSlots` is the legacy un-scoped `None` clear.
- `StatusController` now sets both fields on every Applied and Cleared event.
  - A Cleared event carries the **type that left** and its slot.
  - The re-announce-the-survivor Applied event is **deleted**.
  - An in-slot replacement raises only the new Applied, as before.
- Consumers:
  - `StoryHUD` clears the named slot through the new `HudAbilityIndicatorModel.ClearStatusSlot(bool damageSlot)`.
  - `GlowPresentationController` clears the named layer.
  - Legacy un-scoped payloads keep their old reading.
- The Fighter HUD reads sim state directly and was already correct; it is unchanged.

### M15 — Beacon Anchors HUD
- `scenes/ui/StoryHUD.tscn`: `BeaconAnchors` now sits **immediately right of `RewindCounter`** and ahead of `TimeFreezeIndicator`.
  - The indicator stays its own sibling node, never attached to the rewind counter (F24).
  - `BeaconIcon` is a 24 × 24 `Control` running the new `scripts/UI/BeaconAnchorIcon.cs` (placeholder vector beacon).
  - At zero charges it becomes a hollow outline with a steady `BeaconAnchorIcon.LastStandTint` (`UIPalette.BossRed`), with no pulse.
- New `scripts/UI/AnchorPip.cs`: an 8 × 8 gold diamond. Its states are `Filled | Cracking | Spent`.
  - `Crack()` plays a 0.6 s crack-and-fade (`CrackSeconds`) to a faint spent outline.
  - `SetFilled` redraws without animation.
- `StoryHUD.OnAnchorChargesChanged`:
  - A drop at unchanged capacity is an **Anchor Snap** and cracks the spent pip.
  - Bind, load and a restart refill redraw without the beat.
  - The readout's `AccessibilityName` (and tooltip) is `hud_anchor_charges_accessible` = "Anchor charges: {0}".
- New test seams: `AnchorPips`, `BeaconLastStand`, `AnchorChargesAccessibleText`, `StoryHUD.AnchorChargesAccessibleKey`.

### H04 — core mechanic cues
- **One catalog.** `resources/Audio/core_mechanic_cues.tres` (`CoreMechanicCueCatalog` + `CoreMechanicCueEntry`; IDs in `CoreMechanicCueIDs`) has 29 rows.
  - Each row carries `CueID`, `Bus`, `Critical`, `Stream` and a `DesignNote`.
  - The rows cover Time Freeze activate/drone/thaw-warning/thaw-release, the Post-Landing thaw, the Defy toll and seal shatter, and Echo Step windup/arrival/reject.
  - They also cover Rally reclaim, grab catch/whiff, throw heave/impact, landing tech, knockdown thud, the Integrity tick and the 00:10 pip and final pip.
  - The rest are Ultimate ready, the KO stinger (normal and decisive), the victory fanfare, the Draw sting, the N01 seal charge/lock, and the two stage-hazard fallbacks.
- **Critical flags follow the GDD.** Time Freeze (all four), the Post-Landing thaw, Defy, the Integrity tick, both pips, both KO stingers and the hazard fallbacks are Critical.
  - Critical rows always route through `AudioManager.PlayCriticalCue`. Every other row plays on its authored bus.
- **Silent placeholder streams.** The existing silent kit is reused: `placeholder_sfx_*.tres` and the `RENDER_SILENT` `.ogg` cues. No audible tone was added.
- **Victory fanfares respect roster growth.** There is a shared `victory_fanfare` row plus an optional per-hero `victory_fanfare_<heroID>` override, resolved by convention through `VictoryFanfareFor`. The cast is never enumerated.
- **`StageAudioSet` gained `HazardWarningCue` / `HazardImpactCue`.** All ten Fighter stage sets author both slots on the silent hazard placeholder, and they always play Critical.
- **Presenter.** `CoreMechanicCuePresenter` (`AudioManager.CoreCues`, node `CoreMechanicCues`, `Pausable`) subscribes to existing `EventBus` events:
  - `OnTimeFreezeStateChanged`: activate and drone; the thaw warning timed to the final 0.5 s; the release at thaw.
  - `OnDefySealChanged`: Ready→Spent plays the toll and the shatter.
  - `OnUltimateMeterChanged`: once per fill.
  - `OnRallyEchoChanged`: pitched by the pool left.
  - `OnCollapseTremorChanged`: the Integrity tick at 1 s below 20 % and 0.5 s below 10 %.
  - `OnPlayerDied`, hero slot 0 only: the KO stinger.
  - It seeds `ChronalRewindManager.ThawCueStream` (W1's Post-Landing thaw warning) from the catalog.
  - `AudioManager.OnSceneTransitionStarted` resets its transient state.
- **Fighter cues stay driver-side.** A new presentation region in `FighterSimulationDriver` edge-detects from component state:
  - grab catch/whiff, throw heave/impact, and Echo Step windup, plus arrival vs a completion-time cancel (by travel distance);
  - landing tech, and a tumble ending grounded without a tech (the knockdown thud);
  - Rally reclaim (the echo pool falls while HP rises), and Ultimate ready;
  - the stage hazard warning/impact from each hazard entity's phase edge (`PlayStageHazard`);
  - the 00:10 pips and the higher final pip.
  - The KO stinger (stock loss), the decisive stinger (the match-ending HitFreeze), the winner's fanfare and the Draw sting now go through the catalog. The private `PlayCue` path is deleted.
  - Nothing is written to sim state.

## Deviations
1. **HUD order.** The GDD §17 HUD tree lists `TimeFreezeIndicator` between `RewindCounter` and `BeaconAnchors`, while HUD_CONTRACT M15 says the Beacon sits "immediately right of the rewind hourglass". The contract is the more specific adopted resolution, so the order is Rewind → Beacon → Time Freeze.
2. **Echo Step reject in Fighter.** A cost-free pre-validation refusal leaves no state for the driver to observe. `echo_step_reject` plays only on a completion-time cancel, where the wind-up ends with no travel.
3. **Knockdown thud.** This uses a heuristic: a tumble ending grounded without a tech lockout. W3b's component 320 was not in this base, so no knockdown flag was read.
4. **Glow alias.** `GlowLayer.Status` was kept as an alias rather than deleted, so W9's enemy/boss files need no edit.
5. **Time Freeze drone.** The drone is a one-shot placeholder, not a loop held for the freeze. The KO "brief music drop" and the Defy "brief duck of everything else" are not implemented (they need a snapshot/duck, which is Package 10 mixing).

## What did not ship / open
- **Story cues with no publisher.**
  - Grab/throw, Echo Step, landing tech and knockdown have no `EventBus` event in Story, so these cues fire in Fighter only.
  - Nothing raises `OnRallyEchoChanged`: the Story HUD polls, and `PlayerController` is not W10's region. The presenter subscribes, but the Story Rally chime never fires until a publisher is added.
- **Story grab/throw animations.** `PlayerController` does not play `grab`/`throw` for its `Grabbing`/`Thrown` states, because only the single `down_air` call site was in scope.
- **N01 seal cues.** `seal_charge` / `seal_lock` are authored with no caller, because the sealing anchor does not exist (`DEFER-SEALING-ANCHOR`).
- **Per-hero fanfare rows.** None are authored; every hero falls back to the shared placeholder.
- **Rollback.** Fighter cue edges are local presentation. Under a future online rollback correction an edge could re-fire; Package 7 should revisit this.
- **Translation.** `en.en.translation` was not regenerated or committed; the orchestrator runs `--import`. Until then `hud_anchor_charges_accessible` renders as its raw key at runtime (the tests compare against `TranslationServer.Translate`).
- **Playtesting.** Nobody has seen or heard any of this.
