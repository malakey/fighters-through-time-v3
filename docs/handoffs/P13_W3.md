## What shipped

### Level 0 — Part 1 beat 5 "The hold" (S02), beat 6 "the watcher" (S10)
- `Level00Controller` has new phases `Hold → Rift → Arrival → Calibration → …` (`TutorialPhase` is now public; `Phase` is a test seam). `_Ready` defers `StartHoldFight()` (public, idempotent) instead of the old intro.
- The fracture tween is split around the fight: beats 1–2 (the beam bites, the ignition begins) play on load inside `BuildFracturePresentation` (so `ResonanceGrammarTests` still finds every node and pigment); beats 3–4 (the beam breaks, the Warden door opens) play from `PlayBeamBreak()` once the hold is won.
- **The hold** (`FTT.Environment.Level00HoldFight`, pure rules): the voice (`level_00.hold_start`) → three era locals spawn (`EnemyFactory.SpawnSummoned`, so no F05 award; Level 0's ledger pays nothing anyway) → two fall to the ordinary fight (the 3-hit string) → the last is held (`DrillInvulnerable` + rewind-freeze) and winds up a telegraphed Basic swing that repeats every 70 frames until it is blocked (`level_00.hold_guard`, "Guard — now!") → it can then be finished → "It's catching — hold on!" (`level_00.hold_catching`) → `BreakTheBeam()`.
- **Unlosable:** new `PlayerController.ScriptedHPFloor` (default 0; `ApplyDamage` clamps to it) is 1 for the hold and cleared at the beam break. The controller hands back meter above the hold's baseline and clears the Rally echo every physics frame (new `PlayerController.ClearRallyEcho()`), so the fight grants no meter, Rally, dust, rewind use or Integrity (the tutorial has no clock).
- `Level00HoldFight.LocalMobFor(heroID)`: Leonardo `cyber_guard`, Joan `laser_archer`, Tesla `voltaic_shock_drone`, Cleopatra `plasma_spear_ward`, Shakespeare `holo_page`, Lincoln `laser_rifle_infantry`; the no-shared-level heroes use nearest-era placeholders (Mozart `chrono_rioter`, Einstein `laser_rifle_infantry`), and anyone else (Pocahontas, Tubman) falls back to `chrono_rioter`.
- The unnamed voice speaks as `speaker_unknown_voice` ("???"); `DialogueManager.IsPortraitlessSpeaker` hides the portrait frame for it.
- **The watcher:** `EraserWatcher` (a dark silhouette, a cold `UIPalette.UnboundCold` visor, a long lance) fades in at the tear's edge for `WatcherSeconds` = 2 s and frees itself. **W2's Level 5 Eraser debut should reuse this silhouette** (S27).
- Beat 7–8: a `WardenRiftGate` at `RiftGatePosition`; entering it runs `TranslateToBay()` — the fracture hides, the hero lands at `BayArrivalPosition`, healed, and `level_00.intro` plays.

### Hub (S09, S11, S13, S15a, S16/S35, S23, S42, S47)
- **Chief Engineer Wren** (`HubWorldController.WrenNodeName`, Training Wing beside the Holodeck); one tip per act via `HubDialogueSelector.WrenSequenceFor` (`hub.wren_act1/act2/act3/epilogue`).
- **Triage readout** (`FTT.Environment.HubTriageReadout`, `TriageReadout` on the hub): wordless, no collision. The next era flashes red at the top, unsealed eras burn cold in route order, the era sealed by the level just completed flares gold and fades, older sealed eras are gone; dark from Act III and after the campaign. Placeholder shards.
- **Per-line gating:** Sarah's Act I (`hub.sarah_act1.1..4`, line 4 after `level_04_paris`) and Act II (`hub.sarah_act2.1..4`, line 1 after `level_06_pompeii`, line 3 after `level_09_berlin`, line 4 after `level_11_gettysburg`) sets play **one line per visit, in order**; an unmet gate waits and later lines never jump. A visit = the next mission's level ID; a Collapse or Holodeck return is the same visit. Order is derived from the effect ledger; the visit stamp is the new `StorySaveData.LastHubLineVisit`. API: `ArrivalSequenceFor(period, hasSeen, isLevelCompleted, visitKey, lastLineVisit)` (the 2-arg overload survives), `SarahLinesFor`, `SarahTalkSequenceFor`, `IsPerVisitLine`, `HubWorldController.ResolveArrivalSequence` / `CurrentVisitKey`.
- **Act I→II boundary** `hub.act1_boundary` (and `@mozart`): Okafor's per-hero line, the faint-shard report (Prague, 1787; Princeton, 1955 for Mozart), "It's not only the eras we can reach", Sarah's hesitation, the hero, "Pompeii's failing next. - **Act II→III departure** `hub.sarah_act3`: Okafor's per-hero line, the Beacon handover on the Bridge (S42), "We can hear you…", "Then I'll get up."
- **Go home (S39):** `HubPortalAction.TimelineRestored` → `GoHome`; `HubWorldController.GoHome()` runs `CampaignCompletionSequence.BeginGoHome` (send-off + credits, never the Homecoming). A completed save's hero has no aura (`GlowPresentationController.SetHeroAura(false)`). `hub_campaign_complete` / `hub_portal_timeline_restored` values updated.

## v8 save fields declared (additive, engine-free, Phase C composes — CurrentVersion NOT bumped)
All on `StorySaveData`, initializers equal to their legacy meaning, `Normalize()` null-coalesces the list/string:
- `bool SkippedCalibration` (false — every pre-v8 slot ran the full calibration)
- `List<string> ShownFirstUseTooltips` (empty)
- `string LastHubLineVisit` ("" — the next arrival plays the next unheard line)
- `bool HomecomingSeen` (false). A slot already `IsCompleted` before v8 finished the old ending, which had no Homecoming; it can never re-run the ending (Go home skips the Homecoming), so false is harmless. **No derivation is required for any of the four.**
