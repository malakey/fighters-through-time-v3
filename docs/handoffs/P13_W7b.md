# Package 13 W7b — Kits: Joan, Mozart, Cleopatra, Lincoln (non-Ultimate) (handoff)

Branch `p13/W7b` (from main `045b1e5`). Scope: plan §7 W7b — character review J01–J03, J06, M01–M04,
C01–C03, LN01–LN03 and A08, in both modes, plus the shared **ground-wave** primitive. Ultimates are W6's

## What shipped

### The shared ground-wave primitive (both modes)
- **Sim — `FTT.FighterSim.FighterGroundWave`** (`scripts/FighterSim/FighterKitMotion.Reach.cs`). Rides the
  existing projectile component (302), identified by `ProjectileTypeID` (Joan S1 = 11, Lincoln S1 = 31,
  Mozart S2 = 72) — **no new field, no new component**. `Spawn(...)` snaps the wave to the surface under
  the caster (`TryFindSurfaceBelow`: an authored one-way platform or the main floor where it exists; over a
  pit no wave is made, the cooldown is still spent) and bottom-anchors the resource's `HitboxSize` on it.
  `FighterProjectileSystem` destroys a wave at a wall or where its surface ends (`SurfaceContinues`, per-x,
  so Open-stage pits and platform ends stop it), and `CanStrike` makes `HitsGroundedOnly` waves (Smite,
  Emancipator) strike only a grounded fighter on that same surface. A wave is **not a projectile for
  projectile immunity**: Tesla's blink pass-through skips it. A wave is consumed by its first hit (1v1).
- **Story — `FTT.Combat.StoryGroundWave`** (`scripts/Combat/StoryGroundWave.cs`), a plain C# helper ticked
  by the casting ability (no node churn): `Start` snaps to the floor with a downward ray (Environment |
  OneWayPlatform, 480 px), `Advance` stops at a wall ray or where the floor probe fails, strikes every
  target once through `Hurtbox.TakeHit`, and a grounded-only wave skips (without spending its strike) any
  hurtbox whose `CharacterBody2D` is not `IsOnFloor()` (`IsGroundedTarget`; `GroundedProbe` is a test seam).
  Never a `PlaceholderProjectile`, never in a projectile group, hitbox ID `ground_wave`.

### Joan
- **Righteous Smite (J02):** a ground wave, 600 px/s × 0.25 s = 2.5 units, grounded only, `HitstunFrames`
  12 → **30**, `Launches` **false**, knockback (5,−2) → **(2,−1)**, recovery 18 → **20**, `HitboxSize` 40×30.
  Story `JoanRighteousSmite.StrikeWithWave` applies the ability-scoped `AbilityDamage` lane and raises
  `NotifyStoryHitLanded` itself (Wings Refresh reads it; the wave no longer goes through `Hitbox`).
- **Divine Piercing (J01/J03):** `BaseDamage` 6 × 4 → **8 × 3**, cooldown **11 s**, startup **13**, recovery
  **20**, still `ShieldBreaker`. It **lunges ~3 units** (`KitReachRules.DivinePiercingLungeUnits`) across its
  12 active frames with the thrusts spread along it. Sim: kit phase `FighterKitMotion.PiercingLunge` (24),
  started in `FighterAbilityEntitySystem` on press for an authored **Melee** Special 2 (a synthetic loadout
  keeps its old shape); thrusts resolve in `ResolveReachKitHits`, each **exactly once** even when a hitstop
  holds the lunge on its thrust frame (thrusts resolved are packed into the direction int), only the last
  carries the knockback/launch. Story: `JoanDivinePiercing.LungeSpeedPixelsPerSecond` drives the owner's
  velocity during Active; non-final thrusts don't launch.
- **Ascendant Wings (A08, D13):** `MovementType.Glide` → **`MovementType.WingDive = 10`**, `MovementDuration`
  3 → **1 s** (the dive cap). The rise is kept: Story climbs at 420 px/s for the whole 36-frame cast (no
  gravity in `UsingMovementAbility` — that is the shipped Story rise, ≈4.2 units); the sim now matches it
  (`WingRise` 25, constant `MovementSpeed` for the cast's `TotalFrames`). Held button when the rise ends →
  **Wing-Dive** (`WingDive` 26): (±5, −9) units/s for up to 60 frames; release, landing or the cap end it into
  a normal fall; **Attack during the dive** ends it and starts the aerial string on the same tick
  (`FighterKitMotion.TryWingDiveAttackCancel` before the phase machine; Story `JoanAscendantWings
  .TryCancelDiveForAttack` + a kit hook in `PlayerController.ProcessUsingMovementAbility`). No glide, no
  float. J06 wording was already fine.

### Mozart
- **Fortissimo Wave (M01):** the lob is retired in both modes (the sim `fortissimoLob`/`GravityPerSecond`
  path and Story `ConfigureArc` call are gone). A ground wave, **not grounded-only**: 240 px/s × 1.5 s =
  6 units, `HitboxSize` 30×**90** (1.5 units tall), 24 damage, heaviest pushback, 12 s, 16 f.
- **Requiem Chord (M03):** 840 px/s (14 u/s), lifetime 3 s (crosses any stage). Contact **4** + a burst of
  `HitCount` 3 × 4 in 1.2 units (`KitReachRules.RequiemBurstRadiusUnits`) every 6 frames = **16** (the sim
  used to fold 12 into one hit and skip the contact). The contact and the first two pulses hold with no
  impulse; the last pulse carries the knockback. Sim: burst = zone type 71 via
  `FighterReachKitRules.SpawnRequiemBurst`; the chord bursts at a **wall** too. Story: the chord bursts at
  Environment terrain via a per-frame point query (`MozartRequiemChord.TrackChordAgainstTerrain`).
  **W7a's shared burst-on-terrain primitive was not on main; this is a local rule to replace.**
- **Tempo (M02):** an execution that hits (not a block) shaves 2 s (120 frames) off Fortissimo's remaining
  cooldown **once per execution**. Sim: the contact's `ApplyFighterHit` result, then the burst zone's new
  `ExecutionFlags` bit. Story: `MozartRequiemChord.ShaveFortissimo` (contact via the new
  `PlaceholderProjectile.LastImpactDamage`, pulses via dealt > 0).
- **Sonata Drift (M04, D14 both modes):** a rising **glissando** — `DistanceMoved` 180 px (3 units) over
  `MovementDuration` 0.3 s / `ActiveFrames` 18 along the held direction (8-way, Down dropped, neutral = up;
  `MozartSonataDrift.ResolveGlissandoDirection`, sim `Glissando` 29). The staff (2.0 units: scene collider
  200 → **120** px, `SonataPlatformNode.StandHalfWidthPixels` 100 → **60**) is placed under his feet **where
  it ends**. The half-cooldown refund is **at most once per airtime** in both modes: Story
  `MozartSonataDrift.ResolveStaffRefund` (Extra Note obeys it; the once-per-platform
  `SonataPlatformNode.TryConsumeLandingRefund` is deleted); sim staff platforms are now **walkable one-way
  surfaces for either fighter** (`FighterReachKitRules.TryLandOnStaff` / `StandsOnStaff`, hooked into the
  movement system's landing and ground-support checks) and the refund latch is `StaffRefundUsed` on
  component **320**, reset by real ground, a ledge grab or a stock loss.

## Deviations and open items (for §9 and the Phase C ledger)
1. **`VERIFY-WING-DIVE-LEAP` (D13).** "Keep the current rise" was read as Story's shipped rise — 420 px/s
   held for the whole 36-frame cast (≈4.2 units, no gravity in the ability state) — and the sim now climbs
   the same way instead of its old 3.5 u/s boost + 180-frame float. Dive speed (5, 9) u/s (~61° below
   horizontal) is a provisional W7b value in `KitReachRules`.
2. **`DEFER-SIM-SPECIAL-PHASES` (D9)** applies to every new startup/recovery: Smite 12/20, Piercing 13/20,
   etc. live in the `.tres` (Story-authoritative); the sim fires on press (the lunge, the waves, the chord).
3. **Requiem terrain burst is local**, pending W7a's primitive: sim bursts at the side walls only (one-way
   platforms are not terrain in the sim); Story bursts at any Environment body. Replace both with W7a's
   primitive at merge.
4. **Design-silent choices, provisional (A07):** Righteous Smite wave speed 10 u/s (the review sets only
   the 2.5-unit travel); Emancipator/Smite/Fortissimo waves' box sizes from the `.tres` `HitboxSize`; the
   Requiem contact and first two pulses are impulse-free with only the last pulse knocking back; Requiem
   pulses reclaim Rally as direct hits in both modes; Splitting Strike on a **grounded** target is a
   horizontal shove with no launch (the review specifies only the airborne spike); the sim vortex is
   ground-snapped to the surface under the 5-unit point (over a pit it stays at the caster's height); the
   glissando's 12-frame Story startup holds Mozart still (the sim has no startup).
5. **Rail Charge armor in Story also covers its 12-frame startup** — the shared
   `StoryCombatRules.HyperArmorPreventsInterruption` window is Startup|Active and is not W7b's region. The
   sim's armor is exactly the 30 travel frames.
6. **Every sim `MovementType.Dash` is now the Rail Charge** (only Lincoln authors Dash; the synthetic
   `FighterLoadout.Default` movement is a Dash, so default-loadout fighters now charge instead of getting a
   one-tick impulse).
7. **Sim staff platforms are walkable for either fighter** (Story's are one-way platforms too); only the
   owner's landing refunds. A ground wave does not follow a staff.
8. **Presentation:** `FighterSimulationDriver` has no animation mapping for the new kit phases (the lunge
   reads as `run`, the rush/glissando/dive as `jump`/`fall`) — placeholder, Package 10.
9. **`DEFER-FIGHTER-KIT-RULES`:** the Mozart half (the staff refund in the sim) is closed by D14; Leonardo's
   glide bolt is W7a's.
10. `FighterLoadoutFactory.SpecialDamage` still folds multi-hit Melee/Projectile totals into the tuning
    component (Joan S2 24, Mozart S1 12). The W7b paths read the per-hit contract instead; the fold is left
    for any reader of the tuning total (the CPU, the Move List).
11. Copy W7b authored beyond the design: the twelve ability description values (en.csv) — review them.
12. Not done: the plan's LN01 playstyle wording has no string in `en.csv` (nothing to change); AGENTS.md is
    Phase C's; `en.en.translation` not committed.
