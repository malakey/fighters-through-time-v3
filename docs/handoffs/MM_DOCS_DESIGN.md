## Decisions

- Rules are written as built. Where the plan guessed and a handoff corrected it: the Fighter Defy ring fires on the **blast frame** (INTEG ruling); the **grid minors read "Minor Momentum"** (FIXB1 ruling); two Defies pressed together blast nothing (SIM's correction to P5); the Defier is free at press + 38 and its victims at press + 44 (+6); the CPU's corner and wind-up Defies need the target within 3.0 units (MM-CPU); the Level 0 lesson's HP floor and 50-meter top-up (MM-SDEFY); Sarah's line is MM-TEXT's final copy.
- Provisional values are marked provisional in the text and named by ledger ID (`PROVISIONAL-MOMENTUM-RULES`, `PROVISIONAL-DEFY-FRAMES`, `PROVISIONAL-DEFY-PRESENTATION`) rather than invented; no ledger row was written (MM-DOCS-REPO owns the ledger).
- P31 held: the catch-up was done only inside rewritten spans (the old Resonance Momentum history, the Story ×0.35 / ×0.5 scale, Time Freeze on L3, the Echo Step leftovers). Section 5's per-kit cooldown figures were not rewritten one by one; a single dated note at the Specials design principle marks them as retired, relative-weight history.
- Polling, not events: the design says the Momentum gauge raises no EventBus event (both HUDs poll it), contrary to the recon's suggested `OnMomentumChanged`.
