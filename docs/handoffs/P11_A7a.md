untouched.

**Level 6's 0 → 1 is the single largest economy deviation in this workstream** and closes recon G8
(the Chrono-Warden had a resource, three kits, a manifest row, localization and two behaviour suites
— and zero spawn sites anywhere in the campaign).

Every moved row is sanctioned by F05 landing in the same wave: **A10 replaces the flat per-tier award
those rows encode with a per-level required-encounter pool**, under which a salted hunter costs
nothing to add. Each affected level's content test carries that reasoning inline rather than just a
changed number.

   the flat awards.
3. **A6 owns the Sarah ambush barks.** Every `dlg_*` row is A6's under §2.11; the Level 13 pair is
   placed and commented in the spawn table, and the line drops into the authored dialogue set without
   touching placement. The 4A barks are likewise unwritten.

   plan marks it optional, nothing gates it, and `EnemyAbilityVisualLibrary.TryResolve` is
   best-effort, so the Eraser falls back to the shared placeholder VFX taxonomy.
