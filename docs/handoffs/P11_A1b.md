## 1. What shipped

### 1.1 One ordered resolution, per mode (D01 / D02b)

Per distinct eligible hit contact, both modes now resolve:

> hit eligibility / invulnerability → **D04 Defy window** → projectile immunity → **Temporal Aegis**
> → **HP barrier** → ordinary block → HP

and **stop at the first layer that fully prevents the hit**.

- **Story.** `PlayerController.OnHurtboxHit` is now a thin adapter over the new
  `ResolveIncomingHit(hit)`. The absorption itself lives in `ResolveProtectionLayers(damage,
  bypassesFiniteShields)`, shared by the hurtbox path, `ApplyPersistentDamage` (D03c DoT ticks) and
  `ApplyEnvironmentalDamage` (D03c hazard ticks). **The barrier no longer drains inside
  `ApplyDamage`** — that was after block, which is exactly backwards under D01.
- **Sim.** `FighterDamageRules.ApplyFighterHit` gained the same order above its block branch.
- Full absorption returns early with shield-impact feedback: no `BlockSystem.ResolveHit` call, no
  Henry's Bastion grant, no shieldstun, and under D03a/D03e no hitstun, knockback, status or mark —
  including at exact depletion. Partial absorption passes **only the remainder**, at the **original**
  `AttackClass` / `BlockChargeCost`.

### 1.2 Klotho component 312 — `FighterDefenseComponent` (MaxCount = 2)

Per §2.7, assigned to A1b and nobody else. Eight ints:
`DefyProtectionAwaitControl`, `DefyProtectionFrames`, `DefyProcIdentity`, **`DefyBarred`**,
`AegisActive`, `BarrierPoints`, `BarrierCapacity`, `BarrierRemainingFrames`, `BarrierEffectID`.
Snapshot and hash state. `FighterDefenseRules` (in `FighterEntitySystems.cs`) owns every transition:
`IsDefyProtected`, `BeginDefyProtection`, `Tick(suspended, actionable)`, `ClearBarrier`, `Clear`,
`Seal(...)`, plus `DefyProtectionTicks = 60` and `GrantedShieldLifetimeTicks = 480`.

### 1.3 The Story shield model — `scripts/Combat/StoryDefense.cs` (new)

`StoryShieldEffect` (None / HenrysBastion / RoyalAegis / LeafBarrier / Wardenclyffe),
`StoryShieldInstance { EffectId, Points, Capacity, RemainingFrames, GrantEventId }` with
`NoExpiry = -1`, and `StoryDefenseRules` holding every number once:
`GrantedShieldLifetimeFrames = 480`, `GrantedShieldCapacityShare = 0.10f`,
`WardenclyffeCapacityShare = 0.15f`, `WardenclyffeRechargeSharePerSecond = 0.025f`,
`WardenclyffeDamageDelaySeconds = 3.0f`, `DefyProtectionFrames = 60`.

`PlayerController.GrantStoryShield(effect, capacity, lifetimeFrames, grantEventId)` is the D02a
entry point: a repeat grant of the **same** effect refills to cap and **restarts** the timer, never
adds; a repeated `grantEventId` is refused outright (duplicate callbacks, polling, recreated visuals,
restored state). Henry's Bastion uses the block counter, Leaf Barrier the accepted-glide-entry
counter, Royal Aegis the accepted-cast counter.

### 1.4 D02d — the Wardenclyffe rewrite (three shipped defects)

| Defect | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | flat `2f` HP/s, ignores MaxHP | `0.025f × MaximumHP` per live second → empty→full is exactly 6 s at any MaxHP |
| Damage delay | **absent** | 3 live seconds, armed by capacity loss *or* HP loss at the chokepoint, ticking in or out of range |
| Absorption out of range | **never disabled** (only recharge was gated) | gated on `PlayerController.WardenclyffeInCoilRange`, written every frame by `TeslaTeslaCoil.IsInsideAnOwnedCoilRadius()` |

Plus: zero-charge first acquisition, no D02c lifetime, stored charge preserved across range exit and
re-entry, eligibility recomputed every frame (so coil destruction / expiry / replacement / Cataclysm
detonation all drop it), respec clears it, and the delay is freeze-aware.

### 1.5 D02e — Story gained Temporal Aegis

`OrbEffect.TemporalAegis` appended (§2.8 append-only), `PlayerController.HasTemporalAegis` /
`GrantTemporalAegis()` / `ClearTemporalAegis()` — an **active flag**, never a charge count, with no
time expiry. A pickup while protected is consumed with no second charge. Sim: the authoritative flag
moved to `FighterDefenseComponent.AegisActive`; `FighterRuntimeComponent.AegisHits` is kept in step
as a presentation mirror.

### 1.6 D04 — Defy protected recovery, both modes

On a proc the survivor is **released** from the triggering hit's hitstun, launch, tumble, stashed DI
and capture; the defied hit's knockback, hitstun and attached status are zeroed; `AwaitControl` is
set with 60 ticks. The countdown starts at the first resumed **normal-control** tick and pauses under
hitstop / Time Freeze / suspended presentations. Ticks 1–60 are protected, **tick 61 is not**. The
gate rejects hits, damaging DoT ticks and damaging hazards *before* Aegis, barrier and block, and a
rejected contact spends nothing and grants nothing. Not immunity to non-hit deaths — `KillPlayerNonHit`
is untouched.

### 1.7 The rest

- **D03d** — `bypassesFiniteShields` on `ApplyFighterHit` and `HitPayload.BypassesFiniteShields`.
  `ResolveThrow` passes it: **a throw against an active Aegis used to be absorbed entirely**, which
  D03d forbids. The Story secondary thrown-mob collision deliberately does **not** set it.
- **D03c** — the Paris Neural Dampening Beam no longer eats an Aegis charge (a pure meter drain must
  not spend a finite shield). DoT and hazard ticks now route through the shared protection layers.
- **D03f (the Rally clamp)** — both chokepoints now compute
  `min(pool, credited × 2.0, missingHP)` and debit **only the HP actually healed**. A full-health
  attacker used to burn its whole pool for nothing.
- **D03g** — `collectsEcho: false` at the sim zone pulse and all three expiry launches, and at
  `PlaceholderZone.CreditOwner`. The `AddInfluenceFromDamageDealt` doc comment that listed "zone
  pulse" as a reclaiming source is rewritten.
- **D03h** — `AddInfluenceFromDamageDealt(dealt, collectsEcho, ultimateOrigin)`; all nine ultimate
  scripts, `Hitbox` (via `AttackClass.Ultimate`), `PlaceholderZone` (via `AbilityData.Slot`), the sim
  zone pulse, `ApplyIntent`'s ultimate branch and the Cataclysm coil chain. `FighterProjectileComponent`
  gained `UltimateOrigin`.
- **F15** — `DivinePiercingBlockChargeCost` and the Joan branch deleted from the sim; `TryShredBlock`
  and `TryDepleteBlockCharges` deleted from Joan and Lincoln. Both are ordinary Special-class full
  shatters in both modes now (the two modes disagreed before — Lincoln's sim S1 already passed 0).
- **F17** — `RestoreAllCharges()` no longer zeroes `_lockoutTimer`.
- **F10** — `StorySaveData.StoryDefyHistoryUsed` (additive), written by
  `StoryManager.WriteAttemptStateToSave`, restored by `RestoreAttemptStateFromSave`, cleared by
  `ClearLevelAttemptState`, seeded onto the controller by `CharacterFactory` behind the
  `applyStoryProgression` isolation seam, and written back by the proc.
- **F13/F22** — `PlayerController.DefySeal` + `PublishDefySealIfChanged()` (change-only publish) and
  `FighterDefenseRules.Seal(...)`. Sudden Death now sets `DefyBarred` instead of pre-marking
  `DefyHistoryUsed`, so *Spent* and *Unavailable* are distinguishable without clearing the spent flag.

## 7. Risk flags for the orchestrator

- **The `ref FighterDefenseComponent` thread is the widest surface in this branch.** It is mechanical
  and the compiler catches every miss, but it touches four public sim entry points, so a Wave 2
  branch that added a call site before this merges will need one argument inserted per site.
- **Two Sudden Death tests and ten ultimate-meter assertions were inverted, not added.** If a later
  agent re-derives them from the old numbers they will re-break. Both changes are the design, not a
  regression: `verb.DefyHistoryUsed == 0` plus `defense.DefyBarred == 1` in Sudden Death, and
  `Influence == 0` after any ultimate cast.
- **The Wardenclyffe range gate depends on `TeslaTeslaCoil._PhysicsProcess` running.** In a headless
  fixture with no coil node the shield is inert by default (`WardenclyffeInCoilRange` is false), which
  is the safe direction but means a test must set the seam explicitly.
