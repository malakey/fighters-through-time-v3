# P13 W6 — Ultimate activation strike (A02) and Ultimate totals (D15)

Branch `p13/W6` (from `main` at `d41d92d`, Package 13 Phase 0). Items: A02 (the Fighter-mode activation strike, both modes' halves), D15 (`FinaleDamage` / `FinaleLaunches`), the per-character totals and activation strikes E06 / J05 / L04 / T03 / S04 / M05 / C04 / LN05, the CPU Ultimate policy, the Mirror Paradox clone's Fighter version, and the replacement of `FighterUltimateRules`' restated `.tres` timing constants. Klotho component **321** (plan D7) is the only ID used.

**Nothing here is balanced or played.** The 20 / 10 / 45 activation timing is A02's provisional target, the activation hitbox sizes are my placeholders (the design gives ranges only), and the new totals have never been felt in a match.

## 1. What shipped

### A02 — the activation strike (Fighter sim)

Timeline (sim ticks, `P` = the press tick):

| Ticks | Phase (`FighterUltimateActivationComponent.Phase`) | Caster |
|---|---|---|
| `P` | accepted: meter → 0 (D03h), string and universal movement cancelled, `HyperArmorFrames = windup + active` | — |
| `P+1 … P+19` | **Windup** (20) | input discarded, planted (`Velocity.x = 0`; an airborne start also `Velocity.y = 0`, i.e. frozen in the air) |
| `P+20 … P+29` | **Active** (10): one contact test per tick | a Melee activation lunges `ActivationRange` over the 10 frames; otherwise planted |
| contact tick `T` | **Cinematic**: the character's existing Ultimate starts on the held victim | input discarded, ordinary physics (Joan's dash, Mozart's hover) |
| `P+29 … P+73` on a whiff | **WhiffRecovery** (45), armor off | input discarded — Echo Step, block, roll, jump and grab cannot undo it |

- **Clocks.** Wind-up, active and whiff recovery pause while the caster is in hitstop; the cinematic never does. All phase work happens in `FighterCombatSystem` (PostUpdate) — `AdvanceUltimate` then `TryCharacterUltimate` (acceptance) — and the movement system only reads the component (`DiscardInput`, `ApplyCasterMotion`, `HoldCaptured`).
- **Cancellation.** A grab (the caster becomes `BeingHeld`) or a stock loss ends the activation with the meter spent. Hitstun or daze during whiff recovery ends it (the punish owns the fighter). Sudden Death entry clears everything on 321.
- **Contact** (`FighterUltimateActivationRules.StrikeConnects`). Unblockable: it is an overlap test, not a hit, so block, block charges and hyper-armor never enter. Shapes:
  - **Melee** — the `ActivationHitboxSize` box rides in front of the (lunging) caster.
  - **Projectile** — a straight shot from the caster's position at the first active frame; each active frame tests the segment the front travelled that frame (`range × k/10 … range × (k+1)/10`) widened by the box, so nothing tunnels.
  - **GroundWave** — the same sweep, grounded targets only (a jump avoids it); a grounded caster's wave stays on its own floor tier (±0.5 u), an airborne slam reaches any grounded target in range.
- **Avoidance** (`IsAvoided`): `InvulnerabilityFrames > 0` (roll, tech, knockdown, respawn — and W1's air dodge if it uses the same field), the D04 Defy window, out of stocks / on the respawn platform, already held. **Temporal Aegis and HP barriers are not avoidance**: an absorbed contact still starts the cinematic and those layers resolve against its hits (D03b); pinned by `AnAegisAbsorbedContactStillStartsTheCinematic`.
- **The cinematic hold.** On contact the victim's 321 records `CaptorSlot = caster + 1`, `CapturedFrames` and `CaptureAnchor = victim position`; their own activation, grab start-up, Echo Step wind-up, swing, universal movement, ledge hang and pending launch are cancelled. While held (`FighterMovementSystem`, right after the hitstop gate) they take no input, cannot move (position pinned to the anchor, velocity 0), and their statuses/cooldowns keep ticking. The hold lasts `HitCount × interval` when a finale is authored (the finale lands on the release tick) or `(HitCount − 1) × interval` when not (the last regular pulse's own knockback releases them). The hold ends early — with no finale — if the victim is KO'd, respawning, or Defy-protected.
- **The world is not paused.** "Standard gameplay pauses" is read as *the two fighters are suspended*; projectiles, hazards, constructs and the match clock keep running (deviation 1).

### D15 — the finale, both modes

- `AbilityData.FinaleDamage` (float, 0 = none) and `FinaleLaunches` (bool). The total is `HitCount × BaseDamage + FinaleDamage`; helpers `CinematicHitCount`, `CinematicHitDamage(i)`, `IsFinaleHit(i)`, `CinematicHitCarriesStatus(i)`, `UltimateImpactTotal`, all over the shared rulebook `FTT.Combat.UltimateActivationRules` (plain C#, no Godot types).
- **Status rule** (`UltimateActivationRules.HitCarriesStatus` / `StatusRidesFinale`): a **Damage-slot** status (Venom, Radiant Burn) rides the finale only, so it lands exactly once (C04's Venom 2.0 × 3 s = 12 counts toward 78); a **Control-slot** hold (the Vitruvian Matrix's and Union Indestructible's Root) rides every regular hit and never the finale. Both modes.
- **Sim.** The finale is delivered by the combat system when the hold ends (`DeliverUltimateFinale`): one Ultimate-class direct hit of `FinaleDamage`, the authored `UltimateKnockback`, `UltimateActivationRules.FinaleHitstunFrames` (30), `launches: FinaleLaunches`, hit origin one unit behind the victim so it is driven along the caster's facing (toward the blast zone), `creditInfluence: false` (D03h), Rally reclaim retained (D03g). The superseded per-type code is deleted: Einstein's and Leonardo's zero-damage expiry launches (`ApplyCosmologicalLaunch`, `ApplyMatrixExpiryExplosion`) and the Joan / Mozart / Shakespeare "last pulse carries the knockback" branches with their hitstun constants. Lincoln and Pocahontas (no finale) keep their final-pulse knockback.
- **Story.** Each of the eight Ultimate scripts walks `CinematicHitCount` hits with `CinematicHitDamage(i)`; the finale uses `UltimateActivationRules.FinaleHitstunSeconds` (0.5 s) and the status rule above. Story keeps its screen-clearing shapes and pulls (Einstein, Joan, Leonardo, Mozart, Shakespeare, Cleopatra, Tesla, Lincoln scripts; Pocahontas untouched).

### Component 321 — `FighterUltimateActivationComponent`

`[KlothoComponent(321, MaxCount = 2)]`, `Pack = 4`, **64 bytes**, all-zero inactive, added to every fighter by `FighterWorldSystem.SpawnFighter`:

| Field | Type | Bytes | Side | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Phase` | int | 4 | caster | 0 None, 1 Windup, 2 Active, 3 WhiffRecovery, 4 Cinematic |
| `PhaseFrames` | int | 4 | caster | frames left in the phase |
| `StartedAerial` | int | 4 | caster | 1 = accepted airborne (gravity 0 through wind-up + active) |
| `FacingRight` | int | 4 | caster | facing committed at acceptance |
| `CaptorSlot` | int | 4 | victim | captor PlayerID + 1; 0 = not held |
| `CapturedFrames` | int | 4 | victim | hold frames left; > 0 = held |
| `LungeVelocityX` | FP64 | 8 | caster | a Melee activation's signed lunge speed (set at acceptance) |
| `Origin` | FPVector2 | 16 | caster | aerial anchor, then the strike origin from the first active frame |
| `CaptureAnchor` | FPVector2 | 16 | victim | where the victim is held |

Rules: `FighterUltimateActivationRules` (`PhaseNone…PhaseCinematic`, `IsCasterBusy`, `IsCaptured`, `IsArmored`, `Accept`, `ClearCaster`, `ReleaseCapture`, `DiscardInput`, `ApplyCasterMotion`, `HoldCaptured`, `ResolveStrikeBox`, `IsAvoided`, `StrikeConnects`). Read-out: `FighterSimulation.TryGetFighterUltimateActivation(playerID, out …)`.

### CPU Ultimate policy (`FighterCpuController.UltimateActivationCanConnect`)

Recorded rule: every band commits only when the strike can plausibly connect — the target is inside `CloseRange` (2 u, the existing Ultimate gate, well inside every 5–7 u activation so the wind-up's travel cannot carry it out of reach), **not invulnerable, not rolling, and grounded** (a grounded target has not already started the jump that clears a straight shot or a ground wave). A Story-shaped observation with no target verb state (the Mirror adapter) applies the range rule only. Hard keeps its setup requirement on top. MIRROR_PARADOX's "first legal opportunity" (Easy/Normal) now reads "first opportunity at which the strike can connect". Pinned by `TheCpuCommitsOnlyWhenTheStrikeCanPlausiblyConnect`; `CpuVerbPolicyTests.MediumsImmediateUltimatePolicyTakesPriorityOverEchoStep` passes unchanged.

### Story — the Mirror Paradox clone plays the Fighter version

`PlayerController.UsesFighterUltimateActivation` (set only by `MirrorParadoxController` on its clone; false for every player) routes `CheckUltimateInput` to `BeginUltimateActivationStrike`: meter drained at acceptance, `ApplyStoryHyperArmor(windup + active)`, the ordinary `UsingUltimate` state with a private activation phase (`UltimateActivationPhase` read-out: 0/1/2/3). `ProcessUsingUltimate` runs `ProcessUltimateActivationStrike` first: planted / frozen-in-air / lunging motion, a per-active-frame rectangle query at the caster's hurtbox height on the slot's target hurtbox layer (`StoryShapeQuery.DeliveryMask`), avoidance through the target's `IsUltimateActivationAvoided` (dead/respawning, roll invulnerability, tech invulnerability, knockdown, post-rewind invulnerability, Defy window), ground wave = `IsOnFloor()` targets only. Contact lends the meter for the execute call exactly like the F04 path and restores the earned value, so the real Story Ultimate runs at full damage; a whiff sits in `UsingUltimate` for the whiff recovery (no action reachable) and then returns to Idle/Airborne. The edit is confined to `CheckUltimateInput`'s ordinary branch (one line) and `ProcessUsingUltimate` plus a new region above `BeginUltimateCast` — the F04 Nexus lines W2 deletes are untouched.

## 2. What did not ship

1. **The world does not pause** for the Fighter cinematic; only the two fighters are suspended (deviation 1 — propose `VERIFY-ULT-CINEMATIC-PAUSE`).
2. **Cleopatra's order.** C04 lists the sarcophagus slam first; the build delivers the six cobras and then the 12-damage slam as the D15 finale carrying the Venom (the total and the once-only Venom are exact). Propose `VERIFY-ULT-FINALE-ORDER` if the slam must lead.
3. **The Story clone's cinematic does not hold the player.** The Story Ultimate scripts are the screen-clearing versions with their own pulls/radii; a player who escapes their radius after contact takes less than the full total. Only the Fighter sim has the hold.
4. **No telegraph presentation** (red Ultimate tint / X glyph on the wind-up) in either mode.
5. **Activation hitbox sizes** are placeholders; only ranges and shapes come from ABILITY_DATA. `ActivationHitboxOffset` and `ActivationProjectileScene` (listed in the GDD's AbilityData table) were not added — the sim strike is geometric, and no projectile scene exists for it.
6. **Air dodge.** Treated as avoidance only if W1 grants it through `InvulnerabilityFrames` (the roll's field); if W1 uses a different flag, add it to `FighterUltimateActivationRules.IsAvoided` and `PlayerController.IsUltimateActivationAvoided` at merge.
7. **A04 despawn (W1).** When the caster loses a stock the activation/cinematic ends here; the zones themselves are W1's A04 sweep.
8. `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` are not edited (Phase C rewrites them for components 321/322); the ledger rows are proposed below, not written.
9. The ultimate `.tres` `HitstunFrames` (12; Lincoln 30) are now read by nothing in the sim for an Ultimate (the finale uses the shared 30). Left untouched to avoid colliding with W1's `AbilityHitContractTests` rewrite; a later pass can make the finale read the authored value.
