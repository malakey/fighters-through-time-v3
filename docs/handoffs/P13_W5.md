### Fighter sim (`scripts/FighterSim/`)

- **`FighterCharacterID.Tubman = 9`** appended; **`Pocahontas = 8`** kept, `[Obsolete]`, reserved. `FighterLoadoutFactory.TryGetCharacterID("tubman")` → 9; `"pocahontas"` no longer resolves. Persistent type **4** (`vine_snare`) retired in `PersistentObjectTypeID`. Zone type **83** retired (both `FighterEntitySystems` branches and `TidewaterSurgeHitstunFrames` deleted). Kit phase codes **19–20** (Spirit Strike) retired; `ResolveSpiritEagles` deleted. The string-profile array keeps index 8 as the template.
- **Component 322 `FighterCounterComponent`** (`scripts/FighterSim/FighterCounter.cs`), `[KlothoComponent(322, MaxCount = 2)]`, `Pack = 4`, **16 bytes**, all-zero inactive, added to every fighter by `FighterWorldSystem.SpawnFighter`:

  | Field | Type | Bytes | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `Phase` | int | 4 | 0 None, 1 Startup, 2 Window, 3 WhiffRecovery, 4 Sidestep |
  | `PhaseFrames` | int | 4 | frames left in the phase |
  | `AnswerPending` | int | 4 | 1 = a trigger owes the counter strike |
  | `CountersLanded` | int | 4 | triggers this match (read-out) |

  Rules: `FighterForesightRules` (`Phase*`, `IsBusy`, `IsWindowOpen`, `Begin`, `Clear`, `Advance`, `TryCounter`, `AnswerReaches`). Read-out: `FighterSimulation.TryGetFighterCounter(playerID, out …)`.
- **Foresight flow.** `FighterAbilityEntitySystem` accepts Tubman's Special 2 (arms the 600-frame cooldown so the combat system's melee intent never also fires) and calls `Begin`. `FighterMovementSystem` advances the clock after the hitstop gate (4 → 20 → 24; the press tick is the first startup frame) and discards the fighter's input for the whole stance (`DiscardInput`, the A02 idiom). Hitstun, daze, a grab (`FighterGrabRules.IsBusy`), an Ultimate capture or a stock loss clears it. `TryCounter` is asked **before** `ApplyFighterHit` by exactly four paths: a string swing (`ApplyBasicSwing`), a directional swing (`ApplyDirectionalSwing`, incl. the Down-Air slam), a melee Special intent (`ApplyIntent`), and a fighter projectile (`FighterProjectileSystem`, which destroys the caught shot). A trigger applies nothing, closes the window (one per activation), arms a 12-frame sidestep (`InvulnerabilityFrames`, action-locked) and sets `AnswerPending`; an already-invulnerable defender neither triggers nor spends it. `FighterCombatSystem.ResolveForesightAnswer` then lands the answer after every ordinary hit of the tick (a caught projectile's answer lands next tick): if the opponent is within 2.5 u (squared FP64 distance), one Special-class hit of `SpecialTwoDamage` (20) with the Special 2 contract's hitstun, signed knockback and launch flag, pushed away from her, facing turned toward them. It never asks the opponent's own Foresight. Throws, Ultimate activation strikes and cinematic zones, zone/construct ticks, hazards, Lorentz chain arcs and finales never ask.
- **Conductor's Call** is the ordinary Special 1 projectile (type **91**) plus one rule, `FighterConductorsCallRules.CanStrike`: it contacts grounded targets only, and passing an airborne one does not consume it. A local stand-in for W7b's ground-wave primitive (not on `main` when W5 branched).
- **North Star Leap** — kit phases **21 `LeapTravel`** and **22 `LeapEnd`** in the existing universal-movement slot of 305 (no new state): `FighterKitMotion.StartNorthStarLeap(ref runtime, facing, travelFrames)` latches an 8-way direction from the stick (neutral = facing), travels the loadout's `MovementDistance` over `MovementDurationFrames` (diagonals × 1/√2), then one weightless zero-velocity settle frame. Gravity is suspended through both. `ApplyMovement` dispatches it on `CharacterID == Tubman` ahead of the `MovementType` switch. **Ledge snap:** `FighterKitMotion.HasLedgeSnapBonus` (travel + settle) makes `FighterMovementSystem.TryGrabLedge` search with `FighterStageGeometry.TryFindLedge(position, LeapLedgeSnapBonus, out anchor)` — every capture box widened by 0.5 u (new `IsInCaptureBox(position, side, extraHalfWidth)` overload; same search order). The ordinary `CanGrab` gates still apply (a fast upward leap catches on its settle frame).
- **The Freedom Line** — `FighterUltimateRules.TryExecute` routes Tubman to `SpawnCinematicZone` (zone **93**, 7 × 18-frame pulses on the held victim; the combat system's D15 finale is the 20-damage final rush). `ResolveZoneShape` gives zone 93 a 6 × 2-unit box.
- **CPU.** `CpuRecoveryProfile.MobilitySpecialFor` returns `None` for every character (the Spirit Strike approval is retired; the planner branch and `RecoveryMobilitySpecialPercent` stay, dormant). New `CpuRecoveryProfile.MovementLedgeSnapBonus` (0.5 u for Tubman, 0 otherwise) is added to `MovementHorizontalReach` for directional abilities. Tubman plans the leap as a directional (Blink-kind) move, aimed diagonally up toward the return. No Special is a recovery tool (CPU_RECOVERY).
- **Presentation.** `FighterSimulationDriver.ResolveAbilityPresentation` holds `special_2` for the whole Foresight stance.

## 2. What did not ship / provisional

1. **Ground wave.** Conductor's Call uses a local grounded-projectile rule (sim) and a local ground sweep (Story), not W7b's ground-wave primitive. Unify at merge (propose `DEFER-GROUND-WAVE-UNIFY`). An airborne cast rushes at her own height in the sim and along her feet line in Story.
2. **Foresight placeholders** (propose `VERIFY-TUBMAN-PROVISIONAL`): the sidestep is 12 frames of invulnerability with no displacement (the design names a sidestep, gives no number); the answer's hitstun is the ability's authored 12 (AbilityHitContractTests' template value); the answer lands one tick after the trigger; the Story answer strikes the **nearest** hostile within 2.5 u rather than tracing the attacker. `DEFER-SIM-SPECIAL-PHASES` (D9) applies: the sim stance runs 4/20/24 from `TubmanKitRules`, while Conductor's Call fires on press in the sim.
3. **North Star Leap** has no authored startup in the design: Story 1 / 18 / 8 frames, sim 18 travel + 1 settle frame. The Story ledge snap checks once, at the end of travel, with placeholder geometry (body centre 30 px above the feet, the pushbox half-width); a Story leap cannot grab the ordinary way mid-travel (it is `UsingMovementAbility`).
4. **The Freedom Line** Story band 960 × 240 and the sim zone-93 box 6 × 2 are placeholders; "carries opponents along its line" is the W6 hold (the world is not paused — W6 deviation 1).
5. **Safe Passage** trail geometry (±40 px vertical tolerance) is placeholder. No trail VFX.
6. **Presentation** is D5 placeholder only; no Foresight/answer/leap VFX beyond the shared taxonomy scenes; nobody has looked at it.
7. `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` and the ledger are not edited (Phase C). The GDD/contract mirrors already describe Tubman.
8. `Level00HoldFightTests` still lists `"pocahontas"` among its probe IDs (W3's test; it now exercises the fallback mob) — left untouched.
