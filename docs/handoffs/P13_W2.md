  `SealReadiness`, `TemporalCoreAnchor`, `LevelResultsPanel` and `PlaceholderZone` comments no longer reference 4A as live.

A payload whose `CurrentLevelID` starts with the retired prefix: its `LevelChronalDust` (collected, or post-fee held after a

`CurrentLevelID` → Level 5; `LastCheckpointID`, `ActivatedCheckpointIDs`, `DestroyedExtractorIDs`, `FoundSecretIDs`,
`FontUsesConsumed`, `ClaimedRewardSourceIDs` cleared; `LevelIntegrityPercent` / `CheckpointIntegrityPercent` → 100;
`StoryDefyHistoryUsed` false; `AnchorCharges` 0; `AttemptState = CreateFresh("level_05_titanic", 0)`. `CompletedLevels`,
`IntegrityByLevel`, grid, Legacy unlocks and deposited dust are untouched — 4A history is dead data. Literals are pinned
against `StoryManager` by `RetireLegacyLevelResumeTests` (Godot runtime); `CurrentVersion` is **not** bumped.
